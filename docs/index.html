Standard Overview 标准概述

ICO Std 2002:2026 is the world's first international standard to cover the full lifecycle of ranking methodology — from indicator design and data collection through scoring, publication, and audit. It provides a universal, domain-independent framework ensuring that all ranking systems conform to consistent principles of rigour, transparency, and reproducibility.

本标准是全球首个覆盖排名方法论全流程的国际标准，从指标设计与数据采集到评分发布和审计追溯，提供领域无关的通用框架，确保所有排名系统遵循严谨性、透明度和可复现性的一致原则。本标准对标IREG柏林原则和ISO体系，为排名行业建立国际化的方法论基准。

ICO Std 2002 works in concert with ICO Std 2001 (DPP-CQ), the Digital Product Passport — Computational Quality standard. While ICO Std 2001 governs the quality of data inputs, ICO Std 2002 governs the methodological integrity of how those inputs are transformed into ranking outcomes. Together, they form a comprehensive quality assurance chain from data to decision.

Core Specification 核心规范

Foundational Framework · 基础框架
  • 0 Preface 前言
    Background and motivation for the standard — the proliferation of ranking systems and the lack of methodological consistency in the global ranking landscape.
  • 1 Scope 范围
    General requirements for ranking methodology applicable to any organisation; establishes a universal, domain-independent methodological framework.
  • 2 Normative References 规范性引用文件
    Indispensable referenced documents including the IREG Berlin Principles, ISO standards, and other normative instruments.
  • 3 Terms and Definitions 术语和定义
    Core terminology and definitions used throughout the standard, aligned with ISO and IEC terminological databases.
Methodology Core · 方法论核心
  • 4 General Principles 通用原则
    Fundamental principles governing all ranking methodologies — independence, fairness, transparency, and reproducibility as the normative foundation.
  • 5 Indicator System Design 指标体系设计
    Requirements for indicator selection, hierarchical organisation, validity verification, initial weight assignment, and update mechanisms.
  • 6 Weighting Rules 权重分配规则
    Methods, procedures and requirements for weight determination, sensitivity analysis, disclosure, and cross-domain weight coordination.
  • 7 Data Collection Specifications 数据采集规范
    Comprehensive data governance framework: three-tier source classification, processing, quality assurance, ethical compliance, and archival.
  • 8 Scoring and Ranking Methods 评分与排序方法
    Scoring, normalisation, aggregation and ranking methods; handling of ties and uncertainty; verification and standard publication format.
Transparency & Compliance · 透明度与合规
  • 9 Result Publication and Transparency 结果发布与透明度
    Publication requirements, methodological transparency, stakeholder communication, and historical data preservation; aligned with IREG Berlin Principles.
  • 10 Audit and Traceability 审计与可追溯性
    Internal and external audit framework, traceability from results to original data, data version control, and ISO/IEC 17021 alignment.
  • 11 Compliance Declaration and Certification 合规性声明与认证
    Three compliance levels, self-assessment and third-party certification processes, and the relationship with ICO Std 2001 (DPP-CQ).

SE-03

SE-04
Technology Innovation

SE-05
Cultural Heritage & ICH
文化遗产与非遗
Tangible and intangible heritage evaluation with community participation and conservation framework alignment.
SE-06
Restaurants & Gastronomy
餐厅与美食
Culinary assessment methodology balancing subjective sensory experience, cultural context, and temporal consistency.
SE-07
Education & Academia
教育与学术
Educational rankings with enhanced methodological rigour, peer review mechanisms, and educational equity provisions.
SE-08
Sustainability
可持续发展
Environmental performance within planetary boundaries; greenwashing detection and sustainability claim verification.
SE-09
ESG
综合ESG评价
Enterprise-level ESG assessment with E/S/G pillar structure and cross-framework disclosure mapping for investors and regulators.
SE-10
Tourist Attractions
景区排名
Attraction evaluation with classification-based weighting, seasonal adjustment, and conservation-access balance.

Standards Relationship 标准关系

ICO Std 2002 is designed to integrate with and extend existing international standards and frameworks. The diagram below summarises the key relationships.

ICO Std 2001 (DPP-CQ)
数字产品护照 — 计算质量
Governs data quality inputs; ICO Std 2002 governs methodological integrity. Together they form a quality assurance chain from data to ranking outcome.
IREG Berlin Principles
IREG柏林原则
16 principles for university ranking transparency; ICO Std 2002 aligns with and extends these principles to all ranking domains.
ISO 20671 / ISO 10668
品牌评价标准
Brand evaluation frameworks; ICO Std 2002 provides ranking-specific operationalisation aligned with their conceptual structures.
ISO 8000
数据质量标准
Data quality management; ICO Std 2002's three-tier data source classification and quality assurance provisions align with ISO 8000 principles.
UN Sustainable Development Goals
联合国可持续发展目标
Sustainability and ESG extensions (SE-08, SE-09) are designed to support SDG-aligned assessment and reporting.
UNESCO ICH Convention
教科文组织非遗公约
The Cultural Heritage extension (SE-05) aligns with the 2003 Convention for the Safeguarding of Intangible Cultural Heritage.
