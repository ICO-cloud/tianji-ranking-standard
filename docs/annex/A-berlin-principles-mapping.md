# Annex A: IREG Berlin Principles Mapping
## 附录A IREG柏林原则对标映射

**ICO Std 2002 — Annex A (Informative)**

---

## Purpose | 目的

This annex provides a detailed mapping between the IREG Berlin Principles on Ranking of Higher Education Institutions (adopted 20 May 2006, Berlin) and the corresponding provisions of ICO Std 2002. It demonstrates the degree of alignment between the two frameworks and identifies areas where ICO Std 2002 extends beyond the requirements of the Berlin Principles.

本附录提供IREG《高等教育机构排名柏林原则》（2006年5月20日通过，柏林）与ICO Std 2002相应条款之间的详细映射。它展示两个框架之间的一致程度，并识别ICO Std 2002超出柏林原则要求的领域。

---

## A.1 Overview of the Berlin Principles | 柏林原则概览

### A.1.1 History and Context | 历史与背景

The Berlin Principles on Ranking of Higher Education Institutions were adopted on 20 May 2006 at the second meeting of the International Ranking Expert Group (IREG), founded in 2004 by the UNESCO European Centre for Higher Education (UNESCO-CEPES) in Bucharest and the Institute for Higher Education Policy (IHEP) in Washington, DC. The principles consist of 16 items organised into four sections:

IREG《高等教育机构排名柏林原则》于2006年5月20日在国际排名专家组（IREG）第二次会议上通过。IREG于2004年由联合国教科文组织欧洲高等教育中心（UNESCO-CEPES）和华盛顿高等教育政策研究所（IHEP）共同创立。该原则包含16项条款，分为四个部分：

| Section | Title | Principles | 章节 | 标题 | 条款 |
|---------|-------|-----------|------|------|------|
| A | Purposes and Goals of Rankings | R1–R5 | A | 排名的目的与目标 | R1–R5 |
| B | Design and Weighting of Indicators | R6–R9 | B | 指标设计与权重 | R6–R9 |
| C | Collection and Processing of Data | R10–R14 | C | 数据采集与处理 | R10–R14 |
| D | Presentation of Ranking Results | R15–R16 | D | 排名结果的呈现 | R15–R16 |

> **Note**: The Berlin Principles were originally designed for higher education rankings. ICO Std 2002 applies to a broader range of ranking domains (cultural heritage, consumer products, brands, cities, etc.). The mapping below accounts for this domain expansion by identifying where ICO Std 2002 generalises or extends the Berlin Principles beyond their original higher education context.
>
> **注**：柏林原则最初为高等教育排名设计。ICO Std 2002适用于更广泛的排名领域（文化遗产、消费品、品牌、城市等）。以下映射通过识别ICO Std 2002在何范围内将柏林原则推广或扩展至其原始高等教育语境之外，来考虑这一领域扩展。

### A.1.2 Scope of Application | 适用范围

The Berlin Principles apply to rankings of higher education institutions. ICO Std 2002 applies to any ranking system that evaluates entities using a structured methodology, regardless of domain. The mapping in this annex covers all 16 Berlin Principles and identifies, for each principle:

柏林原则适用于高等教育机构排名。ICO Std 2002适用于任何使用结构化方法论评估实体的排名系统，不限于特定领域。本附录的映射涵盖全部16项柏林原则，并对每项原则识别：

a) the specific ICO Std 2002 clause(s) that address the same requirement;
   ICO Std 2002中处理同一要求的具体条款；

b) the degree of coverage (完全覆盖 Full / 部分覆盖 Partial / 超出覆盖 Exceeds);
   覆盖程度；

c) any significant extensions or differences.
   任何重大扩展或差异。

## A.2 Principle-by-Principle Mapping | 逐原则对照

### A.2.1 R1: Ranking as One of Multiple Approaches | 排名作为多种评估方法之一

**Berlin Principle R1** | **柏林原则R1**

> "Be one of a number of diverse approaches to the assessment of higher education inputs, processes, and outputs. Rankings can provide comparative information and improved understanding of higher education, but should not be the main method for assessing what higher education is and does."
>
> "排名应是评估高等教育投入、过程和产出的多种不同方法之一。排名可以提供比较信息和增进对高等教育的理解，但不应成为评估高等教育本质和功能的主要方法。"

| Aspect | Berlin Principle | ICO Std 2002 | Coverage |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| Ranking as complementary assessment | R1 | §4.1 (Independence), §01 (Scope) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Limitations of ranking scope | R1 | §9.1.4 (Limitations Disclosure) | 完全覆盖 Full |

**ICO Std 2002 Mapping** | **ICO Std 2002映射**

ICO Std 2002 §01 (Scope) establishes that the standard applies to ranking systems as one form of comparative assessment, not as a substitute for comprehensive evaluation. §9.1.4 requires explicit disclosure of the conceptual limitations of the indicator system, including "any aspects of the ranking purpose that are not fully captured by the indicators." This fully satisfies R1's requirement that rankings be positioned as complementary rather than definitive.

ICO Std 2002 §01（范围）确立本标准适用于排名系统作为比较评估的一种形式，而非综合评估的替代。§9.1.4要求明确披露指标体系的概念局限性，包括"排名目的中未被指标充分覆盖的方面"。这完全满足R1关于排名应定位为补充性而非决定性评估的要求。

### A.2.2 R2: Clear Purpose and Target Groups | 明确目的与目标群体

**Berlin Principle R2** | **柏林原则R2**

> "Be clear about their purpose and their target groups. Rankings have to be designed with due regard to their purpose."
>
> "应明确其目的和目标群体。排名的设计必须充分考虑其目的。"

| Aspect | Berlin Principle | ICO Std 2002 | Coverage |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| Purpose statement | R2 | §4.1.1 (Organisational Independence), §9.1.2(a) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Target group identification | R2 | §9.1.2(a) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Design aligned with purpose | R2 | §05.1 (Indicator Selection Criteria) | 完全覆盖 Full |

**ICO Std 2002 Mapping** | **ICO Std 2002映射**

§9.1.2(a) requires the methodology statement to include "the ranking purpose and target audience." §05.1 (Indicator Selection Criteria) requires that indicators be selected according to their relevance and validity relative to the stated ranking purpose. This ensures that the indicator system is designed with due regard to the ranking's purpose, as required by R2.

§9.1.2(a)要求方法论声明包含"排名目的和目标受众"。§05.1（指标选取准则）要求指标根据其与声明排名目的的相关性和有效性进行选择。这确保指标体系的设计充分考虑排名目的，符合R2要求。

### A.2.3 R3: Recognise Diversity of Institutions | 认识机构多样性

**Berlin Principle R3** | **柏林原则R3**

> "Recognize the diversity of institutions and take the different missions and goals of institutions into account."
>
> "认识机构的多样性，并将机构的不同使命和目标纳入考量。"

| Aspect | Berlin Principle | ICO Std 2002 | Coverage |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| Diversity recognition | R3 | §4.4.3 (Uniform Application of Criteria), §05.2 (Indicator Hierarchical Structure) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Mission differentiation | R3 | §06.1 (Weight Assignment Methods), SE modules | 完全覆盖 Full |

**ICO Std 2002 Mapping** | **ICO Std 2002映射**

ICO Std 2002 generalises R3 beyond higher education institutions. §4.4.3 requires that criteria be applied uniformly across all ranked entities, while §05.2 allows for indicator systems that accommodate different entity types through hierarchical structures. The extension modules (SE-01 through SE-09) define domain-specific indicators that reflect the diversity of different ranking domains. §06.1 provides for differential weighting that can account for the varying missions and goals of ranked entities.

ICO Std 2002将R3推广至高等教育机构之外。§4.4.3要求评分标准在所有被排实体中一致适用，而§05.2允许通过层级结构容纳不同实体类型的指标体系。扩展模块（SE-01至SE-09）定义了反映不同排名领域多样性的领域特定指标。§06.1提供差异加权，可考虑被排实体的不同使命和目标。

### A.2.4 R4: Information Sources and Messages | 信息来源与信息含义

**Berlin Principle R4** | **柏林原则R4**

> "Provide clarity about the range of information sources for rankings and the messages each source generates."
>
> "明确排名的信息来源范围及每个来源所产生的信息含义。"

| Aspect | Berlin Principle | ICO Std 2002 | Coverage |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| Information source identification | R4 | §7.1 (Data Source Classification), §4.2.2 (Data Source Traceability) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Source-specific messages | R4 | §9.1.3 (Data Summary) | 完全覆盖 Full |

**ICO Std 2002 Mapping** | **ICO Std 2002映射**

§7.1 establishes a three-tier data source classification system (Tier 1: Official/Audited; Tier 2: Survey/Expert; Tier 3: Unverified/Public), which goes beyond the Berlin Principles' general requirement by providing a structured framework for assessing the reliability and limitations of each data source. §4.2.2 requires traceability of all data to its source. §9.1.3 requires publication of a data summary including the proportion of data from each tier.

§7.1建立了三级数据源分类体系（一级：官方/审计；二级：调查/专家；三级：未验证/公开），通过提供评估每个数据源可靠性和局限性的结构化框架，超越了柏林原则的一般要求。§4.2.2要求所有数据可追溯至其来源。§9.1.3要求发布数据摘要，包括来自每层级的数据比例。

### A.2.5 R5: Specify Linguistic, Cultural, Economic, and Historical Contexts | 明确语言、文化、经济和历史背景

**Berlin Principle R5** | **柏林原则R5**

> "Specify the linguistic, cultural, economic, and historical contexts of the educational systems being ranked."
>
> "明确被排名教育系统的语言、文化、经济和历史背景。"

| Aspect | Berlin Principle | ICO Std 2002 | Coverage |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| Context specification | R5 | §4.2.2 (Data Source Traceability), §9.1.4 (Limitations Disclosure) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Cultural sensitivity | R5 | §4.2.2 (Cultural Sensitivity extension), SE-05 (Cultural Heritage) | 超出覆盖 Exceeds |

**ICO Std 2002 Mapping** | **ICO Std 2002映射**

ICO Std 2002 satisfies R5 through §9.1.4, which requires disclosure of conceptual and methodological limitations including contextual factors. Furthermore, ICO Std 2002 extends beyond R5 by incorporating explicit cultural sensitivity requirements in SE-05 (Cultural Heritage) and by requiring domain-specific contextual considerations in all extension modules. The DPP-CQ integration framework (§10.4) provides a mechanism for addressing cultural and contextual differences in cross-domain rankings.

ICO Std 2002通过§9.1.4满足R5的要求，该条款要求披露包括语境因素在内的概念和方法论局限性。此外，ICO Std 2002通过在SE-05（文化遗产）中纳入明确的文化敏感性要求，并通过在所有扩展模块中要求领域特定语境考量，超越了R5。DPP-CQ整合框架（§10.4）提供了处理跨领域排名中文化和语境差异的机制。

### A.2.6 R6: Methodology Transparency | 方法论透明度

**Berlin Principle R6** | **柏林原则R6**

> "Be transparent regarding the methodology used for creating the rankings. The choice of methods used to prepare rankings should be clear and unambiguous. This transparency should include the calculation of indicators as well as the origin of data."
>
> "排名方法论的透明度。用于编制排名的方法选择应清晰明确。透明度应包括指标的计算和数据的来源。"

| Aspect | Berlin Principle | ICO Std 2002 | Coverage |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| Methodology disclosure | R6 | §4.2.1 (Methodology Disclosure), §9.1.2 (Methodology Statement) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Indicator calculation transparency | R6 | §8.2 (Normalisation), §8.3 (Aggregation), §4.2.3 (Scoring Rule Verifiability) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Data origin transparency | R6 | §4.2.2 (Data Source Traceability), §7.1 (Data Source Classification) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Four-level transparency system | — | §9.2 (Transparency Levels) | 超出覆盖 Exceeds |

**ICO Std 2002 Mapping** | **ICO Std 2002映射**

ICO Std 2002 fully satisfies R6 through its comprehensive transparency framework. §4.2.1 requires publication of a complete methodology description. §4.2.3 requires that scoring rules be specified with sufficient precision for third-party verification. §9.2 establishes a four-level transparency classification system (Level 1: Summary Transparency; Level 2: Methodology Transparency; Level 3: Data Transparency; Level 4: Full Reproducibility) that goes significantly beyond the Berlin Principles' general transparency requirement by providing a graduated framework for assessing and improving transparency.

ICO Std 2002通过其全面的透明度框架完全满足R6。§4.2.1要求发布完整方法论描述。§4.2.3要求评分规则具有足够的精确度以供第三方验证。§9.2建立了四级透明度分类体系（一级：摘要透明；二级：方法论透明；三级：数据透明；四级：完全可复现），通过提供评估和改进透明度的分级框架，显著超越了柏林原则的一般透明度要求。

### A.2.7 R7: Indicator Relevance and Validity | 指标相关性与有效性

**Berlin Principle R7** | **柏林原则R7**

> "Choose indicators according to their relevance and validity. The choice of data should be grounded in recognition of the ability of each measure to represent quality and academic and institutional strengths, and not availability of data."
>
> "根据指标的相关性和有效性进行选择。数据选择应基于对每项度量代表质量和学术与机构实力能力的认识，而非数据的可用性。"

| Aspect | Berlin Principle | ICO Std 2002 | Coverage |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| Relevance criterion | R7 | §05.1 (Indicator Selection Criteria) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Validity criterion | R7 | §05.1 (Indicator Selection Criteria), §8.5 (Ranking Result Verification) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Data availability warning | R7 | §05.1 (prohibition of data-driven selection) | 完全覆盖 Full |

**ICO Std 2002 Mapping** | **ICO Std 2002映射**

§05.1 establishes indicator selection criteria that require indicators to be selected based on their relevance and validity to the ranking purpose, not merely on data availability. §8.5 (Ranking Result Verification) provides for internal consistency checks (Cronbach's alpha ≥ 0.70), external validity checks, and robustness checks, which together validate the indicator system's effectiveness. This fully satisfies R7.

§05.1确立了指标选取准则，要求指标根据其与排名目的的相关性和有效性进行选择，而非仅仅基于数据可用性。§8.5（排名结果验证）规定内部一致性检验（克朗巴赫α ≥ 0.70）、外部效度检验和稳健性检验，共同验证指标体系的有效性。这完全满足R7。

### A.2.8 R8: Measure Outcomes in Preference to Inputs | 优先衡量产出而非投入

**Berlin Principle R8** | **柏林原则R8**

> "Measure outcomes in preference to inputs whenever possible. Measures of outcomes provide a more accurate assessment of the standing and/or quality of a given institution or program."
>
> "尽可能优先衡量产出而非投入。产出度量能更准确地评估给定机构或项目的地位和/或质量。"

| Aspect | Berlin Principle | ICO Std 2002 | Coverage |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| Outcome preference | R8 | §05.1 (Indicator Selection Criteria), §05.2 (Indicator Hierarchical Structure) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Input-output balance | R8 | §05.2 (Indicator classification by type) | 完全覆盖 Full |

**ICO Std 2002 Mapping** | **ICO Std 2002映射**

§05.2 requires that the indicator system include a classification of indicators by type (input, process, output, outcome), and §05.1 requires that outcome indicators be given priority in the selection process. The weighting provisions in Chapter 6 allow for differential weighting that can reflect the higher importance of outcome indicators. This satisfies R8.

§05.2要求指标体系包含按类型（投入、过程、产出、结果）对指标进行的分类，§05.1要求在选择过程中优先考虑结果指标。第6章的权重规定允许反映结果指标更高重要性的差异加权。这满足R8。

### A.2.9 R9: Weight Transparency and Stability | 权重透明度与稳定性

**Berlin Principle R9** | **柏林原则R9**

> "Make the weights assigned to different indicators (if used) prominent and limit changes to them. Changes in weights make it difficult for consumers to discern whether an institution's or program's status changed in the rankings due to an inherent difference or due to a methodological change."
>
> "应使不同指标的权重分配（若使用）显著可见，并限制权重变更。权重变更使消费者难以辨别机构或项目在排名中的地位变化是由于内在差异还是方法论变更。"

| Aspect | Berlin Principle | ICO Std 2002 | Coverage |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| Weight prominence | R9 | §9.1.2(d) (Weighting scheme disclosure) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Weight stability | R9 | §06.3 (Weight Change Control) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Weight change justification | R9 | §06.3.2 (Methodological Change Documentation) | 完全覆盖 Full |

**ICO Std 2002 Mapping** | **ICO Std 2002映射**

§9.1.2(d) requires disclosure of the weighting scheme and the method by which weights were determined. §06.3 (Weight Change Control) establishes requirements for limiting and documenting changes to weights, including the requirement that changes be based on methodological or conceptual considerations and not be used to produce different results. This fully satisfies R9.

§9.1.2(d)要求披露权重方案及权重确定方法。§06.3（权重变更控制）规定了限制和记录权重变更的要求，包括变更应基于方法论或概念考虑而非用于产生不同结果的要求。这完全满足R9。

ICO Std 2002通过其全面的独立性和公正性框架满足R10。§4.1确立独立性原则，包含利益冲突识别、披露和回避的具体要求。§4.4确立公正性原则，包括禁止付费上榜（§4.4.1）和利益相关方干预（§4.4.2），通过提供确保客观性的具体操作规则，超越了柏林原则。

### A.2.11 R11: Audited and Verifiable Data | 审计与可验证数据

**Berlin Principle R11** | **柏林原则R11**

> "Use audited and verifiable data whenever possible. Such data have several advantages, including the fact that they have been accepted by institutions and that they are comparable and compatible across institutions."
>
> "尽可能使用审计和可验证的数据。此类数据具有多个优势，包括已被机构接受且在机构间具有可比性和兼容性。"

| Aspect | Berlin Principle | ICO Std 2002 | Coverage |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| Verifiable data requirement | R11 | §7.1 (Data Source Classification), §7.2 (Data Collection Procedures) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Data audit trail | R11 | §10 (Audit and Traceability) | 完全覆盖 Full |
| Data quality assurance | R11 | §7.3 (Data Quality Metrics) | 超出覆盖 Exceeds |

**ICO Std 2002 Mapping** | **ICO Std 2002映射**

§7.1 classifies data sources by reliability tier, with Tier 1 (Official/Audited) data preferred. §7.2 requires that data collection procedures be documented and verifiable. §10 (Audit and Traceability) establishes a complete audit trail for all data and methodological decisions. §7.3 (Data Quality Metrics) goes beyond R11 by establishing quantitative data quality requirements including accuracy, completeness, timeliness, and consistency metrics.

§7.1按可靠性层级对数据源进行分类，优先使用一级（官方/审计）数据。§7.2要求记录数据采集程序并使其可验证。§10（审计与可追溯性）为所有数据和方法论决策建立完整的审计追踪。§7.3（数据质量度量）通过建立包括准确性、完整性、时效性和一致性度量在内的定量数据质量要求，超越了R11。

### A.2.12 R12: Scientific Data Collection Procedures | 科学数据采集程序

**Berlin Principle R12** | **柏林原则R12**

> "Include data that are collected with proper procedures for scientific data collection. Data collected from an unrepresentative or skewed subset of students, faculty, or other parties may not accurately represent an institution or program and should be excluded."
>

| Aspect | Berlin Principle | ICO Std 2002 | Coverage |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|----------|

**ICO Std 2002 Mapping** | **ICO Std 2002映射**

§7.2 establishes data collection procedures that must conform to scientific data collection standards, including requirements for sampling design, response rate thresholds, and data validation. §7.4 provides rules for handling missing data and outliers, including the requirement to exclude data from unrepresentative or skewed subsets. §7.3 establishes data quality metrics that can be used to identify and exclude low-quality data. This fully satisfies R12.

§7.2规定数据采集程序必须符合科学数据采集标准，包括抽样设计、回应率阈值和数据验证的要求。§7.4提供处理缺失数据和异常值的规则，包括排除不具代表性或偏斜子集数据的要求。§7.3建立数据质量度量，可用于识别和排除低质量数据。这完全满足R12。

### A.2.13 R13: Quality Assurance of Ranking Processes | 排名过程的质量保证

**Berlin Principle R13** | **柏林原则R13**

> "Apply measures of quality assurance to ranking processes themselves. These processes should take note of the expertise that is being applied to evaluate institutions and use this knowledge to evaluate the ranking itself. Rankings should be learning systems continuously utilizing this expertise to develop methodology."
>
> "对排名过程本身实施质量保证措施。排名应是学习系统，持续利用专业知识来发展方法论。"

## A.4 ICO Extensions Beyond Berlin Principles | ICO超出柏林原则的扩展

The following features of ICO Std 2002 have no direct counterpart in the Berlin Principles and represent extensions beyond the scope of the original IREG framework:

以下ICO Std 2002的特征在柏林原则中没有直接对应项，代表超出原始IREG框架范围的扩展：

### A.4.1 Four-Level Transparency System | 四级透明度体系

The Berlin Principles require transparency in general terms (R6). ICO Std 2002 establishes a graduated four-level transparency classification (§9.2) that provides:

柏林原则要求一般意义上的透明度（R6）。ICO Std 2002建立分级四级透明度分类（§9.2），提供：

- **Level 1: Summary Transparency** — minimum disclosure set
  一级：摘要透明——最低披露集

- **Level 2: Methodology Transparency** — full methodology documentation
  二级：方法论透明——完整方法论文件

- **Level 3: Data Transparency** — full data access for verification
  三级：数据透明——完整数据访问以供验证

- **Level 4: Full Reproducibility** — complete data and code for independent replication
  四级：完全可复现——完整数据和代码以供独立复制

This graduated system enables stakeholders to assess the transparency of a ranking publication against a defined standard and provides a pathway for ranking entities to progressively improve their transparency.

此分级体系使利益相关方能够根据定义的标准评估排名发布的透明度，并为排名实体提供逐步改进透明度的路径。

### A.4.2 DPP-CQ Integration Framework | DPP-CQ整合框架

The Data Processing Pipeline — Continuous Quality (DPP-CQ) integration framework (§10.4) provides a systematic mechanism for continuous quality improvement of ranking processes. This has no counterpart in the Berlin Principles, which address quality assurance only in general terms (R13).

数据处理管道—持续质量（DPP-CQ）整合框架（§10.4）提供排名过程持续质量改进的系统机制。这在柏林原则中没有对应项，后者仅以一般方式处理质量保证（R13）。

The DPP-CQ framework includes:
DPP-CQ框架包括：

a) automated data quality monitoring throughout the ranking lifecycle;
   排名生命周期中的自动化数据质量监控；

b) trigger-based quality alerts for data anomalies;
   基于触发器的数据异常质量警报；

c) feedback loops from ranking results to methodological improvement;
   从排名结果到方法论改进的反馈循环；

d) version control and change tracking for all methodological decisions.
   所有方法论决策的版本控制和变更追踪。

### A.4.3 Domain Neutrality and Multi-Domain Architecture | 领域中立与多领域架构

The Berlin Principles were designed specifically for higher education rankings. ICO Std 2002 generalises the ranking methodology framework to apply across all domains through a core-layer + extension-module architecture. This domain-neutral design (§4.2.1) ensures that the standard's requirements are applicable to any ranking context while allowing domain-specific adaptations through extension modules (SE-01 through SE-09).

柏林原则专门为高等教育排名设计。ICO Std 2002通过核心层+扩展模块架构，将排名方法论框架推广至适用于所有领域。此领域中立设计（§4.2.1）确保标准的要求适用于任何排名语境，同时允许通过扩展模块（SE-01至SE-09）进行领域特定适配。

### A.4.4 Cultural Sensitivity Requirements | 文化敏感性要求

While the Berlin Principles acknowledge the importance of cultural context (R5), ICO Std 2002 extends this by incorporating explicit cultural sensitivity requirements in the SE-05 (Cultural Heritage) extension module and by requiring that all ranking systems consider the cultural implications of their indicator design and data collection methods. This is particularly relevant for the Tianji Ranking's intangible cultural heritage track.

虽然柏林原则承认文化背景的重要性（R5），ICO Std 2002通过在SE-05（文化遗产）扩展模块中纳入明确的文化敏感性要求，并要求所有排名系统考虑其指标设计和数据采集方法的文化影响，对此进行了扩展。这对天机排行榜的非遗赛道尤为重要。

### A.4.5 Anti-Fraud and Anti-Manipulation Mechanisms | 反欺诈与反操控机制

The Berlin Principles do not address the risk of deliberate manipulation of ranking data by ranked entities. ICO Std 2002 includes explicit anti-fraud provisions (§7.5) that address:

柏林原则未涉及被排实体故意操控排名数据的风险。ICO Std 2002包含明确的反欺诈条款（§7.5），涉及：

a) detection and prevention of data manipulation (e.g., fabricated survey responses, inflated metrics);
   数据操纵的检测和预防（如伪造调查回应、虚增指标）；

b) anti-brush-clicking (反刷单) mechanisms for consumer product rankings;
   消费品排名的反刷单机制；

c) sanctions for entities found to have manipulated data.
   对被发现操纵数据的实体的制裁。

### A.4.6 UN DCP Alignment | 联合国DCP对齐

ICO Std 2002 aligns with the United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples (UNDRIP) and the UNESCO Convention for the Safeguarding of the Intangible Cultural Heritage (2003), providing a framework for ranking systems that respect the rights and dignity of communities and cultural practitioners. This alignment has no counterpart in the Berlin Principles.

ICO Std 2002与《联合国土著人民权利宣言》（UNDRIP）和联合国教科文组织《保护非物质文化遗产公约》（2003年）对齐，为尊重社区和文化从业者权利与尊严的排名系统提供框架。此对齐在柏林原则中没有对应项。

## A.5 Comparative Analysis | 对标分析总结

### A.5.1 Overall Assessment | 总体评估

ICO Std 2002 achieves full coverage of all 16 Berlin Principles, with 6 of 16 principles exceeded by ICO Std 2002's more detailed or broader provisions. No Berlin Principle is partially covered or uncovered. The areas of extension are primarily in:

ICO Std 2002实现对全部16项柏林原则的完全覆盖，其中6项原则被ICO Std 2002更详细或更广泛的条款所超越。没有柏林原则被部分覆盖或未覆盖。扩展领域主要在：

a) **Transparency granularity** — the four-level transparency system provides a graduated framework not present in the Berlin Principles;
   透明度粒度——四级透明度体系提供了柏林原则中不存在的分级框架；

b) **Data quality rigour** — quantitative data quality metrics and the DPP-CQ framework provide systematic quality assurance beyond the Berlin Principles' general requirements;
   数据质量严谨性——定量数据质量度量和DPP-CQ框架提供了超越柏林原则一般要求的系统质量保证；

c) **Anti-fraud provisions** — explicit mechanisms for preventing and detecting data manipulation;
   反欺诈条款——防止和检测数据操纵的明确机制；

d) **Domain scope** — generalisation from higher education to all ranking domains;
   领域范围——从高等教育推广至所有排名领域；

e) **Cultural sensitivity** — explicit requirements for cultural sensitivity in heritage and community contexts;
   文化敏感性——遗产和社区语境中文化敏感性的明确要求；

f) **User empowerment** — the user-adjustable weights framework provides a mechanism for consumer choice beyond the Berlin Principles' general recommendation.
   用户赋能——用户可调权重框架提供了超越柏林原则一般建议的消费者选择机制。

### A.5.2 Implications for IREG Ranking Audit | 对IREG排名审计的启示

An ICO Std 2002-compliant ranking system that covers the higher education domain would satisfy all IREG Ranking Audit criteria derived from the Berlin Principles. The additional provisions of ICO Std 2002 (transparency levels, data quality metrics, anti-fraud mechanisms) would provide additional evidence of quality that could strengthen the ranking's audit assessment.

符合ICO Std 2002的涵盖高等教育领域的排名系统将满足所有源自柏林原则的IREG排名审计准则。ICO Std 2002的附加条款（透明度等级、数据质量度量、反欺诈机制）将提供额外的质量证据，可加强排名的审计评估。

### A.5.3 Recommendations for Future Alignment | 未来对齐建议

a) ICO Std 2002 should be submitted to IREG for formal review and potential recognition as a complementary framework to the Berlin Principles.
   ICO Std 2002应提交IREG进行正式审查，并可能被认可为柏林原则的补充框架。

b) Future revisions of the Berlin Principles may consider incorporating the four-level transparency system and anti-fraud provisions pioneered by ICO Std 2002.
   柏林原则的未来修订可考虑纳入ICO Std 2002首创的四级透明度体系和反欺诈条款。

c) Cross-certification pathways between IREG Ranking Audit and ICO Std 2002 compliance assessment should be explored.
   应探索IREG排名审计与ICO Std 2002合规评估之间的交叉认证路径。
