## D.0 Front Matter | 前言

### D.0.1 Introduction | 引言

This Annex establishes the Rules of Procedure for the Ranking Methodology Technical Committee (RMTC), the governing body responsible for the maintenance, interpretation, and evolution of ICO Std 2002. As a normative annex, this document carries the same authoritative force as the main body of the standard and shall be read in conjunction with the core specification (Chapters 0–11) and the other annexes (A through C).

本附录确立了排名方法学技术委员会（RMTC）的议事规则。RMTC是负责ICO Std 2002的维护、解释和演进的治理机构。作为规范性附录，本文件与标准正文具有同等效力，应与核心规范（第0–11章）及其他附录（A至C）一并阅读。

The establishment of formal rules of procedure is essential to ensure that the governance of the standard is conducted in a manner that is transparent, accountable, and consistent with international best practice for technical governance bodies. These rules are modelled on the procedural frameworks of established international standards organisations, including the International Organization for Standardization (ISO), the International Ranking Expert Group (IREG), and the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC).

制定正式议事规则的目的在于确保标准的治理以透明、负责任且符合国际技术治理机构最佳实践的方式进行。这些规则参照了国际标准组织（包括国际标准化组织ISO、国际排名专家组IREG和国际电工委员会IEC）的程序框架。

### D.0.2 Authority | 授权依据

These Rules of Procedure are established pursuant to Chapter 11 of ICO Std 2002, which mandates the creation of the RMTC as the governance body for this standard, and pursuant to the authority of the International Communication Organization (ICO) as the issuing body of the standard. Any amendment to these Rules shall require approval by a two-thirds majority of the RMTC and ratification by the ICO Secretary-General.

本议事规则依据ICO Std 2002第11章设立，该章规定创建RMTC作为本标准的治理机构，同时依据国际传播组织（ICO）作为本标准发布机构的授权。对本议事规则的任何修订须经RMTC三分之二多数通过，并由ICO秘书长批准。

## D.1 Purpose and Scope | 目的与范围

### D.1.1 Purpose of These Rules of Procedure | 本议事规则的目的

The purpose of these Rules of Procedure is to establish a comprehensive governance framework for the operation of the Ranking Methodology Technical Committee (RMTC). Specifically, these Rules:

本议事规则的目的是为排名方法学技术委员会（RMTC）的运作建立全面的治理框架。具体而言，这些规则：

a) define the organisational structure, membership, and decision-making processes of the RMTC;
界定RMTC的组织结构、成员资格和决策程序；

b) establish procedures for the conduct of meetings, deliberations, and communications among committee members;
建立委员会会议、审议和沟通的程序；

c) prescribe mechanisms for transparency, accountability, and conflict of interest management;
规定透明度、问责制和利益冲突管理的机制；

d) provide a framework for the interaction between the RMTC, the ICO, and external stakeholders; and
提供RMTC与ICO及外部利益相关方之间互动的框架；且

e) ensure that the maintenance and evolution of ICO Std 2002 is conducted in a manner consistent with the principles of openness, fairness, and rigour enshrined in the standard itself.
确保ICO Std 2002的维护和演进以与标准本身所秉持的开放、公正和严谨原则相一致的方式进行。

Without such a governance framework, the integrity and credibility of the standard could be compromised by ad hoc decision-making, insufficient transparency, or real or perceived conflicts of interest. These Rules are designed to prevent such outcomes and to provide stakeholders with confidence that the standard is governed in accordance with internationally recognised principles of technical governance.

若无此类治理框架，标准的完整性和公信力可能因临时决策、透明度不足或实际或表面的利益冲突而受到损害。这些规则旨在防止此类结果，并为利益相关方提供信心——标准按照国际公认的技术治理原则进行管理。

### D.1.2 Scope of Application | 适用范围

These Rules of Procedure shall apply to all governance activities undertaken by the RMTC in relation to ICO Std 2002, including but not limited to:

本议事规则应适用于RMTC就ICO Std 2002开展的所有治理活动，包括但不限于：

a) the consideration and approval of amendments to the core specification (Chapters 0–11);
审议和批准核心规范（第0–11章）的修订；

b) the approval of new extension modules (SE-11 and beyond) and amendments to existing extension modules (SE-01 through SE-10);
批准新的扩展模块（SE-11及以后）以及对现有扩展模块（SE-01至SE-10）的修订；

c) the interpretation of standard provisions in response to requests from ranking entities, auditors, or other stakeholders;
应排名实体、审计方或其他利益相关方的请求对标准条款进行解释；

d) the adjudication of appeals relating to the application or interpretation of the standard;
裁决与标准适用或解释相关的申诉；

e) the approval and publication of annual transparency reports;
批准和发布年度透明度报告；

f) the assessment and certification of standard conformance by ranking entities;
评估和认证排名实体对标准的一致性；

g) coordination with other international standards bodies, including IREG, ISO/TC 279, and relevant technical committees; and
与其他国际标准组织的协调，包括IREG、ISO/TC 279及相关技术委员会；且

h) any other activities related to the maintenance, development, and promotion of ICO Std 2002.
与ICO Std 2002的维护、开发和推广相关的任何其他活动。

These Rules shall also apply to any future revision of ICO Std 2002 (e.g., ICO Std 2002:2027 or subsequent editions) unless and until a revised set of Rules is adopted by the RMTC.

本议事规则亦适用于ICO Std 2002的任何未来修订版（例如ICO Std 2002:2027或后续版本），除非且直到RMTC通过修订版议事规则。

### D.1.3 Legal Basis and Authorising Authority | 法定授权来源

The RMTC is established by the International Communication Organization (ICO) pursuant to Chapter 11 of ICO Std 2002. Chapter 11 mandates the creation of a dedicated technical governance body to oversee the maintenance and evolution of the standard, ensuring that its provisions are applied consistently and that its development is guided by expert judgement and stakeholder input.

RMTC由国际传播组织（ICO）依据ICO Std 2002第11章设立。第11章要求设立专门的技术治理机构，负责监督标准的维护和演进，确保其条款得到一致适用，并确保其发展受到专业判断和利益相关方意见的指导。

The authority of the RMTC is derived from two sources: (a) the mandate conferred by ICO as the issuing body of the standard; and (b) the acceptance of the standard by ranking entities, auditors, and other stakeholders who rely on its provisions. The legitimacy of the RMTC thus rests on both institutional authority and stakeholder trust.

RMTC的权力来源于两个层面：（a）ICO作为标准发布机构赋予的职责；及（b）排名实体、审计方和其他依赖其条款的利益相关方对标准的接受。因此，RMTC的合法性既建立在机构权威之上，也建立在利益相关方的信任之上。

### D.1.4 Relationship with the Standard | 与标准正文的关系

This Annex is designated as a **normative annex** of ICO Std 2002. In accordance with the conventions of international standardisation (see ISO/IEC Directives, Part 2, Clause 6), a normative annex forms an integral part of the standard and has the same authoritative force as the main body. Compliance with the provisions of this Annex is therefore mandatory for any organisation that claims conformance with ICO Std 2002.

本附录被指定为ICO Std 2002的**规范性附录**。按照国际标准化惯例（见ISO/IEC指令第2部分第6条），规范性附录是标准的组成部分，与正文具有同等效力。因此，任何声称符合ICO Std 2002的组织都必须遵守本附录的规定。

In the event of any inconsistency between the provisions of this Annex and the provisions of the main body of the standard, the matter shall be referred to the RMTC for resolution. The RMTC's interpretation shall be definitive and binding on all parties claiming conformance with the standard.

若本附录条款与标准正文条款之间存在任何不一致，该事项应提交RMTC解决。RMTC的解释应为最终解释，对所有声称符合标准的各方具有约束力。

## D.2 Definitions | 定义

For the purposes of these Rules of Procedure, the following definitions shall apply. Where a term is also defined in Chapter 3 of the standard, the definition in Chapter 3 shall prevail unless the context of these Rules requires a different meaning.

就本议事规则而言，以下定义应适用。若术语同时在本标准第3章中有定义，则第3章的定义应优先适用，除非本规则的上下文要求不同含义。

### D.2.1 Committee | 委员会

The "Committee" refers to the Ranking Methodology Technical Committee (RMTC), the body established by ICO pursuant to Chapter 11 of ICO Std 2002 to govern the maintenance, interpretation, and evolution of the standard. The term "Committee" shall be used interchangeably with "RMTC" throughout these Rules.

"委员会"指排名方法学技术委员会（RMTC），即由ICO依据ICO Std 2002第11章设立、负责治理标准的维护、解释和演进的机构。在本规则全文中，"委员会"与"RMTC"可互换使用。

### D.2.2 Member | 委员

A "Member" refers to an individual who has been duly nominated, appointed, and confirmed as a voting member of the Committee in accordance with the procedures specified in Section D.6. Members possess full rights to participate in deliberations, propose motions, and cast votes.

"委员"指依据第D.6节规定的程序正式提名、任命和确认的委员会有投票权的成员。委员享有参与审议、提出动议和投票的完整权利。

### D.2.3 Chair | 主席

The "Chair" refers to the presiding officer of the Committee, elected by the Members from among the sitting Members in accordance with D.5.4. The Chair is responsible for convening and presiding over meetings, ensuring orderly conduct of business, and representing the Committee in its external relations. The Chair shall not have a regular vote but shall cast a tie-breaking vote when the votes are equally divided.

"主席"指委员会的主持官员，依据D.5.4由委员从现任委员中选举产生。主席负责召集和主持会议、确保议事有序进行，并在对外关系中代表委员会。主席不享有常规投票权，但在票数相等时投决定票。

### D.2.4 Vice-Chair | 副主席

The "Vice-Chair" refers to the officer elected to assist the Chair and to preside in the Chair's absence. The Vice-Chair shall be elected from among the Members in accordance with D.5.4 and shall have all the powers and duties of the Chair when acting as presiding officer.

"副主席"指协助主席并在主席缺席时主持会议的当选官员。副主席依据D.5.4从委员中选举产生，在担任主持官员时享有主席的所有权力和职责。

### D.2.5 Secretary | 秘书长

The "Secretary" refers to the officer appointed by the ICO Secretary-General to provide administrative and logistical support to the Committee. The Secretary is responsible for maintaining records, distributing meeting materials, preparing minutes, and ensuring the implementation of the Committee's decisions. The Secretary is not a Member of the Committee and does not have voting rights.

"秘书长"指由ICO秘书长任命、为委员会提供行政和后勤保障的官员。秘书长负责保存记录、分发会议材料、编制会议纪要，并确保委员会决定的实施。秘书长不是委员会委员，不享有投票权。

### D.2.6 Quorum | 法定人数

The "Quorum" refers to the minimum number of Members who must be present (physically or remotely) for the Committee to conduct valid business, as specified in D.7.4. No decision of the Committee shall be valid unless a quorum is present at the time the decision is made.

"法定人数"指委员会进行有效议事所需的最低出席委员人数，按D.7.4规定。除非作出决定时达到法定人数，否则委员会的任何决定均不具效力。

### D.2.7 Observer | 观察员

An "Observer" refers to an individual or representative of an organisation who has been invited to attend Committee meetings without voting rights, in accordance with D.5.2 (Category E). Observers may participate in discussions at the invitation of the Chair but may not propose motions, vote, or block consensus.

"观察员"指依据D.5.2（E类）获邀参加委员会会议但不享有投票权的个人或组织代表。观察员可在主席邀请下参与讨论，但不得提出动议、投票或阻止共识。

### D.2.8 Stakeholder | 利益相关方

A "Stakeholder" refers to any individual, organisation, or entity that has a direct or indirect interest in the development, application, or outcomes of ICO Std 2002. Stakeholders include but are not limited to: ranking entities, ranked entities, data providers, auditors, certification bodies, academic institutions, consumer protection organisations, and the general public.

"利益相关方"指对ICO Std 2002的制定、适用或结果有直接或间接利益的任何个人、组织或实体。利益相关方包括但不限于：排名实体、被排实体、数据提供方、审计方、认证机构、学术机构、消费者保护组织和公众。

### D.2.9 Conflict of Interest | 利益冲突

A "Conflict of Interest" exists when a Member has a personal, financial, professional, or other interest that could reasonably be perceived as influencing, or having the potential to influence, the Member's objectivity or impartiality in the performance of their duties as a member of the Committee. The types and management of conflicts of interest are specified in Section D.9.

"利益冲突"指委员存在个人、财务、专业或其他利益，且该利益可被合理地视为影响或可能影响其作为委员会委员履行职责时的客观性或公正性。利益冲突的类型和管理方式在第D.9节中规定。

### D.2.10 Consensus | 共识

"Consensus" refers to a general agreement among the Members participating in a decision, such that no Member present at the time of the decision sustains a strong objection to the proposed course of action. Consensus does not require unanimity; it is characterised by the absence of sustained and substantive opposition.

"共识"指参与决策的委员之间达成的普遍一致，使得在作出决定时在场的委员中没有人对拟议行动方案持强烈反对意见。共识不要求全体一致；其特征是不存在持续的、实质性的反对。

## D.3 Establishment and Legal Basis | 设立与法律依据

### D.3.1 Establishment Authority | 设立依据

The RMTC is established pursuant to Chapter 11 of ICO Std 2002 (Compliance Declaration and Certification), which mandates the creation of a dedicated technical governance body to: (a) oversee the maintenance and evolution of the standard; (b) adjudicate disputes regarding the interpretation and application of the standard; (c) approve amendments, extension modules, and interpretive guidance; and (d) ensure the ongoing credibility and relevance of the standard.

RMTC依据ICO Std 2002第11章（合规性声明与认证）设立，该章要求设立专门的技术治理机构，以：（a）监督标准的维护和演进；（b）裁决有关标准解释和适用的争议；（c）批准修订、扩展模块和解释性指引；及（d）确保标准的持续公信力和相关性。

### D.3.2 Legal Status | 法律地位

The RMTC is established as an independent technical governance body under the auspices of the International Communication Organization (ICO). The RMTC operates with operational autonomy in matters of technical decision-making, subject to the overarching authority of the ICO General Assembly in matters of institutional governance, budget, and strategic direction.

RMTC在国际传播组织（ICO）的主持下设立为独立的技术治理机构。RMTC在技术决策事务中具有运作自主权，但在机构治理、预算和战略方向等事务上受ICO全体大会的最高管辖。

The RMTC shall have the authority to:
RMTC有权：

a) adopt and amend its own Rules of Procedure (subject to the requirements of Section D.12);
采纳和修订其自身的议事规则（须符合第D.12节的要求）；

b) establish sub-committees, working groups, or task forces as may be required;
根据需要设立小组委员会、工作组或专项工作组；

c) issue interpretive guidance and technical corrigenda to the standard;
发布标准的解释性指引和技术勘误；

d) approve or reject proposals for amendments to the standard; and
批准或驳回对标准的修订提案；且

e) communicate directly with external bodies on matters within its competence.
在其职权范围内直接与外部机构沟通。

### D.3.3 Independence Principle | 独立性原则

The RMTC shall exercise its functions independently and impartially. The technical decisions of the RMTC — including decisions regarding amendments to the standard, interpretation of provisions, and adjudication of appeals — shall not be influenced by any commercial interest, political pressure, or the interests of any individual ranking entity, ranked entity, or stakeholder group.

RMTC应独立、公正地行使其职能。RMTC的技术决策——包括关于标准修订、条款解释和申诉裁决的决定——不应受到任何商业利益、政治压力或任何排名实体、被排实体或利益相关方群体的利益的影响。

To safeguard this independence, the following institutional arrangements shall be maintained:
为保障这一独立性，应维持以下制度安排：

a) **Financial independence**: The RMTC shall be funded through ICO's institutional budget, not through fees charged to ranking entities or other commercial revenues. Members shall serve without remuneration but may be reimbursed for reasonable travel and accommodation expenses incurred in the performance of their duties.
**财务独立性**：RMTC应通过ICO的机构预算获得资金，而非通过对排名实体收取的费用或其他商业收入。委员应无偿服务，但可就履行职责过程中产生的合理差旅费和住宿费获得报销。

b) **Appointment independence**: While Members are formally appointed by the ICO Secretary-General, the nomination and selection process shall be designed to ensure that appointees are chosen on the basis of their expertise and integrity, not their affiliation with any particular organisation or interest group.
**任命独立性**：虽然委员由ICO秘书长正式任命，但提名和遴选程序应确保被任命者基于其专业能力和正直品格被选中，而非因其与任何特定组织或利益群体的隶属关系。

c) **Decision-making independence**: ICO, as the issuing body, may attend RMTC meetings as an observer but shall not participate in RMTC decisions. The ICO Secretary-General retains the authority to ratify amendments approved by the RMTC but may not reject a ratification on technical grounds; ratification may only be withheld on procedural grounds (e.g., if the approval process did not follow the prescribed Rules of Procedure).
**决策独立性**：ICO作为发布机构，可作为观察员参加RMTC会议，但不得参与RMTC的决策。ICO秘书长保留批准RMTC批准的修订的权力，但不得以技术理由拒绝批准；仅可以程序理由暂缓批准（例如，如果批准过程未遵循规定的议事规则）。

### D.3.4 Three Red Lines | 三条红线制度

The independence of the RMTC is further reinforced by the "Three Red Lines" (三条红线) established in ICO Std 2002. These fundamental prohibitions constitute non-negotiable constraints on the operation of any ranking system claiming conformance with the standard and, by extension, on the governance of the standard itself:

RMTC的独立性还通过ICO Std 2002中确立的"三条红线"得到进一步加强。这些根本性禁令构成了任何声称符合标准的排名系统运作的不可协商的约束，并由此延伸至标准本身的治理：

a) **Zero Fees (零收费)**: The RMTC shall not charge any fees to ranking entities, ranked entities, or any other party in connection with its governance activities. The interpretation of standard provisions, the adjudication of appeals, and the provision of compliance guidance shall all be provided free of charge. This prohibition ensures that the RMTC's decisions cannot be influenced — or perceived to be influenced — by financial considerations.
**零收费**：RMTC在治理活动中不得向排名实体、被排实体或任何其他方收取任何费用。标准条款的解释、申诉的裁决以及合规指引的提供均应免费。此项禁令确保RMTC的决定不受——或被视为受——财务因素的影响。

b) **Zero Score Adjustment (零调分)**: The RMTC shall not direct or permit any adjustment to the scores or rankings produced by a conformant ranking system for the purpose of favouring or disfavoring any particular entity. The RMTC's role is to govern the methodology, not the outcomes. Any attempt to manipulate ranking results through the RMTC's governance mechanisms shall constitute a fundamental breach of these Rules.
**零调分**：RMTC不得指示或允许对合规排名系统产生的分数或排名进行任何调整，以利于或不利于任何特定实体。RMTC的角色是治理方法论，而非结果。任何试图通过RMTC的治理机制操控排名结果的行为，均构成对本规则的根本性违反。

c) **No Political or Administrative Rankings (不做政绩排名)**: The RMTC shall not approve, endorse, or facilitate any ranking system whose primary purpose is to serve as a tool for political evaluation, administrative performance assessment, or government accountability ranking. ICO Std 2002 is designed for the ranking of products, brands, services, educational institutions, cultural heritage, and similar entities — not for the political evaluation of governmental bodies or officials.
**不做政绩排名**：RMTC不得批准、认可或便利任何以政治评估、行政绩效考核或政府问责排名为主要目的的排名系统。ICO Std 2002旨在用于产品、品牌、服务、教育机构、文化遗产及类似实体的排名——而非用于对政府机构或官员的政治评估。

These three red lines shall be treated as jus cogens provisions of these Rules — that is, peremptory norms from which no derogation is permitted. Any amendment to these Rules that would weaken, qualify, or create exceptions to the Three Red Lines shall be void ab initio.

这三条红线应被视为本规则的绝对法条款——即不允许任何减损的强制性规范。任何旨在削弱、限定或为三条红线创设例外的本规则修订应自始无效。

## D.4 Mandate and Functions | 职权与职能

### D.4.1 Core Functions | 核心职能

The RMTC shall perform the following core functions:
RMTC应履行以下核心职能：

a) **Approval and Publication of Standard Amendments** | **审批和发布标准修订**: The RMTC shall consider proposals for amendments to the core specification (Chapters 0–11), evaluate their technical merit, and, where appropriate, approve them for inclusion in the next edition or corrigendum of the standard. All amendments shall be subject to the procedures specified in Section D.12.
**审批和发布标准修订**：RMTC应审议对核心规范（第0–11章）的修订提案，评估其技术合理性，并在适当情况下批准将其纳入标准的下一版或勘误表。所有修订应遵守第D.12节规定的程序。

b) **Approval of Extension Modules** | **审批扩展模块**: The RMTC shall consider proposals for new extension modules (e.g., SE-11 and beyond) and amendments to existing extension modules. Approval shall require a two-thirds majority vote and shall be contingent upon a satisfactory demonstration that the proposed module: (i) addresses a coherent ranking domain not adequately covered by existing modules; (ii) is consistent with the core principles and methodology of the standard; and (iii) has been developed through a consultative process with relevant stakeholders.
**审批扩展模块**：RMTC应审议新扩展模块（例如SE-11及以后）和对现有扩展模块的修订提案。批准需三分之二多数票，且须令人满意地证明拟议模块：（i）涉及现有模块未充分涵盖的连贯排名领域；（ii）与标准的核心原则和方法论一致；且（iii）已通过与相关利益相关方的协商过程开发。

c) **Adjudication of Methodological Disputes** | **审议排名方法论争议**: The RMTC shall serve as the final arbiter of disputes regarding the interpretation or application of the standard's methodological provisions. Such disputes may arise from: (i) disagreements between ranking entities and auditors regarding conformance determinations; (ii) challenges to the validity of specific methodological provisions; or (iii) requests for interpretive guidance on ambiguous provisions.
**审议排名方法论争议**：RMTC应作为标准方法论条款解释或适用争议的最终裁决者。此类争议可能源于：（i）排名实体与审计方之间关于一致性判定的分歧；（ii）对特定方法论条款有效性的质疑；或（iii）对模糊条款请求解释性指引。

d) **Appeal Handling** | **受理和裁决申诉**: The RMTC shall establish and maintain an appeal mechanism (see Appendix D-B) through which any stakeholder may challenge: (i) a conformance determination made by an auditor; (ii) a decision of the RMTC or its sub-committees; or (iii) the alleged non-conformance of a ranking system with the standard. Appeals shall be processed in accordance with the procedures specified in Appendix D-B.
**受理和裁决申诉**：RMTC应建立和维护申诉机制（见附录D-B），任何利益相关方可通过该机制对以下事项提出质疑：（i）审计方作出的一致性判定；（ii）RMTC或其小组委员会的决定；或（iii）排名系统据称不符合标准的情况。申诉应按附录D-B规定的程序处理。

e) **Annual Transparency Reporting** | **发布年度透明度报告**: The RMTC shall prepare and publish an annual transparency report within the first quarter of each calendar year, covering the activities of the preceding year. The content requirements for the annual transparency report are specified in Section D.10.
**发布年度透明度报告**：RMTC应在每个日历年第一季度编制和发布上一年度的年度透明度报告。年度透明度报告的内容要求在第D.10节中规定。

f) **Conformance Certification Assessment** | **合规性认证评估**: The RMTC shall establish criteria and procedures for the accreditation of certification bodies that assess ranking systems for conformance with ICO Std 2002. The RMTC shall periodically review the performance of accredited certification bodies and may revoke accreditation for cause.
**合规性认证评估**：RMTC应建立评估排名系统是否符合ICO Std 2002的认证机构的认可标准和程序。RMTC应定期审查已认可认证机构的绩效，并可因正当理由撤销认可。

g) **International Liaison and Coordination** | **与国际标准组织的联络协调**: The RMTC shall maintain active liaison with relevant international standards bodies, including: (i) IREG (International Ranking Expert Group); (ii) ISO/TC 279 (Innovation Management); (iii) ISO/CASCO (Conformity Assessment); and (iv) other bodies whose work intersects with ranking methodology. The RMTC may establish formal liaison agreements with these bodies, including the exchange of observers and the harmonisation of requirements.
**与国际标准组织的联络协调**：RMTC应与相关国际标准组织保持积极联络，包括：（i）IREG（国际排名专家组）；（ii）ISO/TC 279（创新管理）；（iii）ISO/CASCO（合格评定）；及（iv）其工作与排名方法学交叉的其他机构。RMTC可与这些机构建立正式联络协议，包括互派观察员和要求协调。

### D.4.2 Boundaries — What the RMTC Does Not Do | RMTC不做什么（明确边界）

For the avoidance of doubt, the RMTC shall **not**:
为明确起见，RMTC**不得**：

a) **Directly execute rankings**: The RMTC is a governance body, not a ranking entity. It shall not design, implement, or publish any ranking system. Its role is to set and enforce the methodological standards by which ranking entities operate, not to conduct rankings itself.
**直接执行排名**：RMTC是治理机构，而非排名实体。它不得设计、实施或发布任何排名系统。其角色是设定和执行排名实体运作的方法论标准，而非自行开展排名。

b) **Participate in commercial operations**: The RMTC shall not engage in any commercial activity, including the sale of ranking data, licensing of ranking methodologies, or provision of consulting services to ranking entities. The zero-fee principle (see D.3.4) applies to all RMTC activities.
**参与商业运营**：RMTC不得从事任何商业活动，包括出售排名数据、许可排名方法论或向排名实体提供咨询服务。零收费原则（见D.3.4）适用于RMTC的所有活动。

c) **Substitute for audit bodies**: The RMTC shall not perform the functions of an independent audit or certification body. While the RMTC establishes the criteria and procedures for conformance assessment, the actual assessment shall be carried out by independent, accredited audit bodies. The separation of standard-setting (RMTC) from assessment (audit bodies) is a fundamental governance principle.
**替代审计机构**：RMTC不得履行独立审计或认证机构的职能。虽然RMTC建立一致性评估的标准和程序，但实际评估应由独立的、经认可的审计机构进行。标准制定（RMTC）与评估（审计机构）的分离是一项基本的治理原则。

d) **Engage in political or administrative evaluations**: Consistent with the third red line (see D.3.4(c)), the RMTC shall not facilitate or endorse any ranking system designed for political evaluation or government performance assessment.
**参与政治或行政评估**：与第三条红线（见D.3.4(c)）一致，RMTC不得便利或认可任何为政治评估或政府绩效考核设计的排名系统。

## D.5 Composition of the Committee | 委员会组成

### D.5.1 Size | 委员规模

The Committee shall comprise a minimum of seven (7) and a maximum of fifteen (15) voting Members. The number of Members shall, wherever practicable, be an odd number to minimise the likelihood of tied votes. The exact number of Members shall be determined by the RMTC at its inaugural meeting and may be adjusted by a two-thirds majority vote as circumstances require, subject to the minimum and maximum limits specified herein.

委员会应由不少于七（7）名、不超过十五（15）名有投票权的委员组成。委员人数应尽可能为奇数，以最大限度地减少平票的可能性。委员的确切人数应由RMTC在其首次会议上确定，并可经三分之二多数票根据实际情况进行调整，但须遵守本文规定的最低和最高限额。

### D.5.2 Categories of Seats | 五类席位

The Members of the Committee shall be distributed across five categories to ensure balanced representation of the diverse expertise and perspectives required for effective governance of the standard:

委员会委员应在五个类别中分配，以确保均衡代表有效治理标准所需的多样化专业知识和观点：

**Category A: Methodology Experts | A类：方法学专家**

A minimum of two (2) Members shall be methodology experts, defined as individuals with demonstrated expertise in one or more of the following fields: ranking methodology, statistics, psychometrics, evaluation science, data science, or a related quantitative discipline. Category A Members shall hold at least a doctoral degree or equivalent professional experience in their field of expertise, and shall have a record of published contributions to their discipline.

不少于两（2）名委员应为方法学专家，其定义为在以下一个或多个领域具有经证明的专业能力的人员：排名方法学、统计学、心理测量学、评估科学、数据科学或相关定量学科。A类委员应在其专业领域持有博士学位或同等专业经验，并应有其学科的已发表贡献记录。

**Category B: Industry Representatives | B类：行业代表**

A minimum of two (2) Members shall be industry representatives, defined as individuals with substantial professional experience in a domain subject to ranking (including but not limited to brand management, education, tourism, cultural heritage, consumer products, and sustainability). Category B Members shall be nominated by recognised industry bodies or professional associations and shall provide a letter of support from their nominating organisation.

不少于两（2）名委员应为行业代表，其定义为在排名涉及领域（包括但不限于品牌管理、教育、旅游、文化遗产、消费品和可持续发展）具有丰富专业经验的人员。B类委员应由公认的行业机构或专业协会提名，并应提供其提名组织的支持信。

**Category C: Independent Audit/Legal Experts | C类：独立审计/法律专家**

A minimum of one (1) Member shall be an independent audit or legal expert, defined as an individual with professional qualifications and experience in auditing, quality assurance, conformity assessment, or legal practice related to standards or technical regulation. Category C Members play a critical role in ensuring the procedural integrity of the RMTC's operations and the enforceability of its decisions.

不少于一（1）名委员应为独立审计或法律专家，其定义为具有审计、质量保证、合格评定或与标准或技术法规相关的法律实践专业资格和经验的人员。C类委员在确保RMTC运作的程序完整性和其决定的可执行性方面发挥关键作用。

**Category D: ICO Appointed Representatives | D类：ICO指派的常任代表**

One to two (1–2) Members shall be appointed directly by the ICO to serve as standing representatives. Category D Members serve as the institutional link between the ICO and the RMTC, ensuring alignment between the standard's governance and the ICO's strategic direction. Category D Members shall not hold any position within the ICO's executive management to preserve the RMTC's operational independence.

一（1）至两（2）名委员应由ICO直接任命为常任代表。D类委员充当ICO与RMTC之间的制度纽带，确保标准治理与ICO战略方向的对齐。D类委员不得在ICO的执行管理层中担任任何职务，以保持RMTC的运作独立性。

**Category E: Observers | E类：观察员**

There shall be no limit on the number of Observers. Observers may be representatives of academic institutions, international organisations, civil society groups, standards bodies, or other entities with an interest in the governance of ranking methodology. Observers shall have the right to attend meetings, receive meeting materials, and participate in discussions (at the Chair's discretion), but shall not have voting rights, may not propose motions, and may not block consensus. Observers may be invited to serve on working groups or sub-committees.

观察员人数不设上限。观察员可以是学术机构、国际组织、民间社会团体、标准机构或对排名方法论治理有兴趣的其他实体的代表。观察员有权参加会议、接收会议材料并在主席裁量下参与讨论，但不享有投票权、不得提出动议且不得阻止共识。观察员可受邀加入工作组或小组委员会。

### D.5.3 Diversity Requirements | 多元化要求

The composition of the Committee shall reflect the following diversity principles:
委员会的组成应反映以下多元化原则：

a) **Geographic distribution**: The Members shall, to the extent practicable, represent a balanced geographic distribution across at least three of the following regions: Africa, Asia-Pacific, Europe, Latin America and the Caribbean, and North America. No single region shall account for more than half of the voting Members.
**地域分布**：委员应在可行范围内代表以下至少三个地区的均衡地域分布：非洲、亚太地区、欧洲、拉丁美洲和加勒比地区以及北美。任何单一地区不得超过有投票权委员的一半。

b) **Gender balance**: The ICO and the RMTC shall strive for gender balance in the composition of the Committee. As a target, neither gender shall constitute less than 30% of the voting Members.
**性别平衡**：ICO和RMTC应努力在委员会组成中实现性别平衡。作为目标，任何性别不应少于有投票权委员的30%。

c) **Professional complementarity**: The Members collectively shall possess expertise spanning the full range of competencies required for the RMTC's mandate, including methodological rigour, domain knowledge, audit and quality assurance, legal and regulatory expertise, and stakeholder engagement.
**专业互补**：委员集体应具备涵盖RMTC职责所需全部能力范围的专业知识，包括方法论严谨性、领域知识、审计和质量保证、法律和监管专业知识以及利益相关方参与。

### D.5.4 Election of Chair and Vice-Chair | 主席和副主席的产生方式

The Chair and Vice-Chair shall be elected by the voting Members from among their number at the first meeting of each committee term (see D.6.4). The election shall be conducted by secret ballot. A candidate shall be elected if they receive a majority of the votes cast. If no candidate receives a majority in the first round, a second round shall be held between the two candidates receiving the highest number of votes.

主席和副主席应由有投票权的委员在每届委员会任期的首次会议上从其成员中选举产生（见D.6.4）。选举应以无记名投票方式进行。候选人须获得所投票数的多数方可当选。若第一轮中无候选人获得多数，则应在获得最高票数的两名候选人之间进行第二轮选举。

The Chair and Vice-Chair shall each serve a term of two (2) years, coinciding with the regular meeting cycle. They may be re-elected once for an additional two-year term. The Chair and Vice-Chair shall not be from the same Category of membership.

主席和副主席各任期两（2）年，与定期会议周期一致。可连选连任一届额外两年任期。主席和副主席不得来自同一类别的委员。

## D.6 Nomination, Appointment and Term | 提名、任命与任期

### D.6.1 Nomination Procedures | 提名程序

Candidates for membership in the RMTC may be nominated through either of the following two channels:

RMTC委员候选人可通过以下两种渠道之一提名：

a) **Self-nomination**: Any individual who meets the eligibility criteria for the relevant Category may submit a self-nomination to the Secretary, accompanied by: (i) a curriculum vitae; (ii) a statement of interest explaining the candidate's motivation and relevant expertise; and (iii) for Category B candidates, a letter of support from a recognised industry body or professional association.
**自荐**：符合相关类别资格标准的任何个人均可向秘书长提交自荐，附：（i）个人简历；（ii）说明候选人动机和相关专业能力的兴趣声明；及（iii）对B类候选人，来自公认行业机构或专业协会的支持信。

b) **Nomination by a Member or Stakeholder Organisation**: Any sitting Member of the RMTC, or any recognised stakeholder organisation, may nominate a candidate by submitting to the Secretary: (i) the candidate's curriculum vitae; (ii) a letter of nomination explaining the basis for the recommendation; and (iii) the candidate's written consent to be nominated.
**委员或利益相关方组织推荐**：RMTC的任何现任委员或任何公认的利益相关方组织均可通过向秘书长提交以下材料提名候选人：（i）候选人的个人简历；（ii）说明推荐依据的推荐信；及（iii）候选人同意被提名的书面确认。

All nominations shall be submitted to the Secretary no later than sixty (60) days before the scheduled date of the meeting at which the appointment is to be considered.

所有提名应在预定审议任命的会议日期前不少于六十（60）天提交给秘书长。

### D.6.2 Qualification Review | 资格审核

Upon receipt of all nominations, the Secretary shall compile a list of candidates and distribute it to all sitting Members no later than forty-five (45) days before the scheduled meeting. The Members shall review the qualifications of each candidate and may raise objections within fifteen (15) days of receipt.

在收到所有提名后，秘书长应在预定会议前不少于四十五（45）天编制候选人名单并分发给所有现任委员。委员应审查每位候选人的资格，并可在收到名单后十五（15）天内提出异议。

The appointment of each candidate shall require the affirmative vote of a majority of the sitting Members. Voting shall be conducted by secret ballot. Members shall evaluate candidates based on their professional qualifications, relevant experience, and capacity to contribute to the RMTC's mandate.

每位候选人的任命需获得现任委员多数的赞成票。投票应以无记名投票方式进行。委员应根据候选人的专业资格、相关经验和为RMTC职责做出贡献的能力进行评估。

### D.6.3 Formal Appointment | 正式任命

Following the affirmative vote of the Members, the Secretary shall submit the list of approved candidates to the ICO Secretary-General for formal appointment. The ICO Secretary-General shall issue a letter of appointment to each approved candidate within thirty (30) days of receipt of the RMTC's resolution.

在委员投赞成票后，秘书长应将已批准候选人名单提交ICO秘书长正式任命。ICO秘书长应在收到RMTC决议后三十（30）天内向每位已批准的候选人发出任命函。

The letter of appointment shall specify: (a) the Category of membership; (b) the term of appointment; (c) the rights and responsibilities of membership; and (d) the requirement to sign the Code of Conduct (see Appendix D-A).

任命函应指定：（a）委员类别；（b）任期；（c）委员的权利和责任；及（d）签署行为准则的要求（见附录D-A）。

### D.6.4 Term of Office | 任期

Each Member shall serve a term of three (3) years, commencing from the date specified in their letter of appointment. A Member may be reappointed for one additional term of three (3) years, for a maximum total service of six (6) years. After completing the maximum term, a Member shall be ineligible for reappointment for a period of at least two (2) years.

每位委员应任三（3）年，自任命函中规定的日期起算。委员可再连任三（3）年一届，最长服务期限总计六（6）年。完成最长任期后，委员应在至少两（2）年内不具备连任资格。

### D.6.5 Staggered Terms | 交错任期制

To ensure continuity of the Committee's work, the terms of the initial Members shall be staggered. At the inaugural meeting, the ICO, in consultation with the elected Chair, shall designate approximately half of the initial Members to serve an initial term of two (2) years, with the remainder serving an initial term of three (3) years. This ensures that not all Members' terms expire simultaneously, preserving institutional memory and operational continuity.

为确保委员会工作的连续性，初始委员的任期应交错安排。在首次会议上，ICO应与当选主席协商，指定约半数的初始委员首任期为两（2）年，其余委员首任期为三（3）年。这确保并非所有委员的任期同时届满，保持机构记忆和运作连续性。

### D.6.6 Filling of Vacancies | 空缺递补

In the event that a vacancy arises during a Member's term — whether due to resignation, death, incapacitation, removal (see D.6.7), or any other cause — the vacancy shall be filled in accordance with the following procedures:

若委员任期中出现空缺——无论是由于辞职、去世、丧失行为能力、免职（见D.6.7）还是任何其他原因——该空缺应按以下程序递补：

a) The Secretary shall notify the Committee of the vacancy within seven (7) days of its occurrence;
秘书长应在空缺发生后七（7）天内通知委员会；

b) Nominations for the vacant seat shall be solicited in accordance with D.6.1;
空缺席位的提名应按D.6.1征集；

c) The replacement Member shall be appointed in accordance with D.6.2 and D.6.3; and
替换委员应按D.6.2和D.6.3任命；且

d) The replacement Member shall serve for the remainder of the unexpired term. If the unexpired term exceeds two (2) years, this period shall count as a full term for the purposes of D.6.4.
替换委员应服务至未满任期的剩余期限。若未满任期超过两（2）年，则该期间就D.6.4而言应视为一个完整任期。

### D.6.7 Removal from Office | 免职条件

A Member may be removed from office by a two-thirds majority vote of the other Members in any of the following circumstances:

委员可在以下任何情况下经其他委员三分之二多数票免职：

a) **Serious breach of the Code of Conduct**: The Member has committed a serious breach of the RMTC Code of Conduct (see Appendix D-A), including but not limited to: failure to disclose a material conflict of interest; misuse of confidential information; or conduct that brings the RMTC or ICO into disrepute.
**严重违反行为准则**：委员严重违反了RMTC行为准则（见附录D-A），包括但不限于：未披露实质性利益冲突；滥用机密信息；或损害RMTC或ICO声誉的行为。

b) **Persistent absence**: The Member has failed to attend three (3) consecutive meetings of the Committee without providing a satisfactory explanation. The Chair shall issue a written warning after the second consecutive absence.
**连续缺席**：委员连续三（3）次会议未能出席且未提供令人满意的解释。主席应在第二次连续缺席后发出书面警告。

c) **Failure to manage conflict of interest**: The Member has failed to recuse themselves from a matter in which they have a declared conflict of interest, in violation of D.9.2.
**未管理利益冲突**：委员在已申报利益冲突的事项中未按D.9.2回避。

d) **Incapacity**: The Member is, by reason of physical or mental incapacity, unable to perform the duties of membership.
**丧失行为能力**：委员因身体或精神原因无法履行委员职责。

The Member under consideration for removal shall be given written notice of the proposed removal, the grounds therefor, and an opportunity to be heard by the Committee before the vote is taken. The Member under consideration shall not participate in the vote on their own removal.

拟被免职的委员应收到书面通知，说明拟议的免职及其理由，并在投票前获得向委员会陈述意见的机会。被审议的委员不得参与对其自身免职的投票。

## D.7 Meetings | 会议

### D.7.1 Regular Meetings | 定期会议

The Committee shall hold regular meetings at least twice per calendar year (i.e., at minimum once every six months). The schedule of regular meetings for the forthcoming year shall be agreed upon at the last regular meeting of the preceding year and communicated to all Members by the Secretary no later than thirty (30) days before the first scheduled meeting.

委员会应每日历年至少举行两次定期会议（即至少每六个月一次）。来年定期会议的时间表应在上年最后一次定期会议上商定，并由秘书长在第一次预定会议前不少于三十（30）天通知所有委员。

Additional agenda items may be proposed by any Member no later than twenty-one (21) days before the meeting date. The Chair, in consultation with the Secretary, shall finalise the agenda and distribute it to all Members.

任何委员可在会议日期前不少于二十一（21）天提出附加议程项目。主席应与秘书长协商后最终确定议程并分发给所有委员。

### D.7.2 Extraordinary Meetings | 特别会议

An extraordinary meeting of the Committee may be convened by:
委员会的特别会议可由以下方召集：

a) the Chair, acting on their own initiative; or
主席主动召集；或

b) a written request signed by at least one-third (1/3) of the voting Members, specifying the matters to be considered.
不少于三分之一（1/3）有投票权委员的书面请求，指明审议事项。

An extraordinary meeting convened pursuant to a Member request shall be scheduled within thirty (30) days of the receipt of the request by the Secretary. The Secretary shall notify all Members of the date, time, and provisional agenda of the extraordinary meeting no later than fourteen (14) days before the meeting.

依据委员请求召集的特别会议应在秘书长收到请求后三十（30）天内安排。秘书长应在会议前不少于十四（14）天通知所有委员特别会议的日期、时间和临时议程。

### D.7.3 Modes of Meeting | 会议形式

Meetings of the Committee may be held in any of the following modes:
委员会会议可以以下任何形式举行：

a) **In-person**: Members physically assemble at a location determined by the Chair;
**现场会议**：委员在主席确定的地点亲自聚集；

b) **Remote**: Members participate via videoconference, teleconference, or other real-time communication technology, provided that all Members can hear and speak to each other simultaneously;
**远程会议**：委员通过视频会议、电话会议或其他实时通讯技术参与，前提是所有委员可以同时听取和相互发言；

c) **Hybrid**: Some Members attend in person while others participate remotely, subject to the condition that all Members can hear and speak to each other simultaneously.
**混合会议**：部分委员亲自出席而其他委员远程参与，条件是所有委员可以同时听取和相互发言。

The Chair shall determine the mode of each meeting, taking into account Members' preferences, budget constraints, and the urgency and complexity of the matters to be considered.

主席应确定每次会议的形式，同时考虑委员的偏好、预算约束以及审议事项的紧迫性和复杂性。

### D.7.4 Quorum | 法定人数

The quorum for a meeting of the Committee shall be two-thirds (2/3) of the total number of voting Members (including vacant seats). No business shall be transacted at a meeting unless a quorum is present at the time the business is conducted.

委员会会议的法定人数应为有投票权委员总数（包括空缺席位）的三分之二（2/3）。除非在审议事项时达到法定人数，否则不得处理任何事务。

If a quorum is not present within thirty (30) minutes of the scheduled start time, the meeting shall be adjourned and reconvened at a time determined by the Chair, no sooner than seven (7) days and no later than twenty-one (21) days after the adjourned meeting. At the reconvened meeting, the quorum requirement shall be reduced to one-half (1/2) of the total number of voting Members.

若在预定开始时间后三十（30）分钟内未达到法定人数，会议应休会并在主席确定的时间重新召开，不早于休会后七（7）天且不迟于二十一（21）天。在重新召开的会议上，法定人数要求应降至有投票权委员总数的二分之一（1/2）。

### D.7.5 Agenda and Meeting Materials | 议程设置

The Secretary shall distribute the agenda and all supporting materials to all Members no later than fourteen (14) days before the scheduled date of each meeting. The materials shall include:

秘书长应在每次会议预定日期前不少于十四（14）天向所有委员分发议程和所有支持材料。材料应包括：

a) the proposed agenda, with estimated time allocations for each item;
拟议议程，含每项的预计时间安排；

b) minutes of the previous meeting (for confirmation);
上次会议纪要（供确认）；

c) all papers, proposals, and background documents relevant to the matters to be considered;
与审议事项相关的所有文件、提案和背景文件；

d) any written comments received from stakeholders or observers; and
从利益相关方或观察员收到的任何书面意见；及

e) a list of declared conflicts of interest relevant to the agenda items.
与议程项目相关的已申报利益冲突清单。

### D.7.6 Minutes | 会议纪要

The Secretary shall prepare draft minutes of each meeting and distribute them to all Members no later than fourteen (14) days after the conclusion of the meeting. The minutes shall record:

秘书长应在每次会议结束后不超过十四（14）天内编制会议纪要草案并分发给所有委员。纪要应记录：

a) the date, time, location, and mode of the meeting;
会议的日期、时间、地点和形式；

b) the Members present and absent;
出席和缺席的委员；

c) the agenda items considered;
审议的议程项目；

d) the decisions taken, including the voting results (for, against, abstentions) for each matter decided by vote;
所作的决定，包括每项以投票决定的事项的投票结果（赞成、反对、弃权）；

e) any dissenting opinions expressed by Members;
委员发表的任何反对意见；

f) any declarations of conflict of interest and the resulting recusal actions; and
任何利益冲突申报及由此产生的回避行动；及

g) the date and location of the next meeting.
下次会议的日期和地点。

The draft minutes shall be confirmed (with or without amendments) at the next regular meeting. Once confirmed, the minutes shall be signed by the Chair and become the official record of the meeting.

会议纪要草案应在下次定期会议上确认（有或无修正）。确认后，纪要应由主席签署并成为会议的正式记录。

### D.7.7 Public Accessibility | 公开性

The minutes of Committee meetings shall be made publicly available on the ICO Std 2002 website within thirty (30) days of their confirmation, subject to the redaction of:

委员会议纪要应在确认后三十（30）天内在ICO Std 2002网站上公开，但须删节：

a) information relating to specific appeal cases under consideration (to protect the confidentiality of the parties involved); and
与正在审议的具体申诉案件相关的信息（以保护相关方的保密性）；及

b) personal information of Members or third parties that is not relevant to the Committee's decisions (to comply with data protection obligations).
与委员会决定无关的委员或第三方个人信息（以遵守数据保护义务）。

The principle of public accessibility reflects the RMTC's commitment to transparency and accountability, and is consistent with the transparency requirements of the standard itself (see Chapter 9 of ICO Std 2002).

公开性原则反映了RMTC对透明度和问责制的承诺，与标准本身的透明度要求一致（见ICO Std 2002第9章）。

## D.8 Decision-Making | 决策程序

### D.8.1 Consensus First | 共识优先

The Committee shall endeavour to make all decisions by consensus. The Chair shall facilitate discussion aimed at achieving consensus, and shall not call for a vote until it is clear that further discussion is unlikely to produce consensus. Members are encouraged to express their views openly and constructively, and to seek compromises that accommodate legitimate concerns.

委员会应尽力以共识方式作出所有决定。主席应促进旨在达成共识的讨论，且直到明确进一步讨论不太可能产生共识时方可要求进行投票。鼓励委员公开、建设性地表达其观点，并寻求兼顾合理关切的妥协方案。

### D.8.2 Voting Rules | 投票规则

Where consensus cannot be achieved, the following voting rules shall apply:

在无法达成共识的情况下，应适用以下投票规则：

a) **Ordinary matters**: Decisions on ordinary matters (as defined in D.8.3) shall be made by a simple majority of the votes cast by Members present and entitled to vote. In the event of a tied vote, the Chair shall cast a deciding vote.
**一般事项**：关于一般事项（定义见D.8.3）的决定应由出席并有权投票的委员所投票数的简单多数作出。在票数相等的情况下，主席应投决定票。

b) **Major matters**: Decisions on major matters (as defined in D.8.3) shall require a two-thirds majority of the votes cast by Members present and entitled to vote. In the event of a tied vote on a major matter, the proposal shall be deemed not carried.
**重大事项**：关于重大事项（定义见D.8.3）的决定须由出席并有权投票的委员所投票数的三分之二多数作出。在重大事项票数相等的情况下，该提案应视为未获通过。

### D.8.3 Definition of Major Matters | 重大事项定义

The following decisions shall constitute "major matters" requiring a two-thirds majority:

以下决定应构成需要三分之二多数的"重大事项"：

a) amendments to the core specification (Chapters 0–11) of ICO Std 2002;
ICO Std 2002核心规范（第0–11章）的修订；

b) approval of new extension modules or substantive amendments to existing extension modules;
新扩展模块的批准或对现有扩展模块的实质性修订；

c) amendments to these Rules of Procedure (see Section D.12);
本议事规则的修订（见第D.12节）；

d) adoption or revision of the RMTC Code of Conduct (see Appendix D-A);
RMTC行为准则的采纳或修订（见附录D-A）；

e) adoption or revision of the Appeal Handling Procedure (see Appendix D-B);
申诉处理程序的采纳或修订（见附录D-B）；

f) decisions to revoke the accreditation of a certification body;
撤销认证机构认可的决定；

g) decisions on the interpretation of the "Three Red Lines" (see D.3.4);
关于"三条红线"（见D.3.4）解释的决定；

h) decisions regarding the establishment of formal liaison agreements with external organisations; and
关于与外部组织建立正式联络协议的决定；及

i) any other matter designated as a "major matter" by the Chair or by a two-thirds majority of the Members.
由主席或委员三分之二多数指定为"重大事项"的任何其他事项。

### D.8.4 Correspondence Voting | 通讯表决

In cases of urgency where a decision cannot be deferred until the next scheduled meeting, the Chair may initiate a correspondence vote. The procedures for correspondence voting shall be as follows:

在紧急情况下，若决定不能推迟到下次预定会议，主席可发起通讯表决。通讯表决的程序应如下：

a) The Secretary shall distribute the proposal and all relevant background materials to all Members by electronic means (email or secure voting platform);
秘书长应通过电子方式（电子邮件或安全投票平台）向所有委员分发提案和所有相关背景材料；

b) Members shall have seven (7) calendar days from the date of distribution to cast their vote;
委员应自分发之日起有七（7）个日历日的投票期限；

c) For ordinary matters, a simple majority of the votes cast shall suffice; for major matters, a two-thirds majority shall be required; and
对于一般事项，所投票数的简单多数即可；对于重大事项，须三分之二多数；且

d) The quorum requirement for correspondence voting shall be two-thirds (2/3) of all voting Members (not merely those present at a meeting).
通讯表决的法定人数要求应为所有有投票权委员的三分之二（2/3）（而非仅出席会议的委员）。

The result of a correspondence vote shall be recorded in the minutes of the next regular meeting and shall have the same force and effect as a decision made at an in-person meeting.

通讯表决的结果应记录在下次定期会议的纪要中，且与现场会议作出的决定具有同等效力。

### D.8.5 Role of the Chair in Voting | 主席的决策角色

The Chair shall not participate in ordinary votes. The Chair shall only cast a vote in the following circumstances:

主席不应参与常规投票。主席仅在以下情况下投票：

a) to break a tied vote on an ordinary matter, in which case the Chair's vote shall be the deciding vote; or
在一般事项平票时打破僵局，在此情况下主席的投票为决定票；或

b) if the Chair is also a voting Member by virtue of their Category (e.g., Category A through D), the Chair may participate in votes on major matters, but shall not cast an additional tie-breaking vote beyond their regular vote.
若主席因其类别（如A至D类）也是有投票权的委员，主席可参与重大事项的投票，但不得在常规投票之外投额外的决定票。

### D.8.6 Record of Decisions | 决议记录

Every decision of the Committee shall be recorded in writing, including:
委员会的每项决定均应以书面记录，包括：

a) the precise text of the resolution or decision;
决议或决定的精确文本；

b) the voting result (number of votes for, against, and abstentions, and the number of Members not present);
投票结果（赞成、反对和弃权的票数，以及未出席的委员人数）；

c) a summary of any dissenting opinions, including the name(s) of the dissenting Member(s) and the substance of their objection; and
任何反对意见的摘要，包括持反对意见的委员姓名及其反对的实质内容；及

d) the date on which the decision was made and the means by which (in-person meeting or correspondence vote).
作出决定的日期和方式（现场会议或通讯表决）。

This record shall form part of the meeting minutes and shall be preserved in accordance with the RMTC's records management policy.

此记录应构成会议纪要的一部分，并按RMTC的记录管理政策保存。

## D.9 Conflict of Interest | 利益冲突管理

### D.9.1 Disclosure Obligation | 申报义务

Every Member shall submit a Declaration of Interest to the Secretary:
每位委员应向秘书长提交利益申报表：

a) upon appointment, within fourteen (14) days of receiving their letter of appointment;
就任时，在收到任命函后十四（14）天内；

b) annually, no later than 31 January of each calendar year; and
每年，不迟于每个日历年的1月31日；且

c) on an ad hoc basis, whenever a new interest arises that is materially different from or additional to those previously disclosed.
临时性地，每当出现新的利益且与先前申报的利益有实质性不同或为附加利益时。

The Declaration of Interest shall use the standard form prescribed by the RMTC and shall disclose all interests that could reasonably be perceived as relevant to the Member's duties. Failure to submit a Declaration of Interest within the prescribed timeframe shall be grounds for the Chair to issue a written reminder and, if the failure persists for more than thirty (30) days, may constitute grounds for removal under D.6.7.

利益申报表应使用RMTC规定的标准格式，并应披露所有可被合理视为与委员职责相关的利益。未在规定的时间内提交利益申报表可构成主席发出书面提醒的理由，若未提交状态持续超过三十（30）天，则可构成D.6.7项下免职的理由。

### D.9.2 Recusal Rules | 回避规则

A Member who has a direct interest in a matter being considered by the Committee shall:
对委员会正在审议的事项有直接利益的委员应：

a) disclose the nature of their interest before the matter is discussed;
在事项讨论前披露其利益的性质；

b) withdraw from the discussion of the matter; and
退出该事项的讨论；且

c) withdraw from the voting on the matter.
退出该事项的投票。

The Chair shall be responsible for determining whether a Member's declared interest requires recusal. In the event that the Chair's own interest is in question, the Vice-Chair shall make the determination. If any Member disagrees with the Chair's or Vice-Chair's determination, the matter shall be referred to the Committee for resolution by a majority vote (excluding the Member whose interest is in question).

主席应负责判断委员已申报的利益是否需要回避。若涉及主席自身的利益，由副主席作出判断。若任何委员不同意主席或副主席的判断，该事项应提交委员会由多数票（排除利益相关的委员）解决。

### D.9.3 Types of Conflict of Interest | 利益冲突类型

The following categories of interest shall be disclosed and managed in accordance with D.9.1 and D.9.2. This list is not exhaustive; any interest that could reasonably be perceived as compromising a Member's impartiality shall be disclosed regardless of whether it falls within a listed category.

以下类别的利益应按D.9.1和D.9.2进行披露和管理。此列表非穷举性；任何可被合理视为损害委员公正性的利益均应予以披露，无论其是否属于所列类别。

a) **Financial interest**: Direct or indirect financial interest in a ranking entity, ranked entity, data provider, or audit body that is subject to or affected by the Committee's deliberations. This includes shareholdings, consultancy fees, and other financial arrangements.
**经济利益**：在受委员会审议影响或受其约束的排名实体、被排实体、数据提供方或审计机构中的直接或间接财务利益。包括持股、咨询费和其他财务安排。

b) **Employment or office-holding relationship**: Current or recent (within the past two years) employment, board membership, or advisory relationship with an organisation that could be affected by the Committee's decisions.
**任职关系**：在与委员会决定可能受影响的组织中的现任或近期（过去两年内）雇佣关系、董事会成员身份或顾问关系。

c) **Academic or professional competition**: An ongoing academic or professional competitive relationship with an individual or organisation that is the subject of or affected by the Committee's deliberations (e.g., competing for the same research grant, co-authoring a competing methodology).
**学术竞争**：与委员会审议事项涉及或受影响的个人或组织之间正在进行的学术或专业竞争关系（例如，竞争同一研究资助、共同编写竞争性的方法论）。

d) **Personal relationship**: A close personal or family relationship with an individual who is directly affected by the matter under consideration.
**个人关系**：与直接受审议事项影响的个人之间的密切个人或家庭关系。

e) **Recent commercial engagement**: A commercial relationship (e.g., client, vendor, partner) with an organisation affected by the Committee's deliberations that was active within the past two (2) years or is reasonably expected to become active.
**近期商业合作**：与受委员会审议影响的组织之间的商业关系（例如客户、供应商、合作伙伴），该关系在过去两（2）年内是活跃的，或有合理预期将变得活跃。

### D.9.4 Consequences of Non-disclosure or Non-recusal | 违反后果

If it is determined that a Member failed to disclose a conflict of interest or failed to recuse themselves in circumstances where recusal was required:

若确定委员未披露利益冲突或在本应回避的情况下未回避：

a) the Committee may, by a two-thirds majority vote, invalidate any decision in which the Member participated in breach of these Rules;
委员会可由三分之二多数票撤销该委员违反本规则参与的任何决定；

b) the Member may be subject to removal from office in accordance with D.6.7; and
委员可能依据D.6.7被免职；且

c) the incident shall be recorded in the annual transparency report (see D.10.1).
该事件应记录在年度透明度报告中（见D.10.1）。

### D.9.5 Whistleblowing Mechanism | 举报机制

Any stakeholder may report an undisclosed conflict of interest to the Secretary or, if the report concerns the Secretary, directly to the Chair. Reports may be made anonymously. The Committee shall investigate all reports in good faith and shall take appropriate action in accordance with D.9.4 where warranted.

任何利益相关方可向秘书长举报未披露的利益冲突，若举报涉及秘书长，则可直接向主席举报。举报可以匿名方式进行。委员会应真诚地调查所有举报，并在适当情况下依据D.9.4采取适当行动。

The Committee shall not take any retaliatory action against any person who makes a report in good faith, even if the report is ultimately found to be unsubstantiated.

委员会不得对善意举报的任何个人采取任何报复行动，即使举报最终被证实缺乏根据。

## D.10 Transparency and Reporting | 透明度与报告

### D.10.1 Annual Transparency Report | 年度透明度报告

The RMTC shall prepare and publish an Annual Transparency Report within the first quarter of each calendar year. The report shall cover the activities of the preceding calendar year and shall include, at a minimum:

RMTC应在每个日历年第一季度编制和发布年度透明度报告。报告应涵盖上一日历年活动，至少应包括：

a) **Membership roster and changes**: A complete list of current Members, including their Category, term of appointment, and professional affiliation. Any changes in membership during the year (appointments, resignations, removals) shall be noted with the date and reason.
**委员名单及变动**：当前委员的完整名单，包括其类别、任期和专业背景。年度内任何委员变动（任命、辞职、免职）应注明日期和原因。

b) **Meetings held and key decisions**: A summary of all meetings held during the year, including the mode of each meeting, the number of agenda items considered, and a summary of key decisions taken.
**召开会议及主要决议**：年度内举行所有会议的摘要，包括每次会议的形式、审议的议程项目数量以及所作关键决定的摘要。

c) **Standard amendments and new extension modules**: The status of any proposals for amendments to the standard or new extension modules considered during the year, including the outcome of the consideration (approved, rejected, deferred) and a brief rationale.
**标准修订及新增扩展模块**：年度内审议的任何标准修订或新扩展模块提案的状态，包括审议结果（批准、驳回、延期）及简要理由。

d) **Appeals received and outcomes**: The number and nature of appeals received during the year, the outcome of each appeal (upheld, dismissed, partially upheld), and any systemic issues identified through the appeal process.
**申诉受理及处理结果**：年度内收到的申诉数量和性质、每项申诉的结果（支持、驳回、部分支持），以及通过申诉程序识别的任何系统性问题。

e) **Conflict of interest disclosures and recusals**: A summary of all Declarations of Interest submitted during the year and all instances of recusal, without identifying specific cases in a manner that could compromise confidentiality.
**利益冲突申报及回避情况**：年度内提交的所有利益申报表和所有回避情况的摘要，但不得以可能损害保密性的方式识别具体案例。

f) **Work plan for the coming year**: A forward-looking statement of the RMTC's planned activities for the coming year, including the agenda for the next round of standard amendments, scheduled meetings, and any planned outreach or liaison activities.
**下一年度工作计划**：RMTC下一年度计划活动的前瞻性声明，包括下一轮标准修订的议程、预定的会议以及任何计划的外联或联络活动。

### D.10.2 Public Information | 公开信息

The following information shall be made publicly available on the ICO Std 2002 website:

以下信息应在ICO Std 2002网站上公开：

a) confirmed minutes of Committee meetings (subject to redaction per D.7.7);
已确认的委员会议纪要（须经D.7.7删节）；

b) summaries of key decisions and resolutions;
关键决定和决议的摘要；

c) the Annual Transparency Report for each year;
每年的年度透明度报告；

d) the current membership roster of the Committee;
委员会当前委员名单；

e) these Rules of Procedure, as amended from time to time; and
本议事规则（经不时修订的版本）；及

f) the RMTC Code of Conduct and Appeal Handling Procedure.
RMTC行为准则和申诉处理程序。

### D.10.3 Confidentiality Obligations | 保密义务

Notwithstanding the general principle of transparency, the following information shall be treated as confidential:

尽管有透明度的一般原则，以下信息应视为机密：

a) details of specific appeal cases under consideration, until the appeal has been resolved and the decision communicated to the parties;
正在审议的具体申诉案件的细节，直至申诉已解决且决定已通知相关方；

b) personal information of Members or third parties that is not relevant to the Committee's functions (e.g., home addresses, personal contact details, medical information);
与委员会职能无关的委员或第三方个人信息（例如家庭住址、个人联系方式、医疗信息）；

c) draft documents and working papers that have not been approved for publication; and
未经批准发布的草案文件和工作文件；及

d) any other information designated as confidential by the Committee by a two-thirds majority vote.
由委员会三分之二多数票指定为机密的任何其他信息。

Members, Observers, and the Secretary shall be bound by the confidentiality obligations set out in the Code of Conduct (see Appendix D-A).

委员、观察员和秘书长应受行为准则（见附录D-A）中规定的保密义务约束。

### D.10.4 Stakeholder Engagement | 利益相关方参与

The RMTC shall organise at least one public comment period per calendar year, during which any stakeholder may submit written comments on:

RMTC应在每个日历年组织至少一次公开征询，在此期间任何利益相关方可就以下事项提交书面意见：

a) proposed amendments to the standard;
标准的拟议修订；

b) proposed new extension modules;
拟议的新扩展模块；

c) proposed amendments to these Rules of Procedure; or
本议事规则的拟议修订；或

d) any other matter of general interest relating to the governance or application of the standard.
与标准治理或适用相关的任何其他一般利益事项。

The public comment period shall last no less than thirty (30) calendar days. The Secretary shall publish a summary of all comments received and a statement of how the comments were taken into account.

公开征询期应不少于三十（30）个日历日。秘书长应发布所有收到意见的摘要以及关于这些意见如何被纳入考量的声明。

## D.11 Relationship with ICO and Other Bodies | 与ICO及其他机构的关系

### D.11.1 Relationship with ICO | 与ICO的关系

The RMTC is an independent technical governance body established under the auspices of the International Communication Organization (ICO). The relationship between the RMTC and the ICO is characterised by the following principles:

RMTC是在国际传播组织（ICO）主持下设立的独立技术治理机构。RMTC与ICO之间的关系以下列原则为特征：

a) **Administrative support**: The ICO shall provide the RMTC with administrative, logistical, and financial support, including secretarial services, meeting facilities, website hosting, and communication infrastructure.
**行政支持**：ICO应向RMTC提供行政、后勤和财务支持，包括秘书服务、会议设施、网站托管和通信基础设施。

b) **Non-interference in technical decisions**: The ICO shall not interfere with or direct the technical decisions of the RMTC. The ICO Secretary-General's role is limited to the formal ratification of decisions made by the RMTC in accordance with these Rules.
**不干预技术决策**：ICO不得干预或指示RMTC的技术决策。ICO秘书长的角色仅限于正式批准RMTC依据本规则作出的决定。

c) **Budget provision**: The ICO shall include the RMTC's operational budget in its institutional budget, ensuring adequate resources for the Committee's activities. The RMTC shall submit an annual budget proposal to the ICO for inclusion in the institutional budget cycle.
**经费提供**：ICO应将RMTC的运营预算纳入其机构预算，确保为委员会活动提供充足的资源。RMTC应向ICO提交年度预算提案，以纳入机构预算周期。

### D.11.2 Role of ICO | ICO的角色

In its relationship with the RMTC, the ICO serves in the following capacities:

在与RMTC的关系中，ICO承担以下角色：

a) **Standard owner**: ICO is the issuing body and intellectual property rights holder of ICO Std 2002. All decisions regarding the publication and distribution of the standard are ultimately the responsibility of the ICO.
**标准所有权方**：ICO是ICO Std 2002的发布机构和知识产权持有人。关于标准发布和分发的所有决定最终由ICO负责。

b) **Final appointing authority**: ICO Secretary-General formally appoints all Members of the RMTC based on the Committee's nomination decisions.
**最终任命方**：ICO秘书长根据委员会的提名决定正式任命RMTC的所有委员。

c) **Funding provider**: ICO provides the financial resources necessary for the RMTC's operations, in accordance with the approved annual budget.
**经费提供方**：ICO按照批准的年度预算提供RMTC运作所需的财务资源。

### D.11.3 Relationship with Audit Bodies | 与审计机构的关系

The RMTC and independent audit bodies serve complementary but distinct functions within the ICO Std 2002 ecosystem:

RMTC与独立审计机构在ICO Std 2002生态系统中承担互补但不同的职能：

a) The RMTC is responsible for the development, interpretation, and maintenance of the methodological standard. It sets the rules; it does not enforce them through direct assessment.
RMTC负责方法论标准的制定、解释和维护。它制定规则，不通过直接评估来执行规则。

b) Independent audit bodies are responsible for assessing the conformance of ranking systems with the standard. They apply the rules; they do not make them.
独立审计机构负责评估排名系统与标准的一致性。它们适用规则，不制定规则。

c) The separation of standard-setting (RMTC) from assessment (audit bodies) is a fundamental governance principle designed to prevent conflicts of interest and ensure the integrity of the conformance assessment process.
标准制定（RMTC）与评估（审计机构）的分离是一项基本治理原则，旨在防止利益冲突并确保一致性评估过程的完整性。

The RMTC may, where appropriate, provide interpretive guidance to audit bodies on the application of the standard. However, the RMTC shall not direct or influence the outcome of any specific audit or conformance determination.

RMTC可在适当情况下向审计机构提供关于标准适用的解释性指引。但RMTC不得指示或影响任何具体审计或一致性判定的结果。

### D.11.4 Relationship with International Standards Bodies | 与国际组织的联络

The RMTC shall maintain active liaison with relevant international standards bodies to ensure coherence and mutual recognition. Key liaison relationships include:

RMTC应与相关国际标准组织保持积极联络，以确保协调一致和相互认可。关键联络关系包括：

a) **IREG (International Ranking Expert Group)**: The RMTC shall maintain observer-level liaison with IREG, given the foundational role of the IREG Berlin Principles in the development of ICO Std 2002. The RMTC should participate in IREG conferences and working groups where appropriate.
**IREG（国际排名专家组）**：鉴于IREG柏林原则在ICO Std 2002制定中的基础性作用，RMTC应与IREG保持观察员级别的联络。RMTC应酌情参与IREG的会议和工作组。

b) **ISO/TC 279 (Innovation Management)**: The RMTC should maintain liaison with ISO/TC 279 and other relevant ISO technical committees to ensure alignment of terminology and procedures, particularly in areas where ICO Std 2002 interfaces with ISO standards.
**ISO/TC 279（创新管理）**：RMTC应与ISO/TC 279及其他相关ISO技术委员会保持联络，以确保术语和程序的协调一致，特别是在ICO Std 2002与ISO标准交叉的领域。

c) **Other bodies**: The RMTC may establish liaison relationships with other international bodies whose work intersects with ranking methodology, including but not limited to academic ranking initiatives, consumer protection organisations, and quality assurance bodies.
**其他机构**：RMTC可与其工作与排名方法学交叉的其他国际机构建立联络关系，包括但不限于学术排名倡议、消费者保护组织和质量保证机构。

### D.11.5 Relationship with Standard Users | 与标准使用者的关系

The RMTC shall establish and maintain effective channels of communication with ranking practitioners and other users of the standard. This includes:

RMTC应建立和维护与排名从业者及标准其他使用者之间的有效沟通渠道。这包括：

a) maintaining a dedicated communication channel (e.g., email address, web portal) for receiving questions and requests for interpretive guidance from ranking entities;
维护专用的沟通渠道（例如电子邮件地址、网站门户），用于接收排名实体的问题和解释性指引请求；

b) publishing periodic FAQs, technical notes, or guidance documents addressing common questions and emerging issues;
定期发布常见问题解答、技术说明或指引文件，解决常见问题和新兴议题；

c) organising an annual stakeholder consultation event (which may be co-located with a regular RMTC meeting) to gather feedback on the standard's application and identify areas for improvement; and
组织年度利益相关方咨询活动（可与定期RMTC会议结合），收集对标准适用的反馈并识别改进领域；及

d) maintaining a relationship with ICO Std 2001 (DPP-CQ) governance bodies to ensure coherence between data quality standards and ranking methodology standards.
维护与ICO Std 2001（DPP-CQ）治理机构的关系，以确保数据质量标准与排名方法论标准之间的协调一致。

## D.12 Amendments to These Rules | 议事规则的修订

### D.12.1 Right to Propose Amendments | 修订提案权

Amendments to these Rules of Procedure may be proposed by:
本议事规则的修订可由以下方提出：

a) any three (3) or more voting Members acting jointly; or
任何三（3）名或以上有投票权的委员联合提出；或

b) the ICO Secretariat.
ICO秘书处。

Proposals for amendments shall be submitted in writing to the Secretary and shall include: (i) the text of the proposed amendment; (ii) a statement of the rationale for the amendment; and (iii) an assessment of the impact of the amendment on the RMTC's operations.

修订提案应以书面提交秘书长，并应包括：（i）拟议修订的文本；（ii）修订理由说明；及（iii）修订对RMTC运作影响的评估。

### D.12.2 Amendment Procedure | 修订程序

The procedure for amending these Rules shall be as follows:
修订本议事规则的程序应如下：

a) **Distribution**: The Secretary shall distribute the proposed amendment to all Members and Observers within seven (7) days of receipt.
**分发**：秘书长应在收到后七（7）天内将拟议修订分发给所有委员和观察员。

b) **Public comment period**: The proposed amendment shall be published for public comment for a period of no less than thirty (30) calendar days.
**公开征询期**：拟议修订应公开发表征求意见，期限不少于三十（30）个日历日。

c) **Consideration**: The RMTC shall consider the proposed amendment, together with any comments received, at its next regular meeting (or at an extraordinary meeting convened for the purpose).
**审议**：RMTC应在其下次定期会议（或为此目的召集的特别会议）上审议拟议修订及收到的任何意见。

d) **Approval**: The amendment shall require the affirmative vote of a two-thirds majority of the voting Members.
**批准**：修订须获得有投票权委员三分之二多数的赞成票。

e) **Ratification**: Following approval by the RMTC, the amendment shall be submitted to the ICO Secretary-General for ratification. The ICO Secretary-General shall ratify or return the amendment within thirty (30) days.
**批准**：经RMTC批准后，修订应提交ICO秘书长批准。ICO秘书长应在三十（30）天内批准或退回修订。

f) **Effective date**: The amendment shall take effect on the date specified in the ratification or, if no date is specified, thirty (30) days after ratification.
**生效日期**：修订应在批准中指定的日期生效，若未指定日期，则在批准后三十（30）天生效。

### D.12.3 Protection of Independence | 独立性保障

No amendment to these Rules shall have the effect of weakening, qualifying, or creating exceptions to the independence protections set out in Section D.3 (including the Three Red Lines specified in D.3.4). Any amendment purporting to have such effect shall be void ab initio.

对本议事规则的任何修订均不得产生削弱、限定或为第D.3节（包括D.3.4规定的三条红线）中的独立性保障创设例外的效果。任何意图产生此类效果的修订应自始无效。

### D.12.4 Amendment History | 修订记录

All amendments to these Rules shall be recorded in an amendment history table, which shall be published on the ICO Std 2002 website and appended to each printed or electronic version of the Rules. The amendment history shall include:

本议事规则的所有修订应记录在修订历史表中，该表应在ICO Std 2002网站上发布，并附于每份印刷或电子版本的规则之后。修订历史应包括：

a) the amendment number;
修订编号；

b) the date of approval;
批准日期；

c) a brief description of the amendment; and
修订的简要说明；及

d) the effective date.
生效日期。

## D.13 Transitional Provisions | 过渡条款

### D.13.1 Formation of the Initial Committee | 首批委员的产生

Within ninety (90) days of the publication of ICO Std 2002, the ICO Secretariat shall organise the nomination and appointment of the initial Members of the RMTC in accordance with the procedures specified in Section D.6. The ICO Secretariat shall:

在ICO Std 2002发布后九十（90）天内，ICO秘书处应按照第D.6节规定的程序组织RMTC初始委员的提名和任命。ICO秘书处应：

a) publish a call for nominations on the ICO Std 2002 website and through relevant professional and academic networks;
在ICO Std 2002网站上及通过相关专业和学术网络发布提名征集；

b) review all nominations received and compile a shortlist of candidates for each Category;
审查收到的所有提名并编制每个类别的候选人候选名单；

c) convene a nomination panel (comprising at least two ICO representatives and one independent expert) to evaluate the candidates; and
召集提名小组（由至少两名ICO代表和一名独立专家组成）评估候选人；及

d) submit the approved candidates to the ICO Secretary-General for formal appointment.
将已批准的候选人提交ICO秘书长正式任命。

### D.13.2 Inaugural Meeting | 首届会议

The inaugural meeting of the RMTC shall be convened by the ICO Secretariat within thirty (30) days of the formal appointment of the initial Members. At the inaugural meeting, the Members shall:

RMTC的首次会议应由ICO秘书处在初始委员正式任命后三十（30）天内召集。在首次会议上，委员应：

a) confirm their appointments and sign the Code of Conduct;
确认其任命并签署行为准则；

b) elect the Chair and Vice-Chair in accordance with D.5.4;
按D.5.4选举主席和副主席；

c) determine the exact number of Members within the range specified in D.5.1;
在D.5.1规定的范围内确定委员的确切人数；

d) agree on the schedule of regular meetings for the first year; and
商定第一年的定期会议时间表；及

e) consider any urgent matters carried over from the standard development process.
审议从标准制定过程中移交的任何紧急事项。

### D.13.3 Staggered Initial Terms | 首届任期交错

At the inaugural meeting, the ICO, in consultation with the elected Chair, shall designate approximately half of the initial Members to serve an initial term of two (2) years, with the remainder serving an initial term of three (3) years, in accordance with D.6.5. The designation shall be made with a view to ensuring that the Categories of membership are approximately equally represented in each cohort.

在首次会议上，ICO应与当选主席协商，按照D.6.5指定约半数的初始委员首任期为两（2）年，其余委员首任期为三（3）年。指定的目的在于确保每个批次的委员类别大致均衡分布。

### D.13.4 Interim Authority | 过渡期内RMTC的权限

During the period between the publication of ICO Std 2002 and the constitution of the RMTC (i.e., the period described in D.13.1 and D.13.2), the maintenance and interpretation of the standard shall be the responsibility of the ICO Secretariat. The ICO Secretariat may issue interpretive guidance and technical corrigenda during this interim period, but shall not approve substantive amendments to the standard. Any substantive amendments prepared during the interim period shall be held in abeyance until the RMTC is constituted and can consider them.

在ICO Std 2002发布与RMTC组建之间的期间（即D.13.1和D.13.2所述的期间），标准的维护和解释应由ICO秘书处负责。ICO秘书处可在此过渡期内发布解释性指引和技术勘误，但不得批准标准的实质性修订。在过渡期内编制的任何实质性修订应暂缓执行，直至RMTC组建并能够审议。

## Appendix D-A: RMTC Code of Conduct | 附录D-A：RMTC委员行为准则

### D-A.1 General Principles | 一般原则

All Members of the RMTC, upon appointment, shall sign and be bound by this Code of Conduct. The Code establishes the ethical and professional standards expected of Members in the performance of their duties. Breach of this Code may result in disciplinary action, including removal from office in accordance with D.6.7.

RMTC的所有委员应在就任时签署并受本行为准则约束。本准则规定了委员履行职责时应遵循的道德和专业标准。违反本准则可能导致纪律处分，包括依据D.6.7被免职。

### D-A.2 Confidentiality Obligation | 保密义务

Members shall maintain the confidentiality of all information designated as confidential by the Committee, including but not limited to: draft documents, working papers, unpublished decisions, details of appeal cases, and personal information of other Members or third parties.

委员应对委员会指定为机密的所有信息保密，包括但不限于：草案文件、工作文件、未发布的决定、申诉案件细节以及其他委员或第三方的个人信息。

The confidentiality obligation shall continue after the Member's term of office has ended. Members shall not disclose confidential information for personal benefit or for the benefit of any third party, whether during or after their tenure.

保密义务在委员任期结束后仍然有效。委员不得在任期内或任期后为个人利益或任何第三方利益披露机密信息。

Members shall take reasonable measures to protect confidential information in their possession, including secure storage of documents and electronic files, and shall not transmit confidential information through unsecured communication channels.

委员应采取合理措施保护其持有的机密信息，包括安全存储文件和电子文件，且不得通过不安全的通信渠道传输机密信息。

### D-A.3 Impartiality Commitment | 公正性承诺

Members shall perform their duties with complete impartiality and shall not allow any external pressure, personal interest, or organisational affiliation to influence their judgement or decisions.

委员应完全公正地履行职责，不得允许任何外部压力、个人利益或组织隶属关系影响其判断或决定。

Members shall base their contributions on objective evidence, professional expertise, and the requirements of the standard, not on the interests of any particular organisation, sector, or stakeholder group.

委员应基于客观证据、专业能力和标准的要求进行贡献，而非基于任何特定组织、行业或利益相关方群体的利益。

Members shall disclose any circumstance that could reasonably be perceived as affecting their impartiality, including but not limited to those specified in D.9.3, and shall comply promptly with any recusal determination made in accordance with D.9.2.

委员应披露任何可被合理视为影响其公正性的情况，包括但不限于D.9.3中规定的情况，并应迅速遵守依据D.9.2作出的任何回避决定。

### D-A.4 Diligence Obligation | 勤勉义务

Members shall devote sufficient time and attention to the performance of their duties, including: preparing for and attending meetings (aiming for 100% attendance); reviewing meeting materials in advance; contributing constructively to deliberations; and completing any assigned tasks within agreed deadlines.

委员应投入充足的时间和注意力履行职责，包括：准备并参加会议（目标为100%出席）；提前审阅会议材料；建设性地参与审议；并在商定的截止日期内完成任何分配的任务。

Members who anticipate being unable to attend a meeting shall notify the Secretary as early as possible, providing the reason for their absence. Persistent failure to attend meetings without satisfactory explanation may result in removal in accordance with D.6.7(b).

预计无法参加会议的委员应尽早通知秘书长，说明缺席原因。持续未能参加会议且无令人满意的解释，可能导致依据D.6.7(b)被免职。

### D-A.5 Disclosure Obligation | 信息披露义务

Members shall comply fully and promptly with the disclosure obligations set out in Section D.9, including submitting the Declaration of Interest upon appointment, annually, and on an ad hoc basis as required.

委员应完全、及时地遵守第D.9节规定的披露义务，包括就任时、每年以及根据需要临时提交利益申报表。

Members shall also disclose any other information that may be relevant to their ability to perform their duties, including any criminal convictions, professional disciplinary actions, or circumstances that could affect their reputation or the reputation of the RMTC.

委员还应披露与其履行职责能力相关的任何其他信息，包括任何刑事定罪、专业纪律处分或可能影响其声誉或RMTC声誉的情况。

### D-A.6 Post-Tenure Obligations | 离职后义务

After the end of their term of office, former Members shall:
任期结束后，前任委员应：

a) continue to observe the confidentiality obligation set out in D-A.2;
继续遵守D-A.2中规定的保密义务；

b) not represent themselves as current Members of the RMTC or use the RMTC affiliation to promote personal or commercial interests; and
不得以RMTC现任委员身份自居或利用RMTC的隶属关系促进个人或商业利益；及

c) cooperate with the RMTC in the orderly transfer of any documents, records, or materials in their possession relating to the Committee's work.
配合RMTC有序移交其所持有的与委员会工作相关的任何文件、记录或材料。

Former Members may be invited to serve as advisors or consultants to the RMTC on a case-by-case basis, subject to the approval of the Chair and the absence of any conflict of interest.

前任委员可逐案受邀担任RMTC的顾问或咨询员，须经主席批准且不存在任何利益冲突。

## Appendix D-B: Appeal Handling Procedure | 附录D-B：申诉处理程序

### D-B.1 Scope and Purpose | 范围和目的

This Appendix establishes the procedure for handling appeals under ICO Std 2002. Any stakeholder may file an appeal in accordance with this procedure if they believe that:

本附录确立ICO Std 2002项下申诉的处理程序。任何利益相关方若认为以下情况，均可依据本程序提起申诉：

a) a conformance determination made by an accredited certification body is incorrect or procedurally flawed;
认可认证机构作出的一致性判定不正确或存在程序缺陷；

b) a decision of the RMTC or its sub-committees is inconsistent with these Rules of Procedure or with the provisions of the standard; or
RMTC或其小组委员会的决定与本议事规则或标准条款不一致；或

c) a ranking system that claims conformance with ICO Std 2002 does not in fact meet the requirements of the standard.
声称符合ICO Std 2002的排名系统实际上未满足标准要求。

### D-B.2 Conditions for Admissibility | 申诉受理条件

An appeal shall be admissible only if all of the following conditions are satisfied:

申诉仅在满足以下全部条件时方可受理：

a) the appellant is a stakeholder as defined in D.2.8;
申诉方为D.2.8定义的利益相关方；

b) the appeal is submitted in writing within ninety (90) calendar days of the date on which the appellant became aware (or should reasonably have become aware) of the decision or circumstance being challenged;
申诉在申诉方知道（或应合理地知道）被质疑的决定或情况之日起九十（90）个日历日内以书面提交；

c) the appeal identifies the specific decision or circumstance being challenged and the grounds for the challenge;
申诉指明被质疑的具体决定或情况及质疑的理由；

d) the appeal has not been previously adjudicated by the RMTC (res judicata); and
该申诉此前未经RMTC裁决（一事不再理）；且

e) the appeal is not frivolous, vexatious, or manifestly without foundation.
申诉非无理取闹、挑衅性或明显无根据。

### D-B.3 Appeal Handling Process | 申诉处理流程

The appeal handling process shall consist of the following stages:
申诉处理流程应包括以下阶段：

**Stage 1: Submission | 第一阶段：提交**

The appellant shall submit a written appeal to the Secretary, including: (i) the appellant's name and contact information; (ii) a description of the decision or circumstance being challenged; (iii) the grounds for the appeal; (iv) any supporting evidence or documentation; and (v) the outcome sought.

申诉方应向秘书长提交书面申诉，包括：（i）申诉方的姓名和联系信息；（ii）被质疑的决定或情况的描述；（iii）申诉理由；（iv）任何支持性证据或文件；及（v）所寻求的结果。

**Stage 2: Preliminary Review | 第二阶段：初审**

The Secretary shall acknowledge receipt of the appeal within seven (7) calendar days. The Chair (or Vice-Chair, if the Chair has a conflict of interest) shall conduct a preliminary review to determine whether the appeal is admissible under D-B.2. The preliminary review shall be completed within fourteen (14) calendar days of receipt.

秘书长应在七（7）个日历日内确认收到申诉。主席（或副主席，若主席有利益冲突）应进行初步审查，确定申诉是否依据D-B.2可受理。初步审查应在收到后十四（14）个日历日内完成。

If the appeal is found to be inadmissible, the appellant shall be notified in writing with a brief statement of the reasons. The appellant may rectify any deficiencies and resubmit within thirty (30) calendar days.

若申诉被认定为不可受理，应以书面通知申诉方并简要说明理由。申诉方可在三十（30）个日历日内纠正任何缺陷并重新提交。

**Stage 3: Investigation | 第三阶段：调查**

If the appeal is admitted, the Chair shall appoint an Appeal Panel comprising three (3) Members who have no conflict of interest in the matter. The Appeal Panel shall:

若申诉被受理，主席应任命一个由三（3）名在该事项中无利益冲突的委员组成的申诉小组。申诉小组应：

a) review all documentation submitted by the appellant and by the party against whom the appeal is directed;
审查申诉方和被申诉方提交的所有文件；

b) request additional information from either party as necessary;
必要时要求任一方提供补充信息；

c) conduct interviews or hearings as deemed appropriate; and
进行其认为适当的访谈或听证；及

d) prepare a draft finding within sixty (60) calendar days of the appeal's admission.
在申诉受理后六十（60）个日历日内编制调查结论草案。

**Stage 4: Hearing | 第四阶段：听证**

If the Appeal Panel determines that a hearing is necessary, both parties shall be invited to present their case. The hearing may be conducted in person, remotely, or in writing, at the discretion of the Appeal Panel. Both parties shall have the right to:

若申诉小组认为有必要进行听证，应邀请双方陈述其案情。听证可由申诉小组裁量以现场、远程或书面方式进行。双方应有权：

a) present evidence and arguments;
出示证据和论点；

b) respond to the other party's evidence and arguments; and
回应对方的证据和论点；及

c) be accompanied by a representative or advisor.
由代表或顾问陪同。

**Stage 5: Decision | 第五阶段：裁决**

The Appeal Panel shall issue a written decision within fourteen (14) calendar days of the conclusion of the hearing (or, if no hearing was held, within fourteen (14) calendar days of the completion of the investigation). The decision shall include:

申诉小组应在听证结束后十四（14）个日历日内（或若未举行听证，在调查完成后十四（14）个日历日内）发布书面裁决。裁决应包括：

a) a summary of the facts;
事实摘要；

b) the grounds for the decision;
决定的理由；

c) the outcome (appeal upheld, dismissed, or partially upheld); and
结果（申诉支持、驳回或部分支持）；及

d) any remedial actions required.
所需的任何补救措施。

The decision of the Appeal Panel shall be reviewed and endorsed by the full Committee at its next meeting. The endorsement shall require a simple majority vote.

申诉小组的决定应由全体委员会在下次会议上审查和确认。确认需简单多数票。

**Stage 6: Notification | 第六阶段：通知**

The Secretary shall notify both parties of the Committee's decision within seven (7) calendar days of the endorsement. The notification shall include a statement of the appellant's right to request a review by the full Committee if the Appeal Panel's decision was not endorsed.

秘书长应在确认后七（7）个日历日内通知双方委员会的决定。通知应包括申诉方在申诉小组决定未获确认时有权请求全体委员会审查的声明。

### D-B.4 Time Limits | 时限要求

The following time limits shall apply to the appeal handling process. These time limits are targets and may be extended by the Chair for good cause, provided that the appellant is notified of the extension and the reasons therefor.

以下时限应适用于申诉处理流程。这些时限为目标时限，主席可因正当理由予以延长，但须通知申诉方延长期及其理由。

| Stage | Time Limit | 时限 |
|-------|-----------|------|
| Acknowledgment of receipt | 7 calendar days | 7个日历日 |
| Preliminary review | 14 calendar days | 14个日历日 |
| Investigation (draft finding) | 60 calendar days | 60个日历日 |
| Decision (after hearing) | 14 calendar days | 14个日历日 |
| Notification | 7 calendar days | 7个日历日 |
| **Total target** | **102 calendar days** | **102个日历日** |

### D-B.5 Effect of Decision | 裁决的效力

The decision of the RMTC on an appeal shall be final and binding on all parties. The decision may:

RMTC对申诉的决定应为最终决定，对各方具有约束力。决定可：

a) uphold the original decision;
维持原决定；

b) reverse the original decision;
撤销原决定；

c) modify the original decision;
修改原决定；

d) remit the matter to the original decision-maker for reconsideration with specific directions; or
将事项发回原决定者按具体指示重新审议；或

e) require specific remedial actions (e.g., correction of a conformance determination, revision of a ranking methodology, or publication of a corrigendum).
要求具体的补救措施（例如修正一致性判定、修订排名方法论或发布勘误表）。

If the appeal results in a change to the standard or to an interpretation of the standard, the change shall be published on the ICO Std 2002 website and communicated to all known stakeholders.

若申诉导致标准或标准解释的变更，该变更应在ICO Std 2002网站上发布并通知所有已知的利益相关方。
