# Annex B: ISO Standards Alignment
## 附录B ISO标准对齐分析

**ICO Std 2002 — Annex B (Informative)**

---

## Purpose | 目的

This annex documents the systematic alignment between ICO Std 2002 and relevant International Organization for Standardization (ISO) standards. For each ISO standard, it identifies the core requirements, maps them to the corresponding ICO Std 2002 provisions, and assesses the degree of consistency. It also provides a terminology cross-reference table and describes compliance mutual recognition pathways.

本附录记录ICO Std 2002与国际标准化组织（ISO）相关标准之间的系统性对齐。对于每个ISO标准，识别其核心要求，映射至ICO Std 2002的相应条款，并评估一致性程度。本附录还提供术语对照表，并描述合规互认路径。

## B.1 ISO 9001:2015 — Quality Management Systems | 质量管理体系

### B.1.1 Overview | 概览

ISO 9001:2015 specifies requirements for a quality management system (QMS) when an organisation needs to demonstrate its ability to consistently provide products and services that meet customer and applicable statutory and regulatory requirements, and aims to enhance customer satisfaction through the effective application of the system. ICO Std 2002 shares ISO 9001's foundational principles of process control, continuous improvement, and evidence-based decision-making.

ISO 9001:2015规定了质量管理体系（QMS）的要求，适用于需要证明其有能力持续提供满足顾客和适用法律法规要求的产品和服务的组织，并旨在通过体系的有效应用增强顾客满意。ICO Std 2002与ISO 9001共享过程控制、持续改进和循证决策的基础原则。

### B.1.3 Consistency Assessment | 一致性评估

ICO Std 2002 is structurally aligned with ISO 9001 through its adoption of the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) cycle:

ICO Std 2002通过采用PDCA循环，在结构上与ISO 9001对齐：

- **Plan** | **策划**: §05 (Indicator Design), §06 (Weighting Rules) — design and planning of the ranking methodology
  策划：指标设计和权重规则——排名方法论的设计和规划

- **Do** | **实施**: §07 (Data Collection), §08 (Scoring and Ranking) — execution of the ranking process
  实施：数据采集和评分排名——排名过程的执行

- **Check** | **检查**: §08.5 (Ranking Result Verification), §10 (Audit and Traceability) — verification and audit
  检查：排名结果验证和审计可追溯性——验证和审计

- **Act** | **改进**: §10.3 (Continuous Improvement), §10.4 (DPP-CQ Framework) — improvement and corrective action
  改进：持续改进和DPP-CQ框架——改进和纠正措施

**Key Difference**: ISO 9001 is a generic management system standard applicable to any organisation, while ICO Std 2002 is a domain-specific methodology standard for ranking systems. ICO Std 2002's provisions are more specific to the ranking context (e.g., indicator design, weighting rules, normalisation methods) than ISO 9001's generic quality management requirements.

**关键差异**：ISO 9001是适用于任何组织的通用管理体系标准，而ICO Std 2002是排名系统的领域特定方法论标准。ICO Std 2002的条款比ISO 9001的通用质量管理要求更具体地针对排名语境（如指标设计、权重规则、归一化方法）。

## B.2 ISO 10668:2010 — Brand Valuation | 品牌价值评价

### B.2.1 Overview | 概览

ISO 10668:2010 specifies requirements for procedures and methods of monetary brand value measurement. It provides a framework for brand valuation, including objectives, bases of valuation, approaches to valuation, methods of valuation, and sourcing of quality data and assumptions. ICO Std 2002's brand-related ranking provisions (SE-02) align with ISO 10668's valuation framework while extending it to non-monetary evaluation contexts.

ISO 10668:2010规定了品牌货币价值测量的程序和方法要求。它提供了品牌估值的框架，包括目的、估值基础、估值途径、估值方法和质量数据及假设的来源。ICO Std 2002的品牌相关排名条款（SE-02）与ISO 10668的估值框架对齐，同时将其扩展至非货币评价语境。

### B.2.3 Key Differences and Extensions | 关键差异与扩展

a) **Monetary vs. non-monetary evaluation**: ISO 10668 focuses exclusively on monetary brand valuation. ICO Std 2002 extends this to non-monetary brand evaluation (as defined by ISO 20671-1), providing a ranking framework that evaluates brand strength across multiple dimensions without requiring a single monetary value output.
   货币与非货币评价：ISO 10668仅关注品牌货币估值。ICO Std 2002将其扩展至非货币品牌评价（如ISO 20671-1所定义），提供跨多个维度评估品牌强度的排名框架，无需单一货币价值输出。

b) **Comparative ranking**: ISO 10668 evaluates individual brands. ICO Std 2002 provides a framework for comparing multiple brands in a ranking format, which requires additional provisions for normalisation, aggregation, and relative positioning.
   比较排名：ISO 10668评估单个品牌。ICO Std 2002提供以排名格式比较多个品牌的框架，需要额外的归一化、聚合和相对定位条款。

c) **Domain extension**: ISO 10668 is limited to brand valuation. ICO Std 2002's SE-02 extends the brand evaluation framework to include brand rankings, which require additional methodological considerations (e.g., indicator comparability across brands, weighting, and result presentation).
   领域扩展：ISO 10668仅限于品牌估值。ICO Std 2002的SE-02将品牌评价框架扩展至包含品牌排名，需要额外的方法论考量（如跨品牌指标可比性、权重和结果呈现）。

### B.3.3 ICO Std 2002 Extensions Beyond ISO 20671-1 | ICO Std 2002超越ISO 20671-1的扩展

a) **Ranking-specific provisions**: ISO 20671-1 evaluates individual brands. ICO Std 2002 adds ranking-specific provisions for normalisation (§8.2), aggregation (§8.3), and relative positioning (§8.4) that are not addressed in ISO 20671-1.
   排名特定条款：ISO 20671-1评估单个品牌。ICO Std 2002增加了ISO 20671-1中未涉及的归一化（§8.2）、聚合（§8.3）和相对定位（§8.4）的排名特定条款。

b) **Weighting methodology**: ISO 20671-1 does not prescribe weighting methods for brand evaluation dimensions. ICO Std 2002 provides a comprehensive weighting framework (§06) that addresses weight assignment, validation, and change control.
   权重方法论：ISO 20671-1未规定品牌评价维度的权重方法。ICO Std 2002提供全面的权重框架（§06），涵盖权重分配、验证和变更控制。

c) **Robustness and sensitivity analysis**: ISO 20671-1 does not require robustness testing. ICO Std 2002 requires bootstrap resampling, method variation tests, and data perturbation tests (§8.5.3).
   稳健性和敏感性分析：ISO 20671-1不要求稳健性测试。ICO Std 2002要求自助重抽样、方法变异测试和数据扰动测试（§8.5.3）。

## B.10 Compliance Mutual Recognition Pathways | 合规互认路径

### B.10.1 ISO 9001 Compliance | ISO 9001合规

An organisation that holds ISO 9001 certification has demonstrated a QMS that covers many of the process management and continuous improvement requirements of ICO Std 2002. However, ISO 9001 certification alone does not demonstrate compliance with ICO Std 2002, as the latter contains domain-specific requirements (indicator design, weighting, normalisation, aggregation, etc.) that are not addressed by ISO 9001.

持有ISO 9001认证的组织已证明其QMS涵盖了ICO Std 2002的许多过程管理和持续改进要求。然而，仅ISO 9001认证并不能证明符合ICO Std 2002，因为后者包含ISO 9001未涉及的领域特定要求（指标设计、权重、归一化、聚合等）。

**Recognition pathway** | **认可路径**:
- ISO 9001 certification may be accepted as evidence of compliance with the process management and continuous improvement requirements of ICO Std 2002 §10.3.
  ISO 9001认证可作为符合ICO Std 2002 §10.3过程管理和持续改进要求的证据。

### B.10.2 ISO 10668 / ISO 20671-1 Compliance | ISO 10668/ISO 20671-1合规

An organisation that conducts brand valuations in accordance with ISO 10668 or brand evaluations in accordance with ISO 20671-1 has demonstrated compliance with the valuation/evaluation methodology requirements of those standards. ICO Std 2002's SE-02 extends these requirements to the ranking context.

按照ISO 10668进行品牌估值或按照ISO 20671-1进行品牌评价的组织已证明其符合这些标准的估值/评价方法论要求。ICO Std 2002的SE-02将这些要求扩展至排名语境。

**Recognition pathway** | **认可路径**:
- ISO 10668/ISO 20671-1 conformity may be accepted as evidence of compliance with the brand evaluation methodology requirements of SE-02, provided that the ranking-specific requirements (normalisation, aggregation, relative positioning) are additionally addressed.
  ISO 10668/ISO 20671-1一致性可作为符合SE-02品牌评价方法论要求的证据，前提是额外满足排名特定要求（归一化、聚合、相对定位）。

### B.10.3 ISO/IEC 17021-1 Compliance | ISO/IEC 17021-1合规

A certification body accredited to ISO/IEC 17021-1 has demonstrated the competence, impartiality, and consistency required for management system certification. ICO Std 2002's compliance assessment (§11) may be conducted by an ISO/IEC 17021-1 accredited body.

获得ISO/IEC 17021-1认可的认证机构已证明其具备管理体系认证所需的能力、公正性和一致性。ICO Std 2002的合规评估（§11）可由ISO/IEC 17021-1认可的机构进行。

**Recognition pathway** | **认可路径**:
- An ISO/IEC 17021-1 accredited certification body may conduct ICO Std 2002 compliance assessments, provided that the auditors have additional competence in ranking methodology.
  ISO/IEC 17021-1认可的认证机构可进行ICO Std 2002合规评估，前提是审核员具备排名方法论方面的额外能力。

### B.10.4 ISO 14064 Compliance | ISO 14064合规

An organisation that reports GHG emissions in accordance with ISO 14064-1 and has its GHG statement verified in accordance with ISO 14064-3 has demonstrated compliance with the environmental data requirements of ICO Std 2002's SE-09.

按照ISO 14064-1报告GHG排放并按照ISO 14064-3验证其GHG声明的组织已证明其符合ICO Std 2002 SE-09的环境数据要求。

**Recognition pathway** | **认可路径**:
- ISO 14064-1/14064-3 conformity may be accepted as evidence of compliance with the environmental data requirements of SE-09, provided that the ranking-specific requirements (indicator integration, weighting, normalisation) are additionally addressed.
  ISO 14064-1/14064-3一致性可作为符合SE-09环境数据要求的证据，前提是额外满足排名特定要求（指标整合、权重、归一化）。
