# SE-12 : Tea

**ICO Std 2002 — Extension SE-12**

---

This extension module specifies the requirements and methodologies for ranking and evaluating tea and tea-related products. Tea rankings present distinctive methodological challenges: the vast diversity of tea types (six primary categories plus re-processed teas and herbal infusions) requires highly differentiated indicator systems; terroir and cultivar identity are paramount yet difficult to verify objectively; the integration of centuries-old artisanal craftsmanship with modern quality assurance demands balanced evaluation frameworks; and the critical importance of pesticide residue and food safety compliance in tea necessitates rigorous testing protocols. This module extends the core-layer methodology (Chapters 4 through 10) by defining domain-specific indicators, data collection protocols, safety assurance mechanisms, and scoring procedures for tea evaluation. It is applicable to rankings across all categories of tea, from primary tea types through re-processed teas to herbal infusions.

---

## SE-12.1 Scope and Applicable Objects

### SE-12.1.1 Scope

This module applies to the design, execution, and publication of rankings that evaluate tea and tea-related products. It covers:

a) the six primary tea categories, classified by processing method and oxidation level:

1) **Green tea (绿茶)**: unoxidised teas produced by heat treatment (kill-green/杀青) to inactivate polyphenol oxidase. Sub-categories by kill-green method:
   - pan-fired green tea (炒青绿茶): including Longjing/龙井 (ref: GB/T 18650), Biluochun/碧螺春, Xinyang Maojian/信阳毛尖, Liu'an Guapian/六安瓜片, Huangshan Maofeng/黄山毛峰, Taiping Houkui/太平猴魁, Anji Baicha/安吉白茶;
   - baked green tea (烘青绿茶);
   - sun-dried green tea (晒青绿茶);
   - steamed green tea (蒸青绿茶): including Enshi Yulu/恩施玉露 (ref: GB/T 14456.6);

2) **Black tea (红茶)**: fully oxidised teas (ref: GB/T 13738 series):
   - small-leaf black tea / souchong (小种红茶): including Zhengshan Xiaozhong/正山小种 (ref: GB/T 13738.3);
   - congou black tea (工夫红茶): including Qimen/祁门红茶, Dian Hong/滇红, Chuan Hong/川红, Yi Hong/宜红, Min Hong/闽红; Jin Jun Mei/金骏眉 as an independent variety (ref: GB/T 13738.2);
   - broken black tea (红碎茶) (ref: GB/T 13738.1);

3) **Oolong tea (乌龙茶/青茶)**: partially oxidised teas (ref: GB/T 30357 series):
   - Northern Fujian oolong (闽北乌龙): including Da Hong Pao/大红袍 (ref: GB/T 30357.8), Shui Xian/水仙 (ref: GB/T 30357.4), Rou Gui/肉桂 (ref: GB/T 30357.5);
   - Southern Fujian oolong (闽南乌龙): including Tieguanyin/铁观音 (ref: GB/T 30357.2), Huangjin Gui/黄金桂 (ref: GB/T 30357.3);
   - Guangdong oolong (广东乌龙): including Phoenix Dancong/凤凰单丛 (ref: GB/T 30357.6);
   - Taiwan oolong (台湾乌龙): including Dongding/冻顶乌龙, Dongfang Meiren/东方美人, Wenshan Baozhong/文山包种;

4) **White tea (白茶)**: minimally processed teas produced by natural withering and drying (ref: GB/T 22291):
   - Baihao Yinzhen/白毫银针 (Silver Needle);
   - Baimudan/白牡丹 (White Peony);
   - Gongmei/贡眉 (Tribute Eyebrow);
   - Shoumei/寿眉 (Longevity Eyebrow);
   - Key production areas: Fuding/福鼎, Zhenghe/政和, Jianyang/建阳, Songxi/松溪;

5) **Yellow tea (黄茶)**: lightly oxidised teas with a characteristic men huang/闷黄 (sealed yellowing) process (ref: GB/T 21726):
   - yellow bud tea (黄芽茶): including Junshan Yinzhen/君山银针, Mengding Huangya/蒙顶黄芽, Huoshan Huangya/霍山黄芽;
   - yellow small-leaf tea (黄小茶);
   - yellow large-leaf tea (黄大茶);

6) **Dark tea (黑茶)**: post-fermented teas:
   - Pu-erh tea (普洱茶): raw Pu-erh (生普) and ripe Pu-erh (熟普) (ref: GB/T 22111);
   - Anhua dark tea (安化黑茶): including Fu brick/茯砖 (ref: GB/T 32719.5), Hei brick/黑砖, Hua brick/花砖, Qianliang tea/千两茶 (ref: GB/T 32719.2);
   - Liu Bao tea (六堡茶) (ref: GB/T 32719.4);
   - Ya'an Tibetan tea (雅安藏茶);
   - Jingyang Fu tea (泾阳茯茶);
   - Hubei Qing brick (湖北青砖);

b) re-processed tea categories:

1) **Scented tea (花茶)**: tea base blended or layered with flowers (ref: GB/T 22292 for jasmine tea; GB/T 34779 for scented tea processing):
   - jasmine tea (茉莉花茶), rose tea (玫瑰花茶), osmanthus tea (桂花茶);

2) **Compressed tea (紧压茶)**: tea pressed into cakes, bricks, tuo shapes, or other forms (ref: GB/T 9833 series):
   - tuo tea (沱茶), cake tea (饼茶), brick tea (砖茶);

3) **Extract tea (萃取茶)**: instant tea, tea concentrates, and liquid tea extracts (ref: ISO 7516);

4) **Blended/compound tea (调制茶)**: teas blended with other ingredients (ref: GH/T 1247);

5) **Tea bags (袋泡茶)**: tea packaged in filter material sachets;

6) **Artistic/craft tea (工艺茶)**: decorative tea products formed into artistic shapes;

c) herbal infusions (代用茶): non-Camellia sinensis plant materials used for infusion, including:
   - flower-based (花类): chrysanthemum (菊花, including Hangbaiju/杭白菊, Boju/亳菊, Chuju/滁菊, wild chrysanthemum/野菊花), honeysuckle (金银花), rose (玫瑰花), osmanthus (桂花), sophora flower (槐花);
   - fruit-based (果类): goji berry (枸杞, ref: GB/T 18672), monk fruit (罗汉果, ref: GB/T 20357), hawthorn (山楂);
   - leaf/herb-based (叶/草类): lotus leaf (荷叶), tartary buckwheat (苦荞, ref: GB/T 10458), buckwheat (荞麦), cassia seed (决明子), peppermint (薄荷), perilla (紫苏);
   - root/rhizome-based (根茎类): licorice (甘草, ref: GB/T 19618), kudzu root (葛根);
   - other: mulberry leaf (桑叶), eucommia (杜仲), corn silk (玉米须).

### SE-12.1.2 Applicable Objects

This module shall apply to rankings of:

a) individual tea products within a defined category or appellation;

b) tea product lines from a single producer, garden, or factory;

c) tea brands evaluated on aggregate quality metrics, including origin and craftsmanship indicators.

### SE-12.1.3 Exclusions

This module does not apply to:

a) tea-flavoured beverages, ready-to-drink (RTD) tea products, which are covered under SE-01 (Consumer Products);

b) tea-derived pharmaceutical or nutraceutical products subject to separate regulatory frameworks;

c) tea supplements in capsule or tablet form not intended as traditional tea infusion.

## SE-12.3 Indicator System

### SE-12.3.1 Indicator Hierarchy

The tea indicator system shall be organised into the following three-level hierarchy:

a) **Level 1 — Dimensions**: Sensory Quality, Physicochemical Indicators, Origin and Cultivar, Craftsmanship and Grade, Safety and Compliance, Sustainability;

b) **Level 2 — Sub-dimensions**: Decompositions of each dimension as specified in SE-12.3.2 through SE-12.3.7;

c) **Level 3 — Specific measures**: Operationalised variables for which data is collected, as specified in each sub-dimension.

### SE-12.3.2 Sensory Quality (Weight: 0.30)

Sensory quality is the primary dimension for tea evaluation. The ranking entity shall conduct sensory evaluation in accordance with GB/T 23776, using the standardised scoring methodology. Evaluation conditions shall conform to GB/T 23776 requirements: tea-to-water ratio 1:50 (mass ratio), standard white porcelain evaluation cup and bowl set, controlled water temperature and infusion time per tea type.

a) **Dry Tea Appearance (干茶外形)**: Visual assessment of the uninfused tea:

1) **shape and twist (条索/外形)**: tightness, uniformity, and characteristic shape for the declared variety. Sensory focus varies by category:
   - for pan-fired green teas: flatness and smoothness (龙井) or tight curl (碧螺春);
   - for baked green teas: bud-tip prominence and strip integrity (毛峰类);
   - for black teas: twist tightness, tip (golden bud) coverage, and colour (e.g., ebony-black with golden tips for premium congou);
   - for oolong teas: the characteristic twisted-strip or half-ball shape, with particular attention to the "green frog belly" (青腹) quality for Dancong and the sandy-green colour for Tieguanyin;
   - for white teas: bud-to-leaf ratio, silver-white pectin down coverage (银针), or natural leaf form (牡丹/寿眉);
   - for yellow teas: needle form integrity (银针) or natural leaf shape;
   - for dark teas: cake/brick uniformity, surface integrity, visible "golden flowers" (金花/Eurotium cristatum) for Fu-type teas;

2) **colour (色泽)**: hue and lustre appropriate to the tea type, with sub-category differentiation:
   - green teas: silvery-green (炒青), deep green (烘青), or dark green with gloss (steamed);
   - black teas: wu-black lustre (乌黑油润), golden tip coverage;
   - oolong: sandy-green (砂绿) for Tieguanyin, oiled jade-green (油绿) for Dancong, dark brown with oyster-green (青褐) for rock tea;
   - white teas: silvery-white (银白灰绿);
   - yellow teas: tender yellow (嫩黄);
   - dark teas: dark brown (黑褐), oiled lustre for aged samples;

3) **integrity (整碎)**: proportion of whole, unbroken leaves versus fragments;

4) **cleanliness (净度)**: absence of stems, fibre, dust, or foreign matter.

b) **Liquor Colour (汤色)**: Visual assessment of the brewed tea liquor:

1) hue appropriate to the declared category, with differentiation:
   - green tea: bright yellowish-green (嫩绿明亮) or apricot-green (杏绿);
   - black tea: bright red (红艳), red-gold (红金), or orange-red (橙红);
   - oolong: golden-amber (金黄) for Tieguanyin, orange-yellow (橙黄) for Dancong, orange-red (橙红) for rock tea;
   - white tea: pale apricot (浅杏黄) for new tea, amber-orange (橙黄) for aged white tea;
   - yellow tea: apricot-yellow (杏黄明亮);
   - dark tea: red-brown (红浓) for ripe Pu-erh, orange-red (橙红) for raw Pu-erh, red-concentrated (红浓明亮) for Liu Bao;

2) clarity and brilliance (清澈度/明亮度);

3) absence of cloudiness or sediment.

c) **Aroma (香气)**: Olfactory assessment of dry leaf, wet leaf, and liquor:

1) **aroma type**: conformity to the characteristic aroma, with category-specific emphasis:
   - green teas: chestnut aroma (栗香), orchid fragrance (兰花香) for Longjing; fresh green (清香) for Biluochun;
   - black teas:蜜糖香 (honey-sweet) for Qimen, fruity-floral for Dian Hong; pine smoke aroma (松烟香) for Zhengshan Xiaozhong;
   - oolong: osmanthus fragrance (桂花香) and "rock rhyme" (岩韵) for Da Hong Pao; "orchid rhyme" (兰韵) for Tieguanyin; natural floral complex for Dancong;
   - white tea: delicate fresh downy aroma (毫香蜜韵) for Silver Needle, honey-sweet for aged white tea;
   - yellow tea: fresh sweet (清鲜甜爽);
   - dark tea: aged fragrance (陈香) for aged Pu-erh, betel nut aroma (槟榔香) for Liu Bao, "golden flower" fungal aroma (菌花香) for Fu brick;

2) **purity**: absence of off-odours (smoky, musty, stale, fire-scorched, or foreign odours);

3) **persistence**: the duration of aroma across multiple infusions (particularly critical for oolong and rock teas).

d) **Taste and Mouthfeel (滋味)**: Gustatory assessment:

1) **intensity (浓度)**: strength and depth of flavour;

2) **fullness (厚度)**: perceived body and richness on the palate;

3) **hui gan (回甘)**: the quality and persistence of sweet aftertaste;

4) **freshness (鲜爽度)**: particularly important for green and white teas;

5) **astringency and bitterness transformation (化苦/化涩)**: whether harsh notes transform positively;

6) **category-specific taste characteristics**:
   - for green teas: fresh brisk (鲜爽甘醇), absence of grassy rawness;
   - for black teas: sweet-mellow (甜醇) or rich-mellow (浓醇), with "Qimen fragrance" for Qimen;
   - for oolong: the "rhyme" (韵) quality — rock rhyme (岩韵) for Northern Fujian, guanyin rhyme (观音韵) for Tieguanyin, mountain rhyme (山韵) for Dancong;
   - for white teas: sweet-mellow (甜醇) for new, honey-sweet thick (蜜甜醇厚) for aged;
   - for dark teas: thick-mellow (浓醇) for ripe Pu-erh, aged-mellow (陈醇) for aged;

e) **Wet Leaf (叶底)**: Assessment of spent leaves:

1) **tenderness (嫩度)**: proportion of buds and young leaves;

2) **colour uniformity (色泽匀度)**: consistency indicating uniform processing;

3) **integrity and flexibility**: whole, unbroken leaves with appropriate elasticity;

4) **category-specific wet leaf characteristics**:
   - green tea: bright yellowish-green (嫩绿明亮) leaf base;
   - black tea: coppery-red (铜红) uniform colour for well-fermented;
   - oolong: "green leaf with red edge" (绿叶红镶边) for traditional style;
   - white tea: greyish-green (灰绿) for Silver Needle, dark green mottled for寿眉;
   - dark tea: dark brown (褐) for ripe Pu-erh, with flexibility;

### SE-12.3.3 Physicochemical Indicators (Weight: 0.20)

Physicochemical indicators provide objective, measurable parameters that complement sensory evaluation.

a) **General Quality Parameters** (applicable to all tea types):

1) **moisture content (水分)**: shall comply with the limits specified in the applicable product standard. Typical limits: ≤7.0% for green tea (GB/T 14456), ≤6.5% for yellow tea bud type, ≤12.0% for dark tea loose-leaf, ≤14.0% for compressed dark tea;

2) **total ash (总灰分)**: indicative of mineral content and potential adulteration. Typical limits: ≤7.0% for most tea types, ≤8.0% for dark tea;

3) **water extract (水浸出物)**: the soluble solids yield, indicating brew strength, measured per GB/T 8305. Minimum limits vary: ≥34.0% for green tea, ≥32.0% for yellow tea, ≥28.0% for Pu-erh (GB/T 22111);

4) **crude fibre (粗纤维)**: indicative of leaf maturity and processing quality;

5) **powder content (粉末)**: per GB/T 8311, relevant for grading.

b) **Bioactive Compound Analysis**:

1) **tea polyphenols (茶多酚)**: total polyphenol content, measured per GB/T 8313. Particularly important for green tea (≥11.0% per GB/T 14456.1); Pu-erh ripe tea limits ≤15.0% due to fermentation degradation;

2) **catechins (儿茶素)**: individual catechin composition (EGCG, EGC, ECG, EC) measured per GB/T 8313, providing quality differentiation for green and white teas;

3) **caffeine (咖啡碱)**: caffeine content for quality characterisation and consumer information;

4) **L-theanine (茶氨酸)**: theanine content, a key contributor to umami taste, particularly important for shaded teas and high-grade green/white teas;

5) **theaflavins (茶黄素) and thearubigins (茶红素)**: for black tea, the ratio of theaflavins to thearubigins is a critical quality indicator; for dark tea, changes in these compounds during post-fermentation provide maturity markers;

6) **category-specific markers**:
   - for Fu brick tea: "golden flower" (Eurotium cristatum) density assessment;
   - for aged teas: volatile compound profile changes as age indicators.

c) **Pesticide Residue Limits**: The ranking entity shall verify compliance with:

1) GB 2763 (national maximum residue limits);

2) EU Regulation 396/2005 (EU MRLs), for products marketed in or exported to the European Union;

3) Japan Positive List System, for products exported to Japan;

4) where multiple standards apply, the most stringent standard shall be the benchmark.

d) **Heavy Metal Limits**: Compliance with GB 2762:

1) lead (Pb): ≤5.0 mg/kg for tea leaves;

2) arsenic (As): compliance with applicable limits;

3) cadmium (Cd), mercury (Hg) as applicable;

4) for brick tea: fluoride (F) per GB 19965.

### SE-12.3.4 Origin and Cultivar (Weight: 0.20)

This dimension evaluates the geographic origin, terroir, and cultivar identity of the tea.

a) **Geographic Indication Status**: The tea's geographic origin protection:

1) national GI registration (地理标志产品) status. Key GI-designated teas and their protection scope include:
   - West Lake Longjing (西湖龙井): Xihu scenic area per GB/T 18650, with sub-demarcation of West Lake/Qiantang/Yuezhou production areas;
   - Anxi Tieguanyin (安溪铁观音): Anxi County per GB/T 19598;
   - Pu'er tea (普洱茶): designated production areas in Yunnan per GB/T 22111;
   - Qimen black tea (祁门红茶): Qimen County and surrounding areas;
   - Wuyi rock tea (武夷岩茶): Wuyishan National Park and surrounding areas;
   - Junshan Silver Needle (君山银针): Junshan Island, Dongting Lake, Yueyang;

2) recognised sub-appellations (小产区) within broader GI regions (e.g., Niu Lan Keng/牛栏坑, Ma Feng/马头岩 within Wuyi Rock Tea; Shi/狮, Long/龙, Yun/云, Hu/虎, Mei/梅 within West Lake Longjing).

b) **Altitude and Microclimate**: Environmental factors:

1) cultivation altitude: higher altitudes generally associated with enhanced amino acid content and more complex aromas (e.g., Gaoshan tea/高山茶 in Taiwan, Wuyi mountain terrain);

2) microclimatic conditions: temperature variation, rainfall, fog frequency;

3) seasonal characteristics: pre-Qingming (明前), pre-Guyu (雨前), summer, autumn harvests.

c) **Soil Characteristics**:

1) soil type: weathered rock/mineral-rich soil for Wuyi rock tea (contributing to "rock rhyme/岩韵"), red laterite soil for Yunnan Pu-erh, loess soil for certain green tea areas;

2) mineral composition and its documented influence on tea character;

3) soil management and sustainability practices.

d) **Cultivar Purity and Suitability**:

1) declared cultivar verification. Key variety-specific standards:
   - Longjing: Longjing Qunti/龙井群体, Longjing 43, Longjing Changye/龙井长叶, Yingshuang/迎霜, Jiukeng/鸠坑种 per GB/T 18650;
   - Tieguanyin: Tieguanyin original variety/铁观音原生种 (红心歪尾桃) per GB/T 30357.2;
   - Da Hong Pao: Qidan/奇丹, Beidou/北斗, and mother-tree derived cultivars per GB/T 30357.8;

2) molecular (DNA) identification where necessary for cultivar authentication;

3) cultivar suitability for the declared tea type and production area.

### SE-12.3.5 Craftsmanship and Grade (Weight: 0.15)

This dimension evaluates the production process and grade classification accuracy.

a) **Processing Methodology Compliance**: Critical processing steps by category:

1) **green tea**: kill-green (杀青) method differentiation is fundamental:
   - pan-fired (炒青): temperature control (锅温 200-300°C) and duration determine chestnut/crisp aroma; over-firing produces smoky off-notes;
   - baked (烘青): lower temperature, produces cleaner, more delicate aroma;
   - sun-dried (晒青): produces characteristic sun-dried flavour, used as raw material for Pu-erh;
   - steamed (蒸青): preserves deep green colour and fresh marine-like aroma (per GB/T 14456.6);
   - subsequent steps: rolling (揉捻), shaping (整形), drying (干燥) — each affecting final quality;

2) **black tea**: withering (萎凋), rolling/CTC, controlled oxidation (发酵) — temperature, humidity, duration; drying;

3) **oolong tea**: sun-withering (晒青), indoor withering and shaking (做青/摇青) — the most technically demanding step controlling partial oxidation; kill-green; rolling; roasting (炭焙) for rock teas — degree of roast from light (清香型) to heavy (浓香型/传统型);

4) **white tea**: natural withering (萎凋) under controlled conditions — duration and method (indoor/outdoor/solar) critically affect quality; low-temperature drying;

5) **yellow tea**: the men huang/闷黄 (sealed yellowing) process — the defining step, requiring precise temperature and humidity control to produce the characteristic yellow colour and sweet taste;

6) **dark tea**: pile fermentation (渥堆) for ripe Pu-erh — microbial ecology management; "golden flower" cultivation (发花) for Fu brick — temperature, moisture, and timing control for Eurotium cristatum growth; aging/陈化 management for aged teas.

b) **Grade Classification Accuracy**:

1) grade assignment shall be based on sensory and physicochemical criteria defined in the applicable national standard (e.g., GB/T 14456 for green tea: 特级, 一级 through 五级; GB/T 22291 for white tea: Silver Needle/White Peony/Gongmei grades);

2) the ranking entity shall independently verify grade through its own sensory evaluation;

3) discrepancies between declared grade and independently assessed grade shall be documented and scored.

c) **Batch Consistency**: Quality consistency across production batches:

1) inter-batch sensory consistency;

2) inter-batch physicochemical parameter variation;

3) quality control system documentation.

### SE-12.3.6 Safety and Compliance (Weight: 0.10)

a) **Pesticide Residue International Compliance**: Beyond GB 2763 compliance:

1) compliance with EU Regulation 396/2005;

2) compliance with Japan's Positive List System;

3) compliance with Codex Alimentarius MRL standards;

4) the most stringent applicable standard shall be the benchmark.

b) **Organic Certification Authenticity**: For products marketed as organic:

1) verification of valid certification (GB/T 19630 for China, EU Organic per Regulation (EU) 2018/848, USDA NOP);

2) residue testing consistent with organic status;

3) annual surveillance audit results.

c) **Food Safety Management Systems**:

1) HACCP certification or equivalent;

2) ISO 22000 certification;

3) GMP compliance documentation.

d) **Labelling Compliance**: GB 7718 and tea-specific requirements:

1) accurate product category designation per GB/T 30766;

2) origin and cultivar declaration accuracy;

3) grade marking accuracy;

4) production date, shelf life, and storage instructions per GB/T 30375.

### SE-12.3.7 Sustainability (Weight: 0.05)

a) **Tea Garden Ecological Management**: biodiversity management, IPM adoption, soil conservation, water efficiency.

b) **Carbon Footprint**: GHG emissions per unit of tea, renewable energy adoption, processing energy efficiency.

c) **Fair Trade and Community Impact**: fair trade certification, worker welfare, smallholder support, community development.

---

## SE-12.4 Data Collection

### SE-12.4.1 Data Source Categories

The ranking entity shall collect data from:

a) **Sensory Panel Data**: per GB/T 23776;

b) **Laboratory Testing Data**: from ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratories;

c) **Garden and Factory Audit Data**: on-site audit records;

d) **Certification and Regulatory Records**: organic certification, GI registration, food safety system certifications;

e) **Market and Trade Data**: market price data, auction records, brand reputation surveys.

### SE-12.4.2 Sensory Panel Requirements

a) The ranking entity shall convene a panel of no fewer than five trained assessors per tea category, holding recognised qualifications (e.g., national-level tea evaluator certification 国家级茶叶审评师).

b) Evaluations shall be conducted under standardised conditions: standardised brewing parameters per GB/T 23776, standard white porcelain cup and bowl set (审评杯碗), controlled environment.

c) For each tea type, the panel shall apply the specific scoring criteria defined in the applicable national standard.

### SE-12.4.3 Category-Specific Data Requirements

a) For each tea category, the ranking entity shall define category-specific data requirements:

1) **Green tea**: harvest date classification (明前/雨前/谷雨), kill-green method, cultivar, production area (GI designation);

2) **Black tea**: cultivar, harvest season, oxidation level, CTC vs. orthodox, origin (e.g., Qimen, Yunnan for Dian Hong);

3) **Oolong tea**: cultivar, harvest season, oxidation level, roasting degree, origin sub-appellation (e.g., Zhengyan/正岩, Banyan/半岩, Zhoucha/洲茶 for Wuyi);

4) **White tea**: harvest season, cultivar, production area (Fuding vs. Zhenghe), aging duration for aged white tea;

5) **Yellow tea**: men huang process parameters, origin;

6) **Dark tea**: raw material source, pile fermentation parameters, aging duration, production area, "golden flower" presence and density for Fu-type;

7) **Scented tea**: flower type, quality, number of scenting cycles (窨次), tea base grade;

8) **Compressed tea**: raw material grade, compression method, form (tuo/cake/brick), aging potential;

9) **Herbal infusions**: botanical species verification, origin, active compound profile.

---

## SE-12.5 Anti-Counterfeiting and Authentication

### SE-12.5.1 General Requirements

The ranking entity shall implement anti-counterfeiting mechanisms. Origin fraud, cultivar substitution, and grade inflation are prevalent in the tea market; authentication measures are mandatory.

### SE-12.5.2 Origin Authentication

a) The ranking entity shall verify geographic origin through:

1) GI registration verification;

2) supply chain traceability from garden to finished product;

3) multi-element stable isotope or chemical fingerprint analysis where applicable;

4) satellite imagery and garden area verification for volume consistency.

b) Products with false origin claims shall be excluded from the ranking.

### SE-12.5.3 Cultivar and Harvest Date Verification

a) For cultivar-specific claims (e.g., Tieguanyin, Longjing 43, Da Hong Pao): morphological assessment and DNA barcoding where necessary.

b) For harvest date claims (e.g., 明前茶): cross-verification against regional harvest calendars; amino acid to polyphenol ratio as indirect evidence.

### SE-12.5.4 Aged Tea Authentication

a) For aged teas (aged Pu-erh, aged white tea): production date records, sensory indicators of natural aging, chemical age markers, storage condition documentation.

b) Products with unverifiable age claims shall be flagged and scored conservatively.

---

## SE-12.6 Scoring Methodology

### SE-12.6.1 Scoring Scale

a) Each Level 3 indicator shall be scored on a normalised scale from 0 to 100.

b) Quantitative indicators: min-max or z-score method per Chapter 8 of the core layer.

c) Qualitative indicators: defined ordinal scales with documented anchor descriptions.

d) Sensory indicators: 100-point scoring sheet per GB/T 23776, with category-specific weight allocations.

### SE-12.6.2 Weighting

| Dimension | Default Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Sensory Quality | 0.30 |
| Physicochemical Indicators | 0.20 |
| Origin and Cultivar | 0.20 |
| Craftsmanship and Grade | 0.15 |
| Safety and Compliance | 0.10 |
| Sustainability | 0.05 |

a) For herbal infusions (代用茶), adjusted weights shall apply per SE-12.X.

b) Products lacking food safety certification or non-compliant with GB 2762 or GB 2763 shall receive Safety and Compliance score of zero and shall be excluded.

### SE-12.6.3 Aggregation

a) Level 3 to Level 2, and Level 2 to Level 1: weighted sum method per Chapter 8.

b) Overall product score: weighted sum of Level 1 dimension scores.

c) Dimension-level scores shall be reported alongside overall scores.

## SE-12.7 Alignment with International Standards

### SE-12.7.1 ISO Tea Standards Alignment

The Physicochemical Indicators dimension shall be consistent with ISO 3720:2011 (Black tea), ISO 11287:2011 (Green tea), and ISO 7516:2011 (Instant tea). Test methods shall be consistent with referenced ISO methods or documented equivalence shall be provided.

### SE-12.7.2 Food Safety Alignment

The Safety and Compliance dimension shall be consistent with Codex Alimentarius standards, HACCP principles, and ISO 22000:2018.

---

## SE-12.8 Publication and Transparency

### SE-12.8.1 Publication Requirements

Tea ranking publications shall include:

a) tea category definitions and inclusion criteria;

b) declared origin, cultivar, and harvest season for each ranked product;

c) authentication verification results summary;

d) sensory panel composition and methodology;

e) category-specific scoring adjustments;

f) pesticide residue testing results summary.

### SE-12.8.2 Product Identification

Each ranked product shall be uniquely identified by:

1) product name and declared grade;

2) producer or brand name;

3) declared production area (to the most specific appellation);

4) declared cultivar;

5) declared harvest season and year;

6) declared processing method.

## SE-12.X Sub-Module: Herbal Infusions (代用茶)

### SE-12.X.1 Scope and Definition

This sub-module applies to herbal infusions (代用茶): plant-based materials not derived from Camellia sinensis, intended for infusion or decoction. The fundamental distinction from true tea is the absence of tea-specific compounds (polyphenols, caffeine, theanine).

a) Category coverage:

1) **flower-based (花类)**: chrysanthemum (菊花 — Hangbaiju/杭白菊 ref: GB/T 18862, Boju/亳菊, Chuju/滁菊, Gongju/贡菊, wild chrysanthemum/野菊花), honeysuckle (金银花), rose (玫瑰花), osmanthus (桂花), sophora flower (槐花);

2) **fruit-based (果类)**: goji berry (枸杞 ref: GB/T 18672), monk fruit (罗汉果 ref: GB/T 20357), hawthorn (山楂);

3) **leaf/herb-based (叶/草类)**: lotus leaf (荷叶), tartary buckwheat (苦荞 ref: GB/T 10458), buckwheat (荞麦), cassia seed (决明子), peppermint (薄荷), perilla (紫苏);

4) **root/rhizome-based (根茎类)**: licorice (甘草 ref: GB/T 19618), kudzu root (葛根);

5) **other**: mulberry leaf (桑叶), eucommia (杜仲), corn silk (玉米须).

b) Products combining herbal infusion materials with true tea shall be evaluated under re-processed tea provisions rather than this sub-module.

### SE-12.X.2 Sensory Evaluation Methodology for Herbal Infusions

a) Sensory evaluation differs from true tea:

1) **dry material appearance**: colour uniformity, integrity, cleanliness, species-characteristic appearance;

2) **infusion colour**: colour appropriate to botanical material;

3) **aroma**: characteristic botanical aroma, purity, absence of off-odours (sulphur residue, mould);

4) **taste**: characteristic flavour, sweetness, bitterness specific to species;

5) **spent material**: species verification and quality indicators.

b) Traditional tea scoring criteria (GB/T 23776) do not apply directly. Category-specific sensory scoring sheets shall be developed.

### SE-12.X.3 Physicochemical Parameters for Herbal Infusions

a) The indicator system replaces tea-specific parameters with botanical-active compounds:

1) **moisture content**: shall comply with applicable limits for dried botanicals;

2) **total ash**: cleanliness and adulteration indicator;

3) **category-specific active compounds**:

| Botanical | Key Markers | Applicable Standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chrysanthemum (菊花) | total flavonoids (总黄酮), chlorogenic acid (绿原酸) | GB/T 18862 |
| Goji berry (枸杞) | polysaccharides (多糖), betaine (甜菜碱), zeaxanthin (玉米黄质) | GB/T 18672 |
| Monk fruit (罗汉果) | mogrosides (罗汉果苷), particularly mogroside V | GB/T 20357 |
| Honeysuckle (金银花) | chlorogenic acid (绿原酸), luteoloside (木犀草苷) | — |
| Tartary buckwheat (苦荞) | rutin (芦丁), total flavonoids | GB/T 10458 |
| Cassia seed (决明子) | anthraquinone derivatives (蒽醌类) | — |

b) The ranking entity shall identify and measure primary bioactive markers using validated analytical methods.

### SE-12.X.4 Safety Evaluation for Herbal Infusions

a) Specific hazards for herbal infusions:

1) **sulphur dioxide (SO₂)**: many herbal infusions are susceptible to sulphur fumigation. The ranking entity shall measure SO₂ residues and ensure compliance with GB 2760 limits. Excessive residuals shall result in significant safety score deduction;

2) **microbiological limits**: total plate count, coliforms, mould and yeast counts;

3) **heavy metals**: Pb, Cd, As, Hg per GB 2762;

4) **pesticide residues**: per GB 2763;

5) **adulteration detection**: species identity verification through macroscopic/microscopic examination and DNA barcoding where necessary.

b) The ranking entity shall assign a dedicated safety sub-score weighing SO₂ and microbiological safety more heavily than for true tea.

### SE-12.X.5 Category Classification and Evaluation Framework

a) Evaluation shall be within declared botanical category with category-specific benchmarks:

1) **quality grade criteria**: premium, standard, acceptable grades per botanical;

2) **origin benchmarks**: recognised production areas (e.g., Hangzhou for Hangbaiju/杭白菊, Zhongwei/Ningxia for Boju/亳菊, Chuzhou for Chuju/滁菊);

3) **processing standards**: drying method (air-dried, freeze-dried, oven-dried), cleaning, sorting;

4) **authenticity markers**: species-specific identification and adulteration detection protocols.

---

> **Note**: This extension module is part of ICO Std 2002 (Tianji Ranking Methodology Standard). Tea rankings carry significant cultural and economic weight, particularly in major tea-producing countries. Ranking entities shall respect the diversity of tea traditions while maintaining rigorous scientific standards. Particular attention shall be paid to: (a) pesticide residue compliance, as tea is consumed as an infusion where residues may be extracted into the drinking water; (b) geographic indication integrity, as origin fraud is prevalent in the specialty tea market; and (c) accurate grade classification, as grade inflation is a widespread issue. For herbal infusions, sulphur fumigation and botanical authenticity are the primary safety concerns.
