# SE-02: Brand Value

**ICO Std 2002 — Extension SE-02**

## Applicable Objects | 适用对象

Corporate brands, product brands, personal brands, regional brands

## Core Dimensions | 核心维度

- Brand Awareness | 品牌认知度
- Brand Loyalty | 品牌忠诚度
- Brand Association | 品牌联想
- Financial Performance | 财务表现
- Market Share | 市场份额
- Brand Equity | 品牌资产
