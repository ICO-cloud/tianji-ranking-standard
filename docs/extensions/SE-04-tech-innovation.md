# SE-04: Technology Innovation | 技术创新

**ICO Std 2002 — Extension SE-04**

---

This extension module specifies the requirements and methodologies for ranking and evaluating technology innovation capability. Technology innovation ranking addresses the distinctive challenge of assessing entities on their capacity to create, develop, and deploy new technologies — a process that spans from fundamental research through commercialisation, and whose outcomes are inherently uncertain and long-cycle. This module extends the core-layer methodology (Chapters 4 through 10) by defining domain-specific indicators for innovation capability assessment, incorporating Technology Readiness Level (TRL) mapping, establishing methods for evaluating technology frontier positioning, and aligning with the conceptual frameworks of ISO 56002 and the Oslo Manual. It is applicable to rankings of enterprises, research institutions, technology clusters, and national innovation systems.

本扩展模块规定了技术创新能力排名与评价的要求和方法论。技术创新排名面临评估实体创造、开发和部署新技术能力的独特挑战——这一过程从基础研究延伸至商业化，其结果本质上是不确定和长周期的。本模块通过定义创新能力评估的领域特定指标、纳入技术成熟度等级（TRL）映射、建立技术前沿定位评估方法，以及与ISO 56002和奥斯陆手册的概念框架对齐来扩展核心层方法论（第4章至第10章）。它适用于企业、研究机构、技术集群和国家创新体系的排名。

## SE-04.1 Scope and Applicable Objects | 范围与适用对象

### SE-04.1.1 Scope | 范围

This module applies to the design, execution, and publication of rankings that evaluate technology innovation capability. It covers:

本模块适用于评价技术创新能力的排名的设计、执行和发布。它涵盖：

a) enterprise-level innovation capability assessment;

企业级创新能力评估；

b) research institution innovation output assessment;

研究机构创新产出评估；

c) technology cluster and regional innovation ecosystem assessment;

技术集群和区域创新生态评估；

d) national innovation capability assessment.

国家创新能力评估。

### SE-04.1.2 Applicable Objects | 适用对象

This module shall apply to rankings of:

本模块应适用于以下排名：

a) technology enterprises across sectors;

跨行业科技企业；

b) universities and public research organisations;

大学和公共研究机构；

c) technology parks, incubators, and innovation clusters;

科技园区、孵化器和创新集群；

d) national or regional innovation systems as defined by established frameworks (e.g., the European Innovation Scoreboard methodology).

由既定框架（如欧洲创新记分牌方法论）定义的国家或区域创新体系。

### SE-04.1.3 Exclusions | 排除项

This module does not apply to:

本模块不适用于：

a) the evaluation of individual research projects or grant applications;

个别研究项目或资助申请的评价；

b) patent litigation or intellectual property valuation for legal purposes;

专利诉讼或法律目的的知识产权估值；

c) the ranking of individual researchers or scientists (which falls under SE-03 Individual Influence in the core framework).

个别研究人员或科学家的排名（属于核心框架中SE-03人物影响力范畴）。

## SE-04.3 Indicator System | 指标体系

### SE-04.3.1 Indicator Hierarchy | 指标层级

The technology innovation indicator system shall be organised into the following three-level hierarchy:

技术创新指标体系应组织为以下三级层级：

a) **Level 1 — Dimensions** | **一级指标（维度）**: R&D Investment, Patent and IP Output, Technology Commercialisation, Innovation Ecosystem, Market Impact;

研发投入、专利与知识产权产出、技术转化、创新生态、市场影响力；

b) **Level 2 — Sub-dimensions** | **二级指标（子维度）**: Decompositions of each dimension as specified in SE-04.3.2 through SE-04.3.6;

各维度的分解，如SE-04.3.2至SE-04.3.6所规定；

c) **Level 3 — Specific measures** | **三级指标（具体度量）**: Operationalised variables for which data is collected.

采集数据的操作化变量。

### SE-04.3.2 R&D Investment | 研发投入

R&D investment assesses the commitment of resources to research and development activities as a leading indicator of innovation capability.

研发投入评估作为创新能力领先指标的研发活动资源承诺。

a) **R&D Expenditure Intensity** | **研发支出强度**: The ratio of R&D expenditure to revenue (for enterprises) or to total budget (for research institutions). The ranking entity shall:

研发支出与收入（企业）或总预算（研究机构）的比率。排名实体应：

1) use the Frascati Manual definition of R&D expenditure;

使用弗拉斯卡蒂手册对研发支出的定义；

2) distinguish between basic research, applied research, and experimental development expenditure;

区分基础研究、应用研究和试验发展支出；

3) report both the aggregate R&D intensity and the breakdown by research type.

报告研发强度总额和按研究类型的分解。

b) **R&D Human Capital** | **研发人力资本**: The quantity and quality of R&D personnel, including:

研发人员的数量和质量，包括：

1) the number of R&D full-time equivalents (FTEs) as a proportion of total workforce;

研发全时当量（FTE）占总劳动力的比例；

2) the educational composition of R&D staff (proportion with doctoral, masters, and bachelors degrees);

研发人员的教育构成（博士、硕士和学士学位比例）；

3) the retention rate of key R&D personnel.

关键研发人员的保留率。

c) **R&D Infrastructure** | **研发基础设施**: The quality and scale of research infrastructure, including:

研究基础设施的质量和规模，包括：

1) the number and type of research laboratories and testing facilities;

研究实验室和测试设施的数量和类型；

2) the investment in research equipment and instrumentation;

研究设备和仪器的投资；

3) access to shared research infrastructure (e.g., national laboratories, supercomputing centres).

共享研究基础设施的访问（如国家实验室、超算中心）。

d) **R&D Portfolio Diversity** | **研发组合多样性**: The breadth and balance of the R&D portfolio, including:

研发组合的广度和平衡，包括：

1) the number of distinct technology areas in which R&D is conducted;

进行研发的不同技术领域数量；

2) the balance between short-term applied projects and long-term exploratory research;

短期应用项目与长期探索性研究的平衡；

3) the TRL distribution of R&D projects (see SE-04.4).

研发项目的TRL分布（见SE-04.4）。

### SE-04.3.3 Patent and IP Output | 专利与知识产权产出

Patent and IP output assesses the quantity, quality, and impact of intellectual property generated by the entity.

专利与知识产权产出评估实体产生的知识产权的数量、质量和影响。

a) **Patent Volume** | **专利数量**: The number of patents filed and granted. The ranking entity shall distinguish:

专利申请和授权的数量。排名实体应区分：

1) invention patents (utility patents) versus design and utility model patents;

发明专利（实用专利）与外观设计和实用新型专利；

2) domestic versus international patent filings (PCT, EPO, USPTO, JPO, CNIPA);

国内与国际专利申请（PCT、EPO、USPTO、JPO、CNIPA）；

3) first filings versus subsequent filings in the same patent family.

首次申请与同一专利族中的后续申请。

b) **Patent Quality** | **专利质量**: The quality and significance of the patent portfolio, assessed through:

专利组合的质量和重要性，通过以下方式评估：

1) citation impact — the average forward citation count relative to the technology field average;

引用影响——相对于技术领域平均的平均前引次数；

2) patent family size — the number of jurisdictions in which patent protection is sought;

专利族规模——寻求专利保护的管辖区域数量；

3) grant rate — the proportion of filed patents that are ultimately granted.

授权率——最终获得授权的申请专利比例。

c) **Patent Relevance and Concentration** | **专利相关性与集中度**: The degree to which patents are concentrated in the entity's core technology domains and contribute to its competitive advantage, including:

专利集中在实体核心技术领域并贡献于其竞争优势的程度，包括：

1) the proportion of patents in the entity's top-3 technology domains;

实体前3个技术领域的专利比例；

2) the strategic alignment of patents with the entity's stated technology roadmap;

专利与实体声明技术路线图的战略一致性；

3) the patent clustering coefficient — the degree to which patents form interconnected technology clusters.

专利聚集系数——专利形成互联技术集群的程度。

d) **Non-Patent IP Output** | **非专利知识产权产出**: Other forms of intellectual property, including:

其他形式的知识产权，包括：

1) published peer-reviewed research papers and their citation impact;

发表的同行评审研究论文及其引用影响；

2) software copyrights and open-source contributions;

软件著作权和开源贡献；

3) trade secrets and proprietary know-how (assessed through expert evaluation where quantifiable metrics are unavailable).

商业秘密和专有技术（在缺乏可量化指标时通过专家评估）。

### SE-04.3.4 Technology Commercialisation | 技术转化

Technology commercialisation assesses the entity's ability to transform research outputs into market-viable products, services, or processes.

技术转化评估实体将研究成果转化为市场可行的产品、服务或流程的能力。

a) **Technology Transfer Rate** | **技术转移率**: The proportion of R&D outputs that are successfully transferred to commercial application, including:

成功转移至商业应用的研发产出比例，包括：

1) the number of technology licensing agreements per unit of R&D expenditure;

每单位研发支出的技术许可协议数量；

2) the revenue from technology licensing and transfer;

技术许可和转移的收入；

3) the number of spin-off companies created from research outputs.

从研究成果创建的衍生公司数量。

b) **Time-to-Market** | **上市时间**: The average elapsed time from initial R&D project initiation to commercial product or service launch. The ranking entity shall:

从研发项目启动到商业产品或服务发布的平均经过时间。排名实体应：

1) define the start and end milestones for time-to-market measurement;

定义上市时间测量的起止里程碑；

2) report the median and interquartile range of time-to-market;

报告上市时间的中位数和四分位距；

3) compare time-to-market against industry benchmarks.

将上市时间与行业基准进行比较。

c) **Product Innovation Rate** | **产品创新率**: The proportion of revenue derived from products or services introduced within a defined recent period (typically three to five years), consistent with the Oslo Manual's definition of product innovators.

在定义的近期期间（通常三至五年）内引入的产品或服务所贡献的收入比例，与奥斯陆手册对产品创新者的定义一致。

d) **Process Innovation Rate** | **流程创新率**: The proportion of production or service delivery processes that have been significantly improved or newly introduced within a defined recent period, consistent with the Oslo Manual's definition of process innovators.

在定义的近期期间内显著改进或新引入的生产或服务交付流程的比例，与奥斯陆手册对流程创新者的定义一致。

### SE-04.3.5 Innovation Ecosystem | 创新生态

Innovation ecosystem assesses the breadth, depth, and vitality of the entity's innovation network and collaborative relationships.

创新生态评估实体创新网络和协作关系的广度、深度和活力。

a) **Collaboration Breadth** | **协作广度**: The diversity and extent of the entity's innovation collaboration network, including:

实体创新协作网络的多样性和广度，包括：

1) the number and type of collaboration partners (universities, research institutions, enterprises, government agencies);

协作伙伴的数量和类型（大学、研究机构、企业、政府机构）；

2) the geographic diversity of collaboration partners;

协作伙伴的地理多样性；

3) the number of joint research projects and co-authored publications.

合作研究项目和合著论文的数量。

b) **Open Innovation Engagement** | **开放式创新参与**: The degree to which the entity participates in open innovation practices, including:

实体参与开放式创新实践的程度，包括：

1) contribution to and adoption of open-source technologies and standards;

对开源技术和标准的贡献和采用；

2) participation in industry consortia and standard-setting bodies;

参与行业联盟和标准制定机构；

3) the number and impact of hackathons, innovation challenges, or crowdsourcing initiatives.

黑客松、创新挑战或众包倡议的数量和影响。

c) **Innovation Talent Attraction** | **创新人才吸引力**: The entity's ability to attract and retain innovation talent, including:

实体吸引和保留创新人才的能力，包括：

1) the net inflow rate of technology talent;

技术人才净流入率；

2) the proportion of new hires with advanced degrees in relevant fields;

新聘人员中具有相关领域高级学位的比例；

3) the number of industry-leading researchers or "highly cited" scientists affiliated with the entity.

与实体关联的行业领先研究者或"高被引"科学家数量。

d) **Innovation Culture Indicators** | **创新文化指标**: Proxy measures for the entity's innovation culture, including:

实体创新文化的代理度量，包括：

1) the proportion of employees participating in innovation or improvement programmes;

参与创新或改进项目的员工比例；

2) the number of internally generated innovation proposals per employee;

每名员工内部创新提案的数量；

3) the tolerance for failure as measured by the proportion of R&D projects that are discontinued without career penalty for project leaders.

以无职业惩罚终止的R&D项目比例衡量的失败容忍度。

### SE-04.3.6 Market Impact | 市场影响力

Market impact assesses the economic and competitive significance of the entity's innovation outputs in the marketplace.

市场影响力评估实体创新产出在市场中的经济和竞争意义。

a) **Market Share in Technology Segments** | **技术细分市场份额**: The entity's market share in its primary technology segments, including:

实体在主要技术细分市场的市场份额，包括：

1) revenue market share in the entity's top-3 technology segments;

实体前3个技术细分的收入市场份额；

2) market share growth rate in technology segments;

技术细分的市场份额增长率；

3) the number of technology segments in which the entity holds a top-3 market position.

实体持有前3市场地位的技术细分数量。

b) **Technology Standard Influence** | **技术标准影响力**: The entity's influence on industry technology standards, including:

实体对行业技术标准的影响力，包括：

1) the number of standard-essential patents (SEPs) held;

持有的标准必要专利（SEP）数量；

2) leadership positions in standard-setting organisations;

在标准制定组织中的领导职位；

3) the adoption rate of the entity's proposed technical standards.

实体提出的技术标准的采用率。

c) **Revenue from New Products** | **新产品收入**: The revenue generated from products or services based on technologies introduced within a defined recent period, as a proportion of total revenue.

基于近期引入技术的产品或服务所产生的收入占总收入的比例。

d) **Competitive Displacement** | **竞争替代效应**: The degree to which the entity's innovations have disrupted or displaced existing market solutions, including:

实体创新颠覆或替代现有市场解决方案的程度，包括：

1) market share gains attributable to innovation-driven product advantages;

归因于创新驱动产品优势的市场份额增长；

2) the entry of the entity into new market segments through innovation;

通过创新进入新市场细分；

3) the adoption of the entity's technology by competitors (indicating industry leadership).

竞争对手采用实体技术（表明行业领导力）。

## SE-04.4 Technology Readiness Level Mapping | 技术成熟度等级映射

### SE-04.4.1 TRL Framework | TRL框架

a) The ranking entity shall use the Technology Readiness Level (TRL) framework to classify the maturity of technologies under evaluation. The TRL scale shall follow the nine-level framework originally developed by NASA and widely adopted internationally:

排名实体应使用技术成熟度等级（TRL）框架对被评估技术的成熟度进行分类。TRL量表应遵循最初由NASA开发并广泛国际采用的九级框架：

| TRL | Description | 描述 |
|-----|-------------|------|
| 1 | Basic principles observed | 基本原理观察 |
| 2 | Technology concept formulated | 技术概念形成 |
| 3 | Experimental proof of concept | 实验概念验证 |
| 4 | Technology validated in laboratory | 技术实验室验证 |
| 5 | Technology validated in relevant environment | 技术相关环境验证 |
| 6 | Technology demonstrated in relevant environment | 技术相关环境演示 |
| 7 | System prototype demonstration in operational environment | 系统原型运行环境演示 |
| 8 | Actual system completed and qualified | 实际系统完成并合格 |
| 9 | Actual system proven in operational environment | 实际系统运行环境验证 |

b) The ranking entity shall document the TRL classification methodology, including the criteria used to assign each TRL level and the evidence required for each classification.

排名实体应记录TRL分类方法论，包括用于分配各TRL等级的标准和各分类所需的证据。

### SE-04.4.2 TRL Distribution Analysis | TRL分布分析

a) The ranking entity shall calculate the TRL Distribution Profile for each ranked entity, showing the number and proportion of technology projects at each TRL level.

排名实体应为每个被排实体计算TRL分布概况，显示各TRL等级的技术项目数量和比例。

b) The ranking entity shall calculate the Technology Maturity Index (TMI) as:

排名实体应计算技术成熟度指数（TMI）为：

$$TMI = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{9} i \cdot n_i}{9 \cdot N}$$

where $n_i$ is the number of projects at TRL level $i$, and $N = \sum_{i=1}^{9} n_i$ is the total number of technology projects. TMI ranges from 0 (no technology activity) to 1 (all technologies at TRL 9).

其中 $n_i$ 为TRL等级 $i$ 的项目数量，$N = \sum_{i=1}^{9} n_i$ 为技术项目总数。TMI范围从0（无技术活动）到1（所有技术处于TRL 9）。

c) The ranking entity shall report both the TMI and the TRL Distribution Profile, as they convey complementary information. A high TMI with a skewed distribution toward TRL 9 may indicate a mature but potentially declining innovation pipeline, whereas a moderate TMI with a broad distribution may indicate a healthy innovation pipeline.

排名实体应同时报告TMI和TRL分布概况，因为它们传达互补信息。高TMI但分布偏向TRL 9可能表明成熟但潜在衰退的创新管线，而中等TMI但分布广泛可能表明健康的创新管线。

### SE-04.4.3 TRL Progression Rate | TRL推进率

a) The ranking entity should assess the rate at which technologies progress through TRL levels, as a measure of innovation execution efficiency. The TRL Progression Rate shall be calculated as:

排名实体宜评估技术通过TRL等级推进的速率，作为创新执行效率的度量。TRL推进率应计算为：

$$TPR = \frac{\Delta TRL_{\text{avg}}}{T_{\text{elapsed}}}$$

where $\Delta TRL_{\text{avg}}$ is the average TRL advancement of tracked technology projects over the measurement period, and $T_{\text{elapsed}}$ is the measurement period in years.

其中 $\Delta TRL_{\text{avg}}$ 为跟踪技术项目在测量期间的平均TRL进展，$T_{\text{elapsed}}$ 为以年为单位的测量期间。

b) The TRL Progression Rate provides insight into the entity's ability to move technologies from research through development to market — a critical capability that pure R&D investment metrics do not capture.

TRL推进率提供了对实体将技术从研究推进到开发再到市场能力的洞察——这是纯研发投入指标无法捕捉的关键能力。

## SE-04.5 Technology Frontier Assessment | 技术前沿性评估

### SE-04.5.1 Definition | 定义

Technology frontier assessment evaluates the degree to which an entity's technologies represent the leading edge of their respective fields. This dimension addresses the qualitative distinction between incremental innovation and frontier or breakthrough innovation.

技术前沿性评估评估实体的技术代表各自领域领先前沿的程度。本维度应对渐进式创新与前沿或突破性创新之间的质性区别。

### SE-04.5.2 Frontier Positioning Method | 前沿定位方法

a) The ranking entity shall assess each entity's technology frontier positioning using the following multi-method approach:

排名实体应使用以下多方法路径评估每个实体的技术前沿定位：

1) **Patent Landscape Analysis** | **专利景观分析**: Mapping the entity's patent portfolio against the global patent landscape to identify the entity's position relative to technology frontiers. The ranking entity shall use:

将实体的专利组合与全球专利景观进行映射，以识别实体相对于技术前沿的位置。排名实体应使用：

- patent citation network analysis to identify influential and frontier patents;

专利引用网络分析以识别有影响力和前沿的专利；

- technology trajectory analysis to identify emerging technology directions.

技术轨迹分析以识别新兴技术方向。

2) **Expert Panel Assessment** | **专家小组评估**: A structured expert assessment of the entity's technology position, using a Delphi or modified Delphi process as specified in Chapter 5 of the core layer. The expert panel shall include:

使用核心层第5章规定的德尔菲法或改进德尔菲法对实体技术位置的结构化专家评估。专家小组应包括：

- at least five recognised experts in the relevant technology domains;

至少五名相关技术领域的公认专家；

- representatives from both academia and industry.

学术界和产业界的代表。

3) **Publication Impact Analysis** | **出版影响分析**: For research-intensive entities, analysis of the citation impact and journal prestige of published research outputs, using standard bibliometric indicators:

对于研究密集型实体，使用标准文献计量指标分析发表研究成果的引用影响和期刊声望：

- field-weighted citation impact;

领域加权引用影响；

- proportion of publications in top-quartile journals;

前四分位期刊发表论文比例；

- h-index of key research personnel.

关键研究人员的h指数。

b) The Frontier Positioning Score shall be calculated as:

前沿定位评分应计算为：

$$FPS = 0.40 \cdot P_{\text{frontier}} + 0.35 \cdot E_{\text{frontier}} + 0.25 \cdot B_{\text{frontier}}$$

where $P_{\text{frontier}}$ is the patent landscape frontier score, $E_{\text{frontier}}$ is the expert panel frontier score, and $B_{\text{frontier}}$ is the bibliometric frontier score, each normalised to 0-1.

其中 $P_{\text{frontier}}$ 为专利景观前沿评分，$E_{\text{frontier}}$ 为专家小组前沿评分，$B_{\text{frontier}}$ 为文献计量前沿评分，各归一化至0-1。

### SE-04.5.3 Frontier Breadth vs. Depth | 前沿广度与深度

a) The ranking entity shall assess both the breadth and depth of frontier technology positioning:

排名实体应评估前沿技术定位的广度和深度：

1) **Frontier Breadth** | **前沿广度**: The number of distinct technology domains in which the entity has frontier-level technology. An entity with frontier presence in multiple domains shall score higher on breadth.

实体拥有前沿级技术的不同技术领域数量。在多个领域具有前沿存在的实体在广度上应得分更高。

2) **Frontier Depth** | **前沿深度**: The degree of advancement beyond the current state of the art in the entity's primary technology domains. An entity whose technologies significantly surpass the current frontier shall score higher on depth.

实体在主要技术领域超越当前技术前沿的进步程度。技术显著超越当前前沿的实体在深度上应得分更高。

b) The ranking entity shall report both frontier breadth and depth scores alongside the overall Frontier Positioning Score.

排名实体应与整体前沿定位评分一起报告前沿广度和深度评分。

## SE-04.6 Data Collection | 数据采集

### SE-04.6.1 Data Source Categories | 数据来源类别

The ranking entity shall collect data from the following categories of sources:

排名实体应从以下类别来源采集数据：

a) **Patent Databases** | **专利数据库**: Global patent databases (e.g., WIPO Patentscope, EPO Espacenet, national patent office databases) for patent filing, grant, and citation data.

全球专利数据库（如WIPO Patentscope、EPO Espacenet、国家专利局数据库）的专利申请、授权和引用数据。

b) **Financial and Corporate Reports** | **财务与企业报告**: Annual reports, R&D expenditure disclosures, and investor communications from the ranked entities.

被排实体的年度报告、研发支出披露和投资者沟通。

c) **Bibliometric Databases** | **文献计量数据库**: Scientific publication and citation databases (e.g., Scopus, Web of Science) for research output analysis.

科学出版和引用数据库（如Scopus、Web of Science）的研究产出分析。

d) **Government Statistics** | **政府统计数据**: National and international innovation statistics (e.g., OECD Main Science and Technology Indicators, national R&D surveys).

国家和国际创新统计数据（如OECD主要科学技术指标、国家研发调查）。

e) **Industry and Market Data** | **行业与市场数据**: Market research reports, industry surveys, and technology market analysis from established providers.

来自成熟提供商的市场研究报告、行业调查和技术市场分析。

### SE-04.6.2 Patent Data Quality Requirements | 专利数据质量要求

a) Patent data shall be collected from authoritative patent office databases and shall include complete bibliographic data, citation data, and legal status information.

专利数据应从权威专利局数据库采集，并应包含完整的书目数据、引用数据和法律状态信息。

b) The ranking entity shall apply patent family deduplication to avoid double-counting inventions filed in multiple jurisdictions.

排名实体应进行专利族去重，以避免在多个管辖区域申请的发明被重复计算。

c) Citation data shall be adjusted for citation lag (the time delay between patent publication and citation accumulation) using established normalisation methods.

引用数据应使用既定归一化方法调整引用滞后（专利发布与引用积累之间的时间延迟）。

### SE-04.6.3 R&D Data Reporting Standards | 研发数据报告标准

a) R&D expenditure data shall comply with the definitions and classifications of the Frascati Manual (OECD, 2015) to ensure international comparability.

研发支出数据应符合弗拉斯卡蒂手册（OECD, 2015）的定义和分类，以确保国际可比性。

b) Where entities report R&D expenditure using different accounting standards, the ranking entity shall apply reconciliation adjustments and shall document the adjustments made.

当实体使用不同会计准则报告研发支出时，排名实体应进行协调调整并应记录所做的调整。

## SE-04.7 Scoring Methodology | 评分方法

### SE-04.7.1 Scoring Scale | 评分量表

a) Each Level 3 indicator shall be scored on a normalised scale from 0 to 100.

每个三级指标应使用0至100的归一化量表评分。

b) Quantitative indicators shall be normalised using the min-max method or z-score method as specified in Chapter 8 of the core layer.

定量指标应使用核心层第8章规定的最小最大法或z分数法进行归一化。

c) The Frontier Positioning Score (SE-04.5.2) shall be integrated into the overall score through the Market Impact dimension.

前沿定位评分（SE-04.5.2）应通过市场影响力维度整合至总分。

### SE-04.7.2 Weighting | 权重

a) The default weight scheme for the five Level 1 dimensions shall be as follows:

五个一级维度的默认权重方案如下：

| Dimension | 维度 | Default Weight | 默认权重 |
|-----------|------|---------------|---------|
| R&D Investment | 研发投入 | 0.20 | 0.20 |
| Patent and IP Output | 专利与知识产权产出 | 0.20 | 0.20 |
| Technology Commercialisation | 技术转化 | 0.25 | 0.25 |
| Innovation Ecosystem | 创新生态 | 0.15 | 0.15 |
| Market Impact | 市场影响力 | 0.20 | 0.20 |

b) The default weights reflect the principle that technology innovation ranking shall emphasise the conversion of R&D investment into commercial outcomes, while recognising the importance of sustained investment and ecosystem participation.

默认权重反映了技术创新排名应强调研发投入向商业成果转化的原则，同时承认持续投资和生态参与的重要性。

c) For rankings of early-stage technology companies or research institutions where commercialisation outcomes are not yet available, the ranking entity may increase the weights of R&D Investment and Patent Output to a combined total of 0.55, with corresponding reductions in Technology Commercialisation and Market Impact.

对于尚未获得商业化成果的早期技术公司或研究机构的排名，排名实体可将研发投入和专利产出的合并权重增加至0.55，并相应减少技术转化和市场影响力。

### SE-04.7.3 Aggregation | 聚合

a) Scores shall be aggregated using the weighted sum method specified in Chapter 8 of the core layer.

评分应使用核心层第8章规定的加权和法聚合。

b) The overall innovation score, the Technology Maturity Index (SE-04.4.2), and the Frontier Positioning Score (SE-04.5.2) shall all be reported in the ranking publication.

创新总分、技术成熟度指数（SE-04.4.2）和前沿定位评分（SE-04.5.2）均应在排名发布中报告。

c) The ranking entity shall report the TRL Distribution Profile for each ranked entity.

排名实体应报告每个被排实体的TRL分布概况。

## SE-04.8 Alignment with International Standards | 与国际标准的对齐

### SE-04.8.1 ISO 56002 Alignment | 与ISO 56002的对齐

a) The indicator system shall be consistent with the innovation management system guidance of ISO 56002:2019, particularly:

指标体系应与ISO 56002:2019的创新管理体系指南一致，特别是：

1) the emphasis on leadership and commitment to innovation;

对创新领导力和承诺的强调；

2) the focus on innovation processes from ideation through implementation;

对从构思到实施的创新流程的关注；

3) the importance of evaluation and improvement of innovation performance.

创新绩效评估和改进的重要性。

b) The ranking entity shall map each indicator in this module to the corresponding ISO 56002 process area and shall document the mapping.

排名实体应将本模块中的每个指标映射到对应的ISO 56002过程领域并应记录该映射。

### SE-04.8.2 Oslo Manual Alignment | 与奥斯陆手册的对齐

a) The definitions of product innovation, process innovation, and innovation activities used in this module shall be consistent with the Oslo Manual 2018 (4th edition).

本模块中使用的产品创新、流程创新和创新活动的定义应与奥斯陆手册2018（第4版）一致。

b) The ranking entity shall use the Oslo Manual's definitions when classifying innovation types and shall document any extensions or adaptations for ranking purposes.

排名实体在分类创新类型时应使用奥斯陆手册的定义，并应记录为排名目的所做的任何扩展或适配。

c) Data collection on innovation activities should follow the Oslo Manual's survey guidelines where applicable, to ensure comparability with official innovation statistics.

创新活动数据采集在适用时应遵循奥斯陆手册的调查指南，以确保与官方创新统计的可比性。

## SE-04.9 Publication and Transparency | 发布与透明度

### SE-04.9.1 Publication Requirements | 发布要求

In addition to the requirements specified in Chapter 9 of the core layer, technology innovation ranking publications shall include:

除核心层第9章规定的要求外，技术创新排名发布还应包括：

a) the Technology Maturity Index and TRL Distribution Profile for each ranked entity;

每个被排实体的技术成熟度指数和TRL分布概况；

b) the Frontier Positioning Score, including frontier breadth and depth scores;

前沿定位评分，包括前沿广度和深度评分；

c) the patent data sources and deduplication methodology;

专利数据来源和去重方法；

d) the R&D data reporting standard used and any reconciliation adjustments;

使用的研发数据报告标准和任何协调调整；

e) for entity types where commercialisation data is limited, a disclosure of the modified weighting scheme and its rationale.

对于商业化数据有限的实体类型，修改权重方案及其理由的披露。

### SE-04.9.2 Entity Identification | 实体标识

a) Each ranked entity shall be uniquely identified using:

每个被排实体应使用以下方式唯一标识：

1) the legal entity name and registration details;

法律实体名称和注册详情；

2) the scope of innovation activities included (e.g., consolidated group, specific division);

纳入的创新活动范围（如合并集团、特定部门）；

3) the reporting period for financial and R&D data.

财务和研发数据的报告期间。

---

> **Note**: This extension module is part of ICO Std 2002 (Tianji Ranking Methodology Standard). Technology innovation evaluation faces a fundamental tension between measuring inputs (R&D investment), outputs (patents, products), and outcomes (market impact, competitive advantage). No single dimension fully captures innovation capability. The TRL framework and Frontier Positioning Score are distinctive contributions of this module that address the need to assess not just how much innovation activity occurs, but how advanced and how leading-edge that activity is. Ranking entities should be cautious about over-reliance on patent counts as proxies for innovation quality, and should complement quantitative metrics with expert assessment and frontier analysis.
>
> **注**：本扩展模块属于 ICO Std 2002（天机排名方法论标准）。技术创新评价面临测量投入（研发投资）、产出（专利、产品）和结果（市场影响、竞争优势）之间的根本张力。没有单一维度能完全捕捉创新能力。TRL框架和前沿定位评分是本模块的独特贡献，应对了不仅评估发生了多少创新活动，还评估这些活动有多先进和前沿的需求。排名实体应谨慎避免过度依赖专利数量作为创新质量的代理，并应以专家评估和前沿分析补充量化指标。
