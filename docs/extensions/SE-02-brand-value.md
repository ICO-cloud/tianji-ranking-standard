# SE-02: Brand Value | 品牌价值

**ICO Std 2002 — Extension SE-02**

---

This extension module specifies the requirements and methodologies for ranking and evaluating brand value. Brand value ranking occupies a distinctive position in the ranking landscape because it must integrate financial metrics with perceptual and behavioural indicators, navigate the tension between short-term market performance and long-term brand equity, and accommodate the diverse forms that brand value takes across industries and cultures. This module extends the core-layer methodology (Chapters 4 through 10) by defining domain-specific indicators for brand evaluation, data collection protocols that combine market data with survey-based measurement, and scoring procedures that capture both the economic and intangible dimensions of brand value. It aligns with the conceptual frameworks of ISO 10668 and ISO 20671 while providing ranking-specific operationalisation.

本扩展模块规定了品牌价值排名与评价的要求和方法论。品牌价值排名在排名领域中占据独特位置，因为它必须将财务指标与感知和行为指标相整合，处理短期市场表现与长期品牌资产之间的张力，并适应品牌价值在不同行业和文化中所呈现的多样形式。本模块通过定义品牌评价的领域特定指标、结合市场数据与调查测量的数据采集协议，以及捕捉品牌价值经济维度和无形维度的评分程序来扩展核心层方法论（第4章至第10章）。它对齐ISO 10668和ISO 20671的概念框架，同时提供排名特定的操作化。

---

## SE-02.1 Scope and Applicable Objects | 范围与适用对象

### SE-02.1.1 Scope | 范围

This module applies to the design, execution, and publication of rankings that evaluate brand value. It covers:

本模块适用于评价品牌价值的排名的设计、执行和发布。它涵盖：

a) corporate brands (the brand of the enterprise as a whole);

企业品牌（作为整体的企业品牌）；

b) product brands (brands associated with specific product lines or categories);

产品品牌（与特定产品线或类别关联的品牌）；

c) personal brands (the brand value of individuals in professional or public contexts);

个人品牌（个人在专业或公共语境中的品牌价值）；

d) regional or place brands (the brand value of geographic regions, cities, or nations).

区域或地方品牌（地理区域、城市或国家的品牌价值）。

### SE-02.1.2 Applicable Objects | 适用对象

This module shall apply to rankings of:

本模块应适用于以下排名：

a) brands within a defined industry or sector;

定义行业或部门内的品牌；

b) brands across industries using a universal brand valuation methodology;

使用通用品牌估值方法的跨行业品牌；

c) brand portfolios of conglomerate entities.

集团实体的品牌组合。

### SE-02.1.3 Exclusions | 排除项

This module does not apply to:

本模块不适用于：

a) the legal determination of trademark value for accounting or taxation purposes;

为会计或税务目的对商标价值的法律认定；

b) brand licensing transaction pricing;

品牌许可交易定价；

c) goodwill impairment testing under financial reporting standards.

财务报告准则下的商誉减值测试。

## SE-02.3 Indicator System | 指标体系

### SE-02.3.1 Indicator Hierarchy | 指标层级

The brand value indicator system shall be organised into the following three-level hierarchy:

品牌价值指标体系应组织为以下三级层级：

a) **Level 1 — Dimensions** | **一级指标（维度）**: Brand Awareness, Brand Loyalty, Brand Association, Perceived Quality, Brand Equity (Financial);

品牌认知度、品牌忠诚度、品牌联想、感知质量、品牌资产（财务）；

b) **Level 2 — Sub-dimensions** | **二级指标（子维度）**: Decompositions of each dimension as specified in SE-02.3.2 through SE-02.3.6;

各维度的分解，如SE-02.3.2至SE-02.3.6所规定；

c) **Level 3 — Specific measures** | **三级指标（具体度量）**: Operationalised variables for which data is collected.

采集数据的操作化变量。

### SE-02.3.2 Brand Awareness | 品牌认知度

Brand awareness assesses the degree to which a brand is recognised and recalled by the target population.

品牌认知度评估品牌被目标人群识别和回忆的程度。

a) **Top-of-Mind Awareness** | **第一提及认知**: The proportion of the target population that names the brand first when asked about the product category. The ranking entity shall specify:

当被问及产品类别时，首先提及该品牌的目标人群比例。排名实体应规定：

1) the target population and sampling method;

目标人群和抽样方法；

2) the survey prompt or stimulus used;

使用的调查提示或刺激；

3) the survey context (aided vs. unaided recall).

调查语境（辅助回忆 vs. 无辅助回忆）。

b) **Aided Recognition** | **辅助识别**: The proportion of the target population that recognises the brand when presented with brand elements (name, logo, packaging). The ranking entity shall specify the brand elements presented and the presentation format.

当呈现品牌元素（名称、标志、包装）时识别品牌的目标人群比例。排名实体应规定呈现的品牌元素和呈现格式。

c) **Awareness Breadth** | **认知广度**: The extent of brand awareness across demographic segments, geographic regions, and market segments. A brand with broad cross-segment awareness shall score higher than one with concentrated awareness in a single segment.

品牌认知度在人口统计群体、地理区域和市场细分中的广度。具有广泛跨群体认知的品牌应比集中在单一群体的品牌得分更高。

d) **Digital Presence** | **数字存在度**: The strength of the brand's presence in digital channels, including:

品牌在数字渠道中的存在强度，包括：

1) search engine query volume for the brand name;

品牌名称的搜索引擎查询量；

2) social media follower counts and engagement rates;

社交媒体关注者数量和互动率；

3) website traffic and digital mention volume.

网站流量和数字提及量。

### SE-02.3.3 Brand Loyalty | 品牌忠诚度

Brand loyalty assesses the degree to which consumers consistently choose, prefer, and advocate for a brand over alternatives.

品牌忠诚度评估消费者一致地选择、偏好和倡导某品牌而非替代品的程度。

a) **Behavioural Loyalty** | **行为忠诚**: Measured through observable purchasing behaviour, including:

通过可观察的购买行为测量，包括：

1) repeat purchase rate over a defined period;

定义期间内的复购率；

2) share of wallet (proportion of category spending allocated to the brand);

钱包份额（分配给该品牌的品类支出比例）；

3) customer retention rate and churn rate.

客户保留率和流失率。

b) **Attitudinal Loyalty** | **态度忠诚**: Measured through self-reported commitment and preference, including:

通过自我报告的承诺和偏好测量，包括：

1) stated preference for the brand over alternatives;

对品牌相对于替代品的表述偏好；

2) emotional attachment to the brand;

对品牌的情感依附；

3) willingness to pay a premium for the brand.

为品牌支付溢价的意愿。

c) **Advocacy Behaviour** | **倡导行为**: The degree to which existing customers actively recommend and promote the brand, including:

现有客户积极推荐和推广品牌的程度，包括：

1) Net Promoter Score (NPS);

净推荐值（NPS）；

2) organic referral rates and word-of-mouth activity;

自然推荐率和口碑活动；

3) user-generated content creation about the brand.

关于品牌的用户生成内容创作。

d) **Loyalty Resilience** | **忠诚韧性**: The degree to which loyalty persists in the face of competitive pressure or negative events. The ranking entity should assess:

忠诚度在面对竞争压力或负面事件时持续的程度。排名实体宜评估：

1) brand switching rates during promotional periods by competitors;

竞争对手促销期间的品牌转换率；

2) customer response to negative publicity or product issues.

客户对负面宣传或产品问题的反应。

### SE-02.3.4 Brand Association | 品牌联想

Brand association assesses the strength, favourability, and uniqueness of the mental associations linked to the brand in the minds of consumers.

品牌联想评估消费者心目中与品牌关联的心智联想的强度、有利性和独特性。

a) **Association Strength** | **联想强度**: The speed and certainty with which consumers connect specific attributes, benefits, or experiences with the brand. Assessment methods may include:

消费者将特定属性、利益或体验与品牌联系的速度和确定性。评估方法可包括：

1) free association tasks and response latency measurement;

自由联想任务和反应潜伏期测量；

2) brand personality mapping using established frameworks (e.g., Aaker's Brand Personality Dimensions);

使用既定框架（如Aaker品牌人格维度）的品牌人格映射；

3) attribute-brand linkage surveys.

属性-品牌关联调查。

b) **Association Favourability** | **联想有利性**: The degree to which brand associations are positive and aligned with the brand's intended positioning. The ranking entity shall assess:

品牌联想的积极程度及其与品牌预期定位的一致性。排名实体应评估：

1) the ratio of positive to negative associations;

正面与负面联想的比例；

2) the alignment of actual associations with the brand's stated values and positioning.

实际联想与品牌声明价值和定位的一致性。

c) **Association Uniqueness** | **联想独特性**: The degree to which the brand's associations are distinctive and not shared by competitors. Unique associations that differentiate the brand shall contribute positively to the score.

品牌联想的独特性及其不被竞争对手共享的程度。区分品牌的独特联想应正向贡献评分。

d) **Association Consistency** | **联想一致性**: The degree to which brand associations are consistent across different consumer segments, touchpoints, and geographic markets. High consistency shall indicate a coherent brand identity.

品牌联想在不同消费群体、触点和地理市场中一致的程度。高一致性应表明连贯的品牌身份。

### SE-02.3.5 Perceived Quality | 感知质量

Perceived quality assesses the consumer's overall judgement of the brand's product or service quality, distinct from objective quality measurements (see SE-01).

感知质量评估消费者对品牌产品或服务质量的整体判断，有别于客观质量测量（见SE-01）。

a) **Overall Quality Perception** | **整体质量感知**: The consumer's global assessment of the brand's quality, measured through:

消费者对品牌质量的全局评估，通过以下方式测量：

1) rating scales anchored by quality descriptors;

由质量描述词锚定的评分量表；

2) comparative quality assessments against named competitors;

与指名竞争对手的比较质量评估；

3) quality ranking within the product category.

产品类别内的质量排名。

b) **Reliability Perception** | **可靠性感知**: The consumer's belief in the consistency and dependability of the brand's products or services.

消费者对品牌产品或服务一致性和可靠性的信念。

c) **Service Quality Perception** | **服务质量感知**: The consumer's assessment of the brand's customer service and post-purchase support quality.

消费者对品牌客户服务和购买后支持质量的评估。

d) **Innovation Perception** | **创新感知**: The consumer's perception of the brand's innovativeness and ability to introduce new and improved products.

消费者对品牌创新性和引入改良新产品能力的感知。

### SE-02.3.6 Brand Equity (Financial) | 品牌资产（财务）

Brand equity (financial) assesses the economic value attributable to the brand as a separable asset, consistent with the approaches defined in ISO 10668.

品牌资产（财务）评估可归因于品牌作为可分离资产的经济价值，与ISO 10668定义的方法一致。

a) **Revenue Premium** | **收入溢价**: The incremental revenue attributable to the brand, estimated as the difference between the brand's actual revenue and the revenue that would be expected for an unbranded or generic equivalent. The ranking entity shall specify:

可归因于品牌的增量收入，估计为品牌实际收入与无品牌或通用等效产品预期收入之间的差额。排名实体应规定：

1) the method for estimating generic-equivalent revenue;

估计通用等效收入的方法；

2) the adjustments made for differences in distribution, scale, and other non-brand factors.

对分销、规模和其他非品牌因素差异的调整。

b) **Brand Contribution to Market Value** | **品牌对市场价值的贡献**: The estimated proportion of the enterprise's market capitalisation attributable to the brand, using one or more of the following approaches:

使用以下一种或多种方法估计可归因于品牌的企业市值比例：

1) income approach (discounted cash flow of brand-attributable earnings);

收益法（品牌可归属收益的折现现金流）；

2) market approach (comparable brand transactions or licensing multiples);

市场法（可比品牌交易或许可倍数）；

3) cost approach (historical investment in brand development).

成本法（品牌开发的历史投资）。

c) **Licensing and Royalty Potential** | **许可与特许潜力**: The estimated royalty rate that could be charged for licensing the brand, based on comparable licensing agreements or relief-from-royalty calculations.

基于可比许可协议或免除特许费计算，估计品牌许可可收取的特许费率。

d) **Price Premium** | **价格溢价**: The average price premium that the brand commands over generic or competitor products in the same category, expressed as a percentage.

品牌在同品类中相对于通用或竞争对手产品的平均价格溢价，以百分比表示。

## SE-02.4 Data Collection | 数据采集

### SE-02.4.1 Data Source Categories | 数据来源类别

The ranking entity shall collect data from the following categories of sources:

排名实体应从以下类别来源采集数据：

a) **Market Research Surveys** | **市场调研调查**: Structured surveys measuring brand awareness, loyalty, association, and perceived quality. Surveys shall comply with the methodological requirements specified in Chapter 7 of the core layer.

测量品牌认知度、忠诚度、联想和感知质量的结构化调查。调查应符合核心层第7章规定的方法论要求。

b) **Financial Data** | **财务数据**: Publicly available financial statements, annual reports, and market data from established financial data providers.

来自成熟金融数据提供商的公开财务报表、年度报告和市场数据。

c) **Social Media and Digital Analytics** | **社交媒体与数字分析**: Data from social media monitoring platforms, search analytics, and web traffic analysis tools.

来自社交媒体监测平台、搜索分析和网站流量分析工具的数据。

d) **Industry Reports** | **行业报告**: Brand valuation reports and industry analyses from established brand consultancy and research firms.

来自成熟品牌咨询和研究公司的品牌估值报告和行业分析。

e) **Consumer Transaction Data** | **消费者交易数据**: Purchase data, loyalty programme data, and point-of-sale data, subject to privacy regulations and consumer consent.

购买数据、忠诚度计划数据和销售点数据，受隐私法规和消费者同意约束。

### SE-02.4.2 Survey Methodology Requirements | 调查方法论要求

a) Brand perception surveys shall meet the following minimum requirements:

品牌感知调查应满足以下最低要求：

1) sample size sufficient to achieve a margin of error not exceeding ±3% at the 95% confidence level for the target population;

样本量足以在目标人群95%置信水平下实现不超过±3%的抽样误差；

2) probability sampling or validated non-probability sampling with documented weighting adjustments;

概率抽样或经记录加权调整的验证非概率抽样；

3) survey instruments that have been pilot-tested and validated for the target population.

已对目标人群进行试点测试和验证的调查工具。

b) The ranking entity shall document the survey methodology, including:

排名实体应记录调查方法论，包括：

1) sampling frame and method;

抽样框和方法；

2) sample composition and demographics;

样本组成和人口统计；

3) data collection mode (online, telephone, face-to-face, mixed);

数据采集模式（在线、电话、面对面、混合）；

4) response rate and non-response analysis;

响应率和无响应分析；

5) questionnaire design and cognitive testing results.

问卷设计和认知测试结果。

### SE-02.4.3 Financial Data Requirements | 财务数据要求

a) Financial data shall be sourced from audited financial statements where available. Where audited data is not available (e.g., private companies), the ranking entity shall use the best available estimates and shall document the data source and estimation method.

财务数据应从经审计的财务报表获取。当审计数据不可获得时（如私营公司），排名实体应使用最佳可用估计并应记录数据来源和估计方法。

b) Financial data shall be adjusted for:

财务数据应调整以下因素：

1) currency effects (using exchange rates at the valuation date);

货币效应（使用估值日的汇率）；

2) accounting policy differences across jurisdictions;

跨管辖区域的会计政策差异；

3) non-recurring items that distort brand-attributable earnings.

扭曲品牌可归属收益的非经常性项目。

c) The ranking entity shall specify the valuation date and the financial period used for brand value calculation.

排名实体应规定估值日期和用于品牌价值计算的财务期间。

## SE-02.5 Brand Health Index | 品牌健康度指数

### SE-02.5.1 Definition | 定义

The Brand Health Index (BHI) is a composite measure that synthesises the perceptual and behavioural dimensions of brand value into a single diagnostic indicator. The BHI complements the overall brand value score by providing an integrated assessment of brand vitality.

品牌健康度指数（BHI）是一个复合度量，将品牌价值的感知和行为维度综合为单一诊断指标。BHI通过提供品牌活力的综合评估来补充整体品牌价值评分。

### SE-02.5.2 Calculation | 计算

The Brand Health Index shall be calculated as:

品牌健康度指数应计算为：

$$BHI = w_1 \cdot A_{\text{norm}} + w_2 \cdot L_{\text{norm}} + w_3 \cdot Q_{\text{norm}} + w_4 \cdot G_{\text{norm}}$$

where:

其中：

- $A_{\text{norm}}$ = normalised brand awareness score (0 to 1);

归一化品牌认知度评分（0至1）；

- $L_{\text{norm}}$ = normalised brand loyalty score (0 to 1);

归一化品牌忠诚度评分（0至1）；

- $Q_{\text{norm}}$ = normalised perceived quality score (0 to 1);

归一化感知质量评分（0至1）；

- $G_{\text{norm}}$ = normalised brand growth momentum score (0 to 1), calculated as the year-over-year change in the composite of awareness, loyalty, and quality scores;

归一化品牌增长动量评分（0至1），计算为认知度、忠诚度和质量评分综合值的同比变化；

- Default weights: $w_1 = 0.20$, $w_2 = 0.30$, $w_3 = 0.30$, $w_4 = 0.20$.

默认权重：$w_1 = 0.20$，$w_2 = 0.30$，$w_3 = 0.30$，$w_4 = 0.20$。

### SE-02.5.3 Interpretation | 解释

The BHI shall be interpreted as follows:

BHI应解释如下：

a) **BHI ≥ 0.80**: Strong brand health — the brand exhibits high awareness, strong loyalty, favourable quality perception, and positive growth momentum.

强健品牌健康——品牌表现出高认知度、强忠诚度、有利质量感知和正向增长动量。

b) **0.60 ≤ BHI < 0.80**: Moderate brand health — the brand has established presence but faces challenges in one or more dimensions.

中等品牌健康——品牌已建立存在但在一个或多个维度面临挑战。

c) **0.40 ≤ BHI < 0.60**: Weak brand health — the brand shows significant vulnerabilities requiring strategic attention.

弱品牌健康——品牌显示需要战略关注的显著脆弱性。

d) **BHI < 0.40**: Critical brand health — the brand is at risk of decline or irrelevance and requires urgent intervention.

危急品牌健康——品牌面临衰退或无关风险，需要紧急干预。

> **Note**: The BHI thresholds are indicative. The ranking entity may adjust the thresholds based on industry benchmarks, but shall document any adjustment and its rationale.
>
> **注**：BHI阈值是指示性的。排名实体可基于行业基准调整阈值，但应记录任何调整及其理由。

## SE-02.6 Scoring Methodology | 评分方法

### SE-02.6.1 Scoring Scale | 评分量表

a) Each Level 3 indicator shall be scored on a normalised scale from 0 to 100.

每个三级指标应使用0至100的归一化量表评分。

b) Financial indicators shall be normalised using the min-max method within the ranked brand set.

财务指标应使用被排品牌集内的最小最大法进行归一化。

c) Perceptual indicators from surveys shall be scored using the survey instrument's native scale, then normalised to the 0-100 range.

来自调查的感知指标应使用调查工具的原始量表评分，然后归一化至0-100范围。

### SE-02.6.2 Weighting | 权重

a) The default weight scheme for the five Level 1 dimensions shall be as follows:

五个一级维度的默认权重方案如下：

| Dimension | 维度 | Default Weight | 默认权重 |
|-----------|------|---------------|---------|
| Brand Awareness | 品牌认知度 | 0.15 | 0.15 |
| Brand Loyalty | 品牌忠诚度 | 0.25 | 0.25 |
| Brand Association | 品牌联想 | 0.15 | 0.15 |
| Perceived Quality | 感知质量 | 0.20 | 0.20 |
| Brand Equity (Financial) | 品牌资产（财务） | 0.25 | 0.25 |

b) The default weights reflect the principle that brand value is determined by a combination of financial performance and consumer-based brand equity, with loyalty and financial value carrying the highest weights.

默认权重反映了品牌价值由财务表现和消费者品牌资产共同决定的原则，其中忠诚度和财务价值承担最高权重。

c) For rankings focused on consumer brand perception (excluding financial valuation), the ranking entity may reduce the weight of Brand Equity (Financial) and redistribute the weight across the other four dimensions, provided the redistribution is documented and justified.

对于聚焦消费者品牌感知（不含财务估值）的排名，排名实体可降低品牌资产（财务）的权重并将权重重新分配至其他四个维度，前提是重新分配被记录和论证。

### SE-02.6.3 Aggregation | 聚合

a) Scores shall be aggregated using the weighted sum method specified in Chapter 8 of the core layer.

评分应使用核心层第8章规定的加权和法聚合。

b) The overall brand value score and the Brand Health Index shall both be reported in the ranking publication.

品牌价值总分和品牌健康度指数均应在排名发布中报告。

c) Where the overall brand value score and BHI produce divergent rankings (e.g., a brand with high financial value but declining health), the ranking entity shall highlight this divergence in the publication and provide interpretive commentary.

当品牌价值总分和BHI产生分歧排名时（如品牌具有高财务价值但健康度下降），排名实体应在发布中强调这一分歧并提供解释性评论。

## SE-02.7 Alignment with International Standards | 与国际标准的对齐

### SE-02.7.1 ISO 10668 Alignment | 与ISO 10668的对齐

a) The Brand Equity (Financial) dimension (SE-02.3.6) shall be consistent with the three approaches to monetary brand valuation specified in ISO 10668:2010:

品牌资产（财务）维度（SE-02.3.6）应与ISO 10668:2010规定的货币品牌估值三种方法一致：

1) income approach;

收益法；

2) market approach;

市场法；

3) cost approach.

成本法。

b) The ranking entity shall specify which approach or combination of approaches is used for the financial dimension and shall document the assumptions and limitations of the chosen approach.

排名实体应规定财务维度使用哪种方法或方法组合，并应记录所选方法的假设和局限性。

### SE-02.7.2 ISO 20671 Alignment | 与ISO 20671的对齐

a) The indicator system shall be consistent with the dimensions of brand evaluation specified in ISO 20671:2019, particularly:

指标体系应与ISO 20671:2019规定的品牌评估维度一致，特别是：

1) brand strength (encompassing awareness, loyalty, and association);

品牌强度（涵盖认知度、忠诚度和联想）；

2) brand performance (encompassing financial and market performance);

品牌表现（涵盖财务和市场表现）；

3) legal and regulatory compliance.

法律和法规合规性。

b) The ranking entity shall map each indicator in this module to the corresponding ISO 20671 dimension and shall document any deviations.

排名实体应将本模块中的每个指标映射到对应的ISO 20671维度，并应记录任何偏差。

## SE-02.8 Publication and Transparency | 发布与透明度

### SE-02.8.1 Publication Requirements | 发布要求

In addition to the requirements specified in Chapter 9 of the core layer, brand value ranking publications shall include:

除核心层第9章规定的要求外，品牌价值排名发布还应包括：

a) the Brand Health Index for each ranked brand;

每个被排品牌的品牌健康度指数；

b) the brand valuation approach used for the financial dimension and its key assumptions;

财务维度使用的品牌估值方法及其关键假设；

c) the survey methodology summary, including sample size, method, and period;

调查方法论摘要，包括样本量、方法和期间；

d) the valuation date and financial period;

估值日期和财务期间；

e) a statement of compliance with ISO 10668 and ISO 20671 requirements.

ISO 10668和ISO 20671要求的合规声明。

### SE-02.8.2 Brand Identification | 品牌标识

a) Each ranked brand shall be uniquely identified using:

每个被排品牌应使用以下方式唯一标识：

1) the brand name and legal entity;

品牌名称和法律实体；

2) the scope of the brand (corporate, product line, geographic scope);

品牌范围（企业、产品线、地理范围）；

3) the brand elements included in the evaluation (name, logo, trade dress, etc.).

评估中包含的品牌元素（名称、标志、商业外观等）。

---

> **Note**: This extension module is part of ICO Std 2002 (Tianji Ranking Methodology Standard). Brand value evaluation inherently combines objective financial data with subjective perceptual measures. Ranking entities should be transparent about the relative contributions of financial and perceptual dimensions to the overall ranking, and should avoid presenting brand value rankings as precise financial valuations. The Brand Health Index is intended to provide a diagnostic complement to the overall score, highlighting brands whose current financial performance may not be sustained by underlying brand strength.
>
> **注**：本扩展模块属于 ICO Std 2002（天机排名方法论标准）。品牌价值评价本质上结合了客观财务数据与主观感知度量。排名实体应对财务和感知维度对整体排名的相对贡献保持透明，并应避免将品牌价值排名呈现为精确的财务估值。品牌健康度指数旨在为总分提供诊断性补充，突出当前财务表现可能无法由基础品牌强度支撑的品牌。
