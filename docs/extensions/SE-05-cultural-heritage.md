# SE-05: Cultural Heritage | 文化遗产

**ICO Std 2002 — Extension SE-05**

---

This extension module specifies the requirements and methodologies for ranking and evaluating cultural heritage, including tangible heritage, intangible cultural heritage (ICH), and heritage practices. It addresses the unique challenges of heritage assessment — including the valuation of attributes that resist quantification, the imperative of community participation, and the alignment with internationally recognised conservation frameworks. This module extends the core-layer methodology (Chapters 4 through 10) by defining domain-specific indicators, data collection protocols, and scoring procedures that reflect the particularities of cultural heritage evaluation. It is directly applicable to the Tianji Ranking intangible cultural heritage track and other heritage-oriented ranking initiatives.

本扩展模块规定了文化遗产（包括有形遗产、非物质文化遗产及遗产实践）排名与评价的要求和方法论。它应对了遗产评价的独特挑战——包括对难以量化属性的价值评估、社区参与的必要性，以及与国际公认保护框架的对齐。本模块通过定义领域特定指标、数据采集协议和评分程序来扩展核心层方法论（第4章至第10章），以反映文化遗产评价的特殊性。本模块直接适用于天机排行榜非遗赛道及其他面向遗产的排名项目。

---

## SE-05.1 Scope and Applicable Objects | 范围与适用对象

### SE-05.1.1 Scope | 范围

This module applies to the design, execution, and publication of rankings that evaluate cultural heritage items, practices, and traditions. It covers:

本模块适用于评价文化遗产项目、实践和传统的排名的设计、执行和发布。它涵盖：

a) tangible cultural heritage, including monuments, buildings, archaeological sites, and cultural landscapes;

有形文化遗产，包括纪念物、建筑物、考古遗址和文化景观；

b) intangible cultural heritage as defined by the UNESCO Convention for the Safeguarding of the Intangible Cultural Heritage (2003), including oral traditions, performing arts, social practices, rituals, knowledge and practices concerning nature, and traditional craftsmanship;

联合国教科文组织《保护非物质文化遗产公约》（2003年）所定义的非物质文化遗产，包括口头传统、表演艺术、社会实践、仪式、有关自然的知识和实践以及传统手工艺；

c) heritage-in-practice contexts where living communities sustain, transmit, and adapt cultural traditions.

遗产实践情境，即活态社区维持、传承和适应文化传统的情境。

### SE-05.1.2 Applicable Objects | 适用对象

This module shall apply to rankings of:

本模块应适用于以下排名：

a) World Heritage Sites and properties on national tentative lists;

世界遗产地及国家预备名录上的遗产；

b) intangible cultural heritage elements inscribed on national or international registries;

列入国家或国际名录的非物质文化遗产要素；

c) cultural traditions and craft techniques not yet formally inscribed but of demonstrated significance;

尚未正式列入但具有显著意义的文化传统和工艺技术；

d) heritage communities, transmitters, and safeguarding programmes.

遗产社区、传承人和保护项目。

### SE-05.1.3 Exclusions | 排除项

This module does not apply to:

本模块不适用于：

a) the legal determination of heritage status under national or international law;

国家或国际法下遗产地位的法律认定；

b) the management of heritage conservation budgets or funding allocation decisions.

遗产保护预算的管理或资金分配决策。

## SE-05.3 Indicator System | 指标体系

### SE-05.3.1 Indicator Hierarchy | 指标层级

The cultural heritage indicator system shall be organised into the following three-level hierarchy:

文化遗产指标体系应组织为以下三级层级：

a) **Level 1 — Dimensions** | **一级指标（维度）**: Historical Value, Artistic Value, Scientific Value, Social Value, Endangerment Level, Authenticity, Integrity, Community Engagement, State of Conservation;

历史价值、艺术价值、科学价值、社会价值、濒危程度、真实性、完整性、社区参与、保护状况；

b) **Level 2 — Sub-dimensions** | **二级指标（子维度）**: Decompositions of each dimension as specified in SE-05.3.2 through SE-05.3.10;

各维度的分解，如SE-05.3.2至SE-05.3.10所规定；

c) **Level 3 — Specific measures** | **三级指标（具体度量）**: Operationalised variables for which data is collected, as specified in each sub-dimension.

采集数据的操作化变量，如各子维度所规定。

### SE-05.3.2 Historical Value | 历史价值

Historical value assesses the significance of a heritage item in representing, documenting, or bearing witness to historical processes, events, or periods.

历史价值评估遗产项目在代表、记录或见证历史过程、事件或时期方面的意义。

a) **Antiquity** | **年代久远性**: The temporal depth of the heritage item. The ranking entity shall define period classifications relevant to the cultural context (e.g., pre-modern, early modern, modern, contemporary).

遗产项目的时间深度。排名实体应定义与文化语境相关的时期分类（如前现代、早期现代、现代、当代）。

b) **Historical Representativeness** | **历史代表性**: The degree to which the heritage item exemplifies a particular historical period, movement, or cultural development. Assessment shall reference established historiographical frameworks.

遗产项目体现特定历史时期、运动或文化发展的程度。评估应参照既定的史学框架。

c) **Documentary Evidence** | **文献证据**: The extent to which the heritage item is supported by archival, archaeological, or other documentary evidence. Items with rich documentary corroboration shall score higher than those relying solely on oral tradition, unless the oral tradition itself constitutes the heritage value.

遗产项目被档案、考古或其他文献证据支持的程度。具有丰富文献佐证的项目应比仅依赖口头传统的项目得分更高，除非口头传统本身构成遗产价值。

### SE-05.3.3 Artistic Value | 艺术价值

Artistic value assesses the aesthetic achievement and creative significance of a heritage item.

艺术价值评估遗产项目的审美成就和创造性意义。

a) **Aesthetic Excellence** | **审美卓越性**: The degree to which the heritage item demonstrates recognised standards of aesthetic achievement within its cultural tradition. The ranking entity shall employ culture-specific aesthetic criteria and shall not impose external aesthetic standards.

遗产项目在其文化传统内展示公认审美成就标准的程度。排名实体应采用文化特定的审美标准，不应强加外部审美标准。

b) **Creative Innovation** | **创造性创新**: The degree to which the heritage item represents innovation or creative achievement within its artistic tradition, including the adaptation of traditional forms to new contexts.

遗产项目在其艺术传统内代表创新或创造性成就的程度，包括传统形式对新情境的适应。

c) **Influence on Subsequent Traditions** | **对后续传统的影响**: The documented or verifiable influence of the heritage item on subsequent artistic or cultural developments.

遗产项目对后续艺术或文化发展的有记录或可验证的影响。

### SE-05.3.4 Scientific Value | 科学价值

Scientific value assesses the contribution of a heritage item to knowledge generation, research, and understanding of natural or cultural phenomena.

科学价值评估遗产项目对知识生成、研究以及对自然或文化现象理解的贡献。

a) **Research Potential** | **研究潜力**: The extent to which the heritage item offers opportunities for new research findings, including archaeological, anthropological, or scientific insights.

遗产项目提供新研究发现机会的程度，包括考古学、人类学或科学洞见。

b) **Information Content** | **信息含量**: The volume and quality of information that the heritage item preserves or encodes about past technologies, environments, social structures, or knowledge systems.

遗产项目保存或编码的关于过去技术、环境、社会结构或知识体系的信息的数量和质量。

c) **Methodological Significance** | **方法论意义**: The degree to which the study of the heritage item has contributed to or may contribute to the development of research methodologies.

对遗产项目的研究已经或可能对研究方法论发展做出贡献的程度。

### SE-05.3.5 Social Value | 社会价值

Social value assesses the contemporary relevance and societal significance of a heritage item, including its role in identity formation, social cohesion, and cultural continuity.

社会价值评估遗产项目的当代相关性和社会意义，包括其在身份建构、社会凝聚和文化延续中的作用。

a) **Identity Significance** | **身份意义**: The degree to which the heritage item serves as a marker of collective identity for a community or group. Assessment shall include the perspectives of the associated community.

遗产项目作为社区或群体集体身份标志的程度。评估应包含关联社区的视角。

b) **Social Cohesion Function** | **社会凝聚功能**: The extent to which the heritage item contributes to social bonding, intergenerational connection, or community solidarity.

遗产项目促进社会纽带、代际联系或社区团结的程度。

c) **Contemporary Relevance** | **当代相关性**: The degree to which the heritage item remains meaningful and functional in contemporary society, including its adaptation to present-day needs and contexts.

遗产项目在当代社会中保持意义和功能的程度，包括其对当代需求和情境的适应。

### SE-05.3.6 Endangerment Level | 濒危程度

Endangerment level assesses the degree of threat to the continued existence or viability of a heritage item. This dimension shall be assessed with urgency, as it directly informs safeguarding priorities.

濒危程度评估遗产项目持续存在或存续性受威胁的程度。此维度应紧急评估，因为它直接影响保护优先级。

a) **Threat Assessment** | **威胁评估**: The ranking entity shall identify and categorise threats using a standardised taxonomy that includes:

排名实体应使用标准化分类法识别和归类威胁，包括：

1) environmental threats (climate change, natural disasters, environmental degradation);

环境威胁（气候变化、自然灾害、环境退化）；

2) socio-economic threats (urbanisation, economic displacement, labour migration);

社会经济威胁（城市化、经济置换、劳动力迁移）；

3) cultural threats (globalisation, cultural homogenisation, loss of transmission);

文化威胁（全球化、文化同质化、传承中断）；

4) political and legal threats (armed conflict, inadequate legal protection, policy changes).

政治和法律威胁（武装冲突、法律保护不足、政策变化）。

b) **Transmission Continuity** | **传承连续性**: For intangible cultural heritage, the ranking entity shall assess:

对于非物质文化遗产，排名实体应评估：

1) the number of active transmitters (practitioners, masters, apprentices);

活跃传承人（从业者、大师、学徒）数量；

2) the age distribution of practitioners, with particular attention to the absence of younger generations;

从业者的年龄分布，特别关注年轻一代的缺失；

3) the existence and viability of formal or informal transmission mechanisms.

正式或非正式传承机制的存在和存续性。

c) **Viability Index** | **存续性指数**: A composite measure combining threat level and transmission continuity. The ranking entity shall calculate the viability index using the following formula:

结合威胁水平和传承连续性的复合度量。排名实体应使用以下公式计算存续性指数：

$$V = 1 - \frac{T_{\text{norm}} + (1 - C_{\text{norm}})}{2}$$

where $T_{\text{norm}}$ is the normalised threat score (0 = no threat, 1 = extreme threat) and $C_{\text{norm}}$ is the normalised transmission continuity score (0 = no transmission, 1 = robust transmission). Higher $V$ values indicate greater viability.

其中 $T_{\text{norm}}$ 为归一化威胁评分（0 = 无威胁，1 = 极端威胁），$C_{\text{norm}}$ 为归一化传承连续性评分（0 = 无传承，1 = 强健传承）。较高的 $V$ 值表示更大的存续性。

### SE-05.3.7 Authenticity | 真实性

Authenticity assesses the degree to which a heritage item's cultural values are truthfully and credibly expressed through its attributes. This dimension follows the Nara Document on Authenticity (1994) principle that authenticity judgements shall be made within the cultural context to which the heritage item belongs.

真实性评估遗产项目的文化价值通过其属性真实可信地表达的程度。本维度遵循《奈良真实性文件》（1994年）原则，即真实性判断应在遗产项目所属的文化语境中做出。

a) **Material Authenticity** | **物质真实性**: For tangible heritage, the degree to which original materials, construction techniques, and physical fabric are preserved. The ranking entity shall assess the proportion of original fabric retained and the quality of past interventions.

对于有形遗产，原始材料、建造技术和物质载体保存的程度。排名实体应评估保留原始构件的比例和过往干预的质量。

b) **Functional Authenticity** | **功能真实性**: The degree to which the heritage item continues to serve its original or traditionally associated function.

遗产项目继续履行其原始或传统关联功能的程度。

c) **Expressive Authenticity** | **表现真实性**: For intangible cultural heritage, the degree to which the practice retains its essential character, meaning, and mode of expression as recognised by the associated community. Adaptations and evolutions that are community-driven and internally motivated shall not be considered losses of authenticity.

对于非物质文化遗产，实践保持其由关联社区认可的本质特征、意义和表达方式的程度。由社区驱动和内部推动的适应和演变不应被视为真实性的丧失。

d) **Contextual Authenticity** | **语境真实性**: The degree to which the heritage item remains in its original cultural, spatial, or social context. Relocated or decontextualised heritage items shall have reduced authenticity scores unless the relocation itself holds heritage significance.

遗产项目保留在其原始文化、空间或社会语境中的程度。被迁移或去语境化的遗产项目应降低真实性评分，除非迁移本身具有遗产意义。

> **Note**: Authenticity is not a binary quality. The ranking entity shall assess authenticity as a matter of degree along each sub-dimension, and shall document the cultural framework within which the assessment is made.
>
> **注**：真实性不是二元属性。排名实体应沿各子维度将真实性作为程度问题进行评估，并应记录评估所依据的文化框架。

### SE-05.3.8 Integrity | 完整性

Integrity assesses the wholeness and intactness of a heritage item and the extent to which it contains all the elements necessary to express its outstanding universal value or cultural significance.

完整性评估遗产项目的完整性和未受损程度，以及它包含表达其突出普遍价值或文化意义所需全部要素的程度。

a) **Structural Integrity** | **结构完整性**: For tangible heritage, the physical completeness of the heritage item, including all constituent parts, spatial relationships, and visual relationships.

对于有形遗产，遗产项目的物理完整性，包括所有组成部分、空间关系和视觉关系。

b) **Systemic Integrity** | **系统性完整性**: The completeness of the cultural system or practice that the heritage item represents, including its associated knowledge, skills, rituals, and social structures.

遗产项目所代表的文化系统或实践的完整性，包括其关联的知识、技能、仪式和社会结构。

c) **Ecological Integrity** | **生态完整性**: For cultural landscapes and nature-culture interlinked heritage, the degree to which the ecological processes and interactions that sustain the heritage values remain intact.

对于文化景观和自然-文化关联遗产，维持遗产价值的生态过程和相互作用保持完整的程度。

### SE-05.3.9 Community Engagement | 社区参与

Community engagement assesses the degree and quality of involvement of the associated community in the identification, safeguarding, transmission, and management of the heritage item. This dimension recognises the principle established in the UNESCO 2003 Convention that communities, groups, and practitioners should play a central role in safeguarding intangible cultural heritage.

社区参与评估关联社区在遗产项目的认定、保护、传承和管理中参与的程度和质量。本维度确认联合国教科文组织2003年公约所确立的原则，即社区、群体和从业者应在非物质文化遗产保护中发挥中心作用。

a) **Participation in Identification** | **认定参与**: The degree to which the associated community was involved in identifying and defining the heritage item and its cultural values. Rankings shall not evaluate heritage items without evidence of community consultation.

关联社区参与认定和定义遗产项目及其文化价值的程度。在缺乏社区咨询证据的情况下，排名不应评价遗产项目。

b) **Participation in Safeguarding** | **保护参与**: The extent and quality of community involvement in safeguarding measures, including:

社区参与保护措施的范围和质量，包括：

1) the existence of community-initiated safeguarding programmes;

社区发起的保护项目的存在；

2) the allocation of community resources (time, labour, financial) to safeguarding;

社区资源（时间、劳力、资金）向保护的分配；

3) the effectiveness of community governance structures for heritage management.

遗产管理社区治理结构的有效性。

c) **Inclusive Representation** | **包容性代表性**: The ranking entity shall assess whether the participation process includes diverse segments of the associated community, including:

排名实体应评估参与过程是否包含关联社区的多元群体，包括：

1) gender representation among practitioners and decision-makers;

从业者和决策者中的性别代表性；

2) intergenerational participation;

代际参与；

3) representation of minority or marginalised sub-groups within the community.

社区内少数或边缘群体的代表性。

d) **Free, Prior and Informed Consent** | **自由、事先和知情同意**: Where data collection for ranking purposes involves access to community knowledge, the ranking entity shall obtain free, prior and informed consent (FPIC) in accordance with the principles set out in the UN Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples (2007).

当排名目的的数据采集涉及获取社区知识时，排名实体应依照《联合国土著人民权利宣言》（2007年）规定的原则获取自由、事先和知情同意（FPIC）。

### SE-05.3.10 State of Conservation | 保护状况

State of conservation assesses the current condition of a heritage item and the effectiveness of measures taken to preserve it.

保护状况评估遗产项目的当前状况及为保护它所采取措施的有效性。

a) **Physical Condition** | **物理状况**: For tangible heritage, a standardised assessment of the physical state using condition rating scales (e.g., excellent, good, fair, poor, critical) with defined criteria for each level.

对于有形遗产，使用状况评级量表（如优秀、良好、一般、较差、危急）进行标准化物理状况评估，每个等级有定义的标准。

b) **Practice Vitality** | **实践活力**: For intangible cultural heritage, an assessment of the frequency, scale, and quality of ongoing practice, including:

对于非物质文化遗产，对正在进行的实践的频率、规模和质量的评估，包括：

1) frequency of regular practice (daily, weekly, seasonal, annual, irregular);

定期实践的频率（每日、每周、季节性、年度、不定期）；

2) number of active practitioners relative to the total associated population;

活跃从业者占关联总人口的比例；

3) quality of practice as assessed by community-recognised standards.

由社区认可标准评估的实践质量。

c) **Safeguarding Effectiveness** | **保护有效性**: An assessment of whether existing safeguarding measures have achieved their stated objectives. The ranking entity shall use before-and-after comparisons where baseline data is available.

对现有保护措施是否实现其既定目标的评估。在可获得基线数据的情况下，排名实体应使用前后比较。

d) **Risk Monitoring** | **风险监测**: The existence and quality of ongoing monitoring systems for threats and conservation status. The ranking entity shall assess:

威胁和保护状况的持续监测系统的存在和质量。排名实体应评估：

1) whether a monitoring system exists;

是否存在监测系统；

2) the frequency and regularity of monitoring;

监测的频率和规律性；

3) whether monitoring data is publicly accessible or available for audit.

监测数据是否公开可访问或可供审计。

## SE-05.4 Data Collection | 数据采集

### SE-05.4.1 Data Source Categories | 数据来源类别

The ranking entity shall collect data from the following categories of sources:

排名实体应从以下类别来源采集数据：

a) **Official Heritage Records** | **官方遗产记录**: National and international heritage registries, tentative lists, and inscription dossiers (e.g., UNESCO World Heritage List, national ICH inventories).

国家与国际遗产名录、预备名录和申遗档案（如联合国教科文组织世界遗产名录、国家非遗名录）。

b) **Conservation Reports** | **保护报告**: State of conservation reports, periodic reporting documents, and reactive monitoring reports prepared by or submitted to heritage authorities.

保护状况报告、定期报告文件和由遗产主管部门编制或提交的反应性监测报告。

c) **Community-Based Documentation** | **社区文献**: Oral histories, community archives, ethnographic records, and participatory documentation produced by or with the associated community.

口述史、社区档案、民族志记录和由关联社区参与或制作参与的文献。

d) **Academic Literature** | **学术文献**: Peer-reviewed research publications, dissertations, and field reports relevant to the heritage items under evaluation.

与被评估遗产项目相关的同行评审研究出版物、学位论文和田野报告。

e) **Expert Assessment** | **专家评估**: Structured assessments by qualified experts in relevant heritage domains (archaeology, anthropology, conservation science, traditional arts).

相关遗产领域（考古学、人类学、保护科学、传统艺术）合格专家的结构化评估。

### SE-05.4.2 Data Collection Protocols | 数据采集协议

a) The ranking entity shall develop a data collection protocol specific to each heritage ranking project. The protocol shall specify:

排名实体应为每个遗产排名项目制定特定的数据采集协议。协议应规定：

1) the list of indicators from Section SE-05.3 to be measured;

待测量的SE-05.3节指标清单；

2) the data source(s) for each indicator;

每个指标的数据来源；

3) the data collection method (document review, field survey, expert elicitation, community consultation, or combination);

数据采集方法（文献审查、田野调查、专家征询、社区咨询或组合）；

4) the timeframe for data collection;

数据采集时间范围；

5) quality assurance procedures.

质量保证程序。

b) For intangible cultural heritage, the data collection protocol shall include provisions for:

对于非物质文化遗产，数据采集协议应包含以下条款：

1) the use of culturally appropriate communication methods;

使用文化适当的沟通方式；

2) the involvement of community members as co-investigators or cultural mediators;

社区成员作为共同研究者或文化中介的参与；

3) the documentation of the consent process and any conditions imposed by the community.

同意过程的记录和社区施加的任何条件。

### SE-05.4.3 Handling of Intangible Knowledge | 无形知识的处理

a) The ranking entity shall recognise that intangible cultural heritage knowledge may be sacred, secret, or subject to customary access restrictions. The ranking entity shall:

排名实体应认识到非物质文化遗产知识可能是神圣的、秘密的或受习惯性获取限制的。排名实体应：

1) respect community decisions regarding the confidentiality of cultural knowledge;

尊重社区关于文化知识保密性的决定；

2) not require the disclosure of restricted knowledge as a condition for ranking inclusion;

不要求披露受限知识作为排名纳入的条件；

3) provide alternative assessment pathways where full data access is not possible.

在无法获得完整数据访问时提供替代评估路径。

b) Where community knowledge is used in ranking assessment, the ranking entity shall document:

当社区知识用于排名评估时，排名实体应记录：

1) the source and nature of the knowledge;

知识的来源和性质；

2) the consent obtained for its use;

获取的使用同意；

3) any restrictions on further dissemination.

对进一步传播的任何限制。

### SE-05.4.4 Temporal Considerations | 时间因素

a) Heritage ranking data shall reflect the current state of the heritage item at the time of data collection. Historical data may be used for trend analysis but shall not substitute for current-condition data.

遗产排名数据应反映数据采集时遗产项目的当前状况。历史数据可用于趋势分析，但不应替代当前状况数据。

b) For intangible cultural heritage, the ranking entity shall account for the cyclical or seasonal nature of many heritage practices. Data collection shall be timed to capture practice at its most representative period.

对于非物质文化遗产，排名实体应考虑许多遗产实践的周期性或季节性。数据采集的时机应能捕捉最具代表性时期的实践。

c) The ranking entity shall document the date of each data point and the time elapsed between data collection and ranking publication.

排名实体应记录每个数据点的日期及数据采集与排名发布之间经过的时间。

## SE-05.5 Scoring Methodology | 评分方法

### SE-05.5.1 Scoring Scale | 评分量表

a) Each Level 3 indicator shall be scored on a scale appropriate to its measurement type:

每个三级指标应按适合其测量类型的量表评分：

1) Quantitative indicators shall use normalised scales (0 to 1 or 0 to 100);

定量指标应使用归一化量表（0至1或0至100）；

2) Qualitative indicators shall use defined ordinal scales with at least five levels, each with explicit criteria and anchor descriptions.

定性指标应使用至少五级的定义序数量表，每级有明确标准和锚定描述。

b) Where expert judgement is required, the ranking entity shall use structured elicitation protocols (e.g., Delphi method as specified in Chapter 5 of the core layer) and shall report inter-rater reliability statistics.

当需要专家判断时，排名实体应使用结构化征询协议（如核心层第5章规定的德尔菲法），并应报告评分者间信度统计。

### SE-05.5.2 Weighting | 权重

a) The default weight scheme for the nine Level 1 dimensions shall be as follows, unless the ranking entity provides documented justification for alternative weights:

九个一级维度的默认权重方案如下，除非排名实体提供替代权重的书面理由：

| Dimension | 维度 | Default Weight | 默认权重 |
|-----------|------|---------------|---------|
| Historical Value | 历史价值 | 0.15 | 0.15 |
| Artistic Value | 艺术价值 | 0.12 | 0.12 |
| Scientific Value | 科学价值 | 0.10 | 0.10 |
| Social Value | 社会价值 | 0.12 | 0.12 |
| Endangerment Level | 濒危程度 | 0.15 | 0.15 |
| Authenticity | 真实性 | 0.12 | 0.12 |
| Integrity | 完整性 | 0.08 | 0.08 |
| Community Engagement | 社区参与 | 0.10 | 0.10 |
| State of Conservation | 保护状况 | 0.06 | 0.06 |

b) The default weights reflect the principle that cultural heritage rankings shall prioritise historical significance, endangerment, and authenticity, while also recognising the importance of community agency and social relevance.

默认权重反映了文化遗产排名应优先考虑历史意义、濒危性和真实性的原则，同时承认社区能动性和社会相关性的重要性。

c) For rankings focused specifically on intangible cultural heritage, the ranking entity should increase the weight of Community Engagement and Transmission Vitality indicators to reflect the living nature of ICH.

对于专门聚焦非物质文化遗产的排名，排名实体宜增加社区参与和传承活力指标的权重，以反映非遗的活态性质。

d) Weight modifications shall be subject to the procedures specified in Chapter 6 of the core layer, including stakeholder consultation and sensitivity analysis.

权重修改应遵循核心层第6章规定的程序，包括利益相关方协商和敏感性分析。

### SE-05.5.3 Aggregation | 聚合

a) Scores at Level 3 shall be aggregated to Level 2 using the weighted sum method specified in Chapter 8 of the core layer.

三级评分应使用核心层第8章规定的加权和法聚合至二级。

b) Scores at Level 2 shall be aggregated to Level 1 using the same method.

二级评分应使用相同方法聚合至一级。

c) The overall heritage score shall be calculated as the weighted sum of Level 1 dimension scores using the weights defined in SE-05.5.2.

遗产总分应使用SE-05.5.2定义的权重计算为一级维度评分的加权和。

d) The ranking entity shall report dimension-level scores alongside the overall score to enable users to understand the profile of each ranked heritage item.

排名实体应与总分一起报告维度级评分，以使用户能够了解每个被排遗产项目的特征。

### SE-05.5.4 Special Provisions for ICH Rankings | 非遗排名特殊规定

a) For rankings of intangible cultural heritage, the ranking entity shall include a Transmission Vitality Index as a supplementary measure. The Transmission Vitality Index shall be calculated as:

对于非物质文化遗产的排名，排名实体应包含传承活力指数作为补充度量。传承活力指数应计算为：

$$TVI = \frac{P_{\text{active}}}{P_{\text{total}}} \times \frac{A_{\text{young}}}{A_{\text{total}}} \times F_{\text{practice}}$$

where $P_{\text{active}}$ is the number of active practitioners, $P_{\text{total}}$ is the total estimated practitioner population at peak, $A_{\text{young}}$ is the number of practitioners under 40, $A_{\text{total}}$ is the total active practitioners, and $F_{\text{practice}}$ is the practice frequency factor (1.0 = daily, 0.8 = weekly, 0.5 = seasonal, 0.3 = annual, 0.1 = irregular).

其中 $P_{\text{active}}$ 为活跃从业者数量，$P_{\text{total}}$ 为鼎盛时期估计从业者总人口，$A_{\text{young}}$ 为40岁以下从业者数量，$A_{\text{total}}$ 为活跃从业者总数，$F_{\text{practice}}$ 为实践频率因子（1.0 = 每日，0.8 = 每周，0.5 = 季节性，0.3 = 年度，0.1 = 不定期）。

b) The Transmission Vitality Index shall be reported alongside the overall score for all ICH rankings and shall be used as a tiebreaker when overall scores are equal.

传承活力指数应与所有非遗排名的总分一起报告，并应在总分相等时用作排序依据。

## SE-05.6 Cultural Sensitivity and Ethical Requirements | 文化敏感性与伦理要求

### SE-05.6.1 Cultural Sensitivity | 文化敏感性

a) The ranking entity shall ensure that the evaluation framework does not impose external cultural values or aesthetic standards on heritage items from different cultural traditions.

排名实体应确保评价框架不将外部文化价值或审美标准强加于不同文化传统的遗产项目。

b) Where assessment criteria require culture-specific interpretation (e.g., artistic value, authenticity), the ranking entity shall include qualified cultural insiders in the assessment process.

当评估标准需要文化特定的解释时（如艺术价值、真实性），排名实体应在评估过程中纳入合格的文化内部者。

c) The ranking entity shall not rank heritage items from incommensurable cultural traditions against each other without explicit contextualisation. Cross-cultural comparisons shall be accompanied by a methodological note explaining the limitations of such comparisons.

排名实体不应在缺乏明确语境化的情况下将不可通约的文化传统的遗产项目相互排名。跨文化比较应附有方法论说明，解释此类比较的局限性。

### SE-05.6.2 Ethical Obligations | 伦理义务

a) The ranking entity shall not publish rankings that may contribute to the commercialisation, exploitation, or misappropriation of cultural heritage.

排名实体不应发布可能助长文化遗产商业化、剥削或盗用的排名。

b) Where ranking publication may increase visitor pressure or tourism development around a heritage site, the ranking entity shall include a conservation advisory note in the publication.

当排名发布可能增加遗产地周围的游客压力或旅游开发时，排名实体应在发布中包含保护建议说明。

c) The ranking entity shall respect the right of communities to withdraw their heritage from ranking consideration. If a community requests removal, the ranking entity shall comply within one ranking cycle.

排名实体应尊重社区撤回其遗产排名考虑的权利。如果社区请求移除，排名实体应在一个排名周期内遵从。

### SE-05.6.3 Benefit Sharing | 利益共享

a) Where the ranking entity derives commercial benefit from heritage rankings, the ranking entity should establish mechanisms for benefit sharing with the associated communities.

当排名实体从遗产排名中获得商业利益时，排名实体宜建立与关联社区的利益共享机制。

b) Benefit sharing may include financial contributions to safeguarding programmes, capacity building, community development projects, or other forms as agreed with the community.

利益共享可包括对保护项目的财务贡献、能力建设、社区发展项目或与社区商定的其他形式。

## SE-05.7 Alignment with International Frameworks | 与国际框架的对齐

### SE-05.7.1 UNESCO World Heritage Convention Alignment | 与UNESCO世界遗产公约的对齐

a) For rankings of tangible cultural heritage, the indicator system shall be consistent with the criteria for Outstanding Universal Value (OUV) as defined in the Operational Guidelines for the Implementation of the World Heritage Convention.

对于有形文化遗产的排名，指标体系应与《世界遗产公约操作指南》中定义的突出普遍价值（OUV）标准一致。

b) The assessment of authenticity and integrity shall follow the principles established in the Operational Guidelines, paragraphs 79-86 (authenticity) and paragraphs 87-95 (integrity), adapted as necessary for ranking purposes.

真实性和完整性的评估应遵循操作指南第79-86段（真实性）和第87-95段（完整性）确立的原则，并根据排名目的进行必要调整。

### SE-05.7.2 UNESCO ICH Convention Alignment | 与UNESCO非遗公约的对齐

a) For rankings of intangible cultural heritage, the indicator system shall be consistent with the definition and principles of the Convention for the Safeguarding of the Intangible Cultural Heritage (2003).

对于非物质文化遗产的排名，指标体系应与《保护非物质文化遗产公约》（2003年）的定义和原则一致。

b) The ranking entity shall respect the Convention's emphasis on the central role of communities, groups, and individuals in safeguarding ICH, as articulated in Article 15.

排名实体应尊重公约第15条所阐述的社区、群体和个人在保护非遗中的中心作用。

c) The assessment of safeguarding measures shall reference the definitions and categories established in the Convention and its Operational Directives.

保护措施的评估应参照公约及其操作指南中确立的定义和类别。

### SE-05.7.3 Nara Document Alignment | 与奈良文件的对齐

a) The assessment of authenticity shall follow the principles of the Nara Document on Authenticity (1994), particularly:

真实性的评估应遵循《奈良真实性文件》（1994年）的原则，特别是：

1) that authenticity judgements must be linked to the cultural context within which the heritage item exists;

真实性判断必须与遗产项目存在的文化语境相联系；

2) that the attributes against which authenticity is assessed may include form and design, materials and substance, use and function, traditions and techniques, location and setting, language and other forms of intangible heritage, spirit and feeling, and other internal and external factors.

评估真实性的属性可包括形式与设计、材料与实质、用途与功能、传统与技术、位置与环境、语言及其他非物质文化遗产形式、精神与感觉以及其他内外部因素。

## SE-05.8 Publication and Transparency | 发布与透明度

### SE-05.8.1 Publication Requirements | 发布要求

In addition to the requirements specified in Chapter 9 of the core layer, heritage ranking publications shall include:

除核心层第9章规定的要求外，遗产排名发布还应包括：

a) a statement of alignment with the international frameworks referenced in SE-05.7;

与SE-05.7引用的国际框架的对齐声明；

b) the cultural sensitivity assessment conducted under SE-05.6.1;

根据SE-05.6.1进行的文化敏感性评估；

c) for ICH rankings, the Transmission Vitality Index for each ranked item;

对于非遗排名，每个被排项目的传承活力指数；

d) a conservation advisory note, where applicable.

保护建议说明（如适用）。

### SE-05.8.2 Community Notification | 社区通知

a) Before publishing a heritage ranking, the ranking entity shall notify the associated communities of the intended publication and provide a reasonable period (not less than 30 calendar days) for community review and feedback.

发布遗产排名前，排名实体应通知关联社区拟发布的排名，并提供合理期间（不少于30个日历日）供社区审查和反馈。

b) Community feedback shall be documented. Where communities raise substantive concerns about cultural sensitivity or potential harm, the ranking entity shall address these concerns before publication.

社区反馈应予以记录。当社区提出关于文化敏感性或潜在危害的实质性关切时，排名实体应在发布前解决这些关切。

---

> **Note**: This extension module is part of ICO Std 2002 (Tianji Ranking Methodology Standard). Cultural heritage evaluation presents unique methodological challenges arising from the diversity of cultural traditions, the intangible nature of much heritage value, and the ethical imperative of community participation. Ranking entities should approach heritage evaluation with humility and cultural awareness, recognising that quantification can never fully capture the meaning and significance of cultural heritage to its associated communities. The indicators and methods specified in this module are intended to provide a structured and transparent framework for heritage assessment, not to reduce cultural heritage to numerical scores alone.
>
> **注**：本扩展模块属于 ICO Std 2002（天机排名方法论标准）。文化遗产评价面临独特的方法论挑战，这些挑战源于文化传统的多样性、大量遗产价值的无形性质以及社区参与的伦理必要性。排名实体应以谦逊和文化意识对待遗产评价，认识到量化永远无法完全捕捉文化遗产对其关联社区的意义和重要性。本模块规定的指标和方法旨在为遗产评估提供结构化和透明的框架，而非将文化遗产简化为单纯的数字评分。
