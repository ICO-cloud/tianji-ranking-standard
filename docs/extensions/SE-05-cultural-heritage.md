# SE-05: Cultural Heritage and Intangible Cultural Heritage
## 文化遗产与非遗

**ICO Std 2002 — Extension SE-05**

---

## Applicable Objects | 适用对象

World heritage sites, intangible cultural heritage practices, cultural traditions, craft techniques

## Core Dimensions | 核心维度

- Outstanding Universal Value (UNESCO criterion) | 突出普遍价值
- Authenticity | 真实性
- Integrity | 完整性
- State of Conservation | 保护状况
- Community Engagement | 社区参与
- Transmission Vitality | 传承活力
- Economic Sustainability | 经济可持续性
