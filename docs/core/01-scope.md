# Chapter 1: Scope | 第1章 范围

**ICO Std 2002:2026 — Chapter 1**

---

## 1.1 General Scope | 总体范围

This International Standard specifies the general requirements for ranking methodology applicable to any organisation that designs, implements, publishes, or verifies ranking systems. It establishes a universal methodological framework that is domain-independent, ensuring that all ranking systems — regardless of their subject matter, geographic scope, or organisational context — conform to consistent principles of rigour, transparency, and reproducibility.

本国际标准规定了排名方法论的通用要求，适用于任何设计、实施、发布或验证排名系统的组织。它建立了领域无关的通用方法论框架，确保所有排名系统——无论其主题、地理范围或组织背景——均遵循严谨性、透明度和可复现性的一致原则。

### 1.1.1 Ranking Systems Covered | 涵盖的排名系统

This standard applies to ranking systems that produce ordinal, interval, or composite assessments of entities within a defined population. The following categories of ranking systems are within the scope of this standard:

本标准适用于对定义群体内实体产生序数、区间或综合评估的排名系统。下列类别的排名系统属于本标准范围：

a) **Product quality rankings** | **产品质量排名**: Rankings that assess and compare the quality attributes of consumer goods, industrial products, or digital products, including but not limited to safety, performance, durability, and user satisfaction.

评估和比较消费品、工业产品或数字产品的质量属性的排名，包括但不限于安全性、性能、耐久性和用户满意度。

b) **Brand value rankings** | **品牌价值排名**: Rankings that assess and compare the monetary or strategic value of brands, including corporate brands, product brands, and personal brands, consistent with the principles of ISO 20671 and ISO 10668.

评估和比较品牌的货币或战略价值的排名，包括企业品牌、产品品牌和个人品牌，与 ISO 20671 和 ISO 10668 的原则一致。

c) **Individual influence rankings** | **人物影响力排名**: Rankings that assess and compare the influence, impact, or reputation of individuals, including public figures, thought leaders, and professionals.

评估和比较个人影响力、冲击力或声誉的排名，包括公众人物、思想领袖和专业人员。

d) **City image rankings** | **城市形象排名**: Rankings that assess and compare the image, competitiveness, livability, or attractiveness of cities and urban areas.

评估和比较城市和城区的形象、竞争力、宜居性或吸引力的排名。

e) **Cultural heritage rankings** | **文化遗产排名**: Rankings that assess and compare the significance, condition, or preservation status of cultural heritage, including tangible and intangible cultural heritage, consistent with the principles of the UNESCO Convention for the Safeguarding of the Intangible Cultural Heritage.

评估和比较文化遗产的重要性、状况或保存状态的排名，包括物质和非物质文化遗产，与《联合国教科文组织保护非物质文化遗产公约》的原则一致。

f) **Restaurant and gastronomy rankings** | **餐厅美食排名**: Rankings that assess and compare the quality, creativity, or dining experience of restaurants and gastronomic establishments.

评估和比较餐厅及餐饮机构的质量、创意或用餐体验的排名。

g) **Intangible asset rankings** | **无形资产排名**: Rankings that assess and compare the value or significance of intangible assets, including intellectual property, goodwill, and digital assets.

评估和比较无形资产价值或重要性的排名，包括知识产权、商誉和数字资产。

h) **Economy and industry rankings** | **经济产业排名**: Rankings that assess and compare economic performance, industrial competitiveness, or sectoral development at national, regional, or organisational levels.

评估和比较国家、区域或组织层面经济表现、产业竞争力或行业发展的排名。

i) **ESG and sustainability rankings** | **ESG与可持续发展排名**: Rankings that assess and compare environmental, social, and governance performance or sustainability outcomes, aligned with the objectives of the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

评估和比较环境、社会和治理绩效或可持续性结果的排名，与联合国可持续发展目标（SDGs）的目标一致。

> **Note**: The categories listed above are not exhaustive. A ranking system that does not fall within any of the listed categories but produces ordinal or composite assessments of entities is also within the scope of this standard, provided it meets the applicability criteria defined in 1.1.2 and 1.1.3.
>
> **注**：上述类别并非穷举。未列入上述类别但产生实体序数或综合评估的排名系统，若满足 1.1.2 和 1.1.3 定义的适用性标准，亦属于本标准范围。

### 1.1.2 Domains of Application | 应用领域

This standard is applicable to ranking systems operating in the following domains:

本标准适用于以下领域运作的排名系统：

— **Commercial domain** | **商业领域**: Rankings produced by or for commercial entities, including market research firms, industry associations, and media organisations.

商业实体产生或为其产生的排名，包括市场研究公司、行业协会和媒体机构。

— **Academic and research domain** | **学术研究领域**: Rankings produced by or for academic and research institutions, including university rankings, research output assessments, and scholarly impact indices.

学术和研究机构产生或为其产生的排名，包括大学排名、研究产出评估和学术影响力指数。

— **Cultural and creative domain** | **文化与创意领域**: Rankings produced by or for cultural institutions, creative industries, and heritage organisations.

文化机构、创意产业和遗产组织产生或为其产生的排名。

— **Public service and governance domain** | **公共服务与治理领域**: Rankings produced by or for governmental bodies, international organisations, and public interest entities, including public service performance assessments and governance quality indices.

政府机构、国际组织和公共利益实体产生或为其产生的排名，包括公共服务绩效评估和治理质量指数。

> **Note**: A ranking system may span multiple domains of application. In such cases, all applicable requirements of this standard shall be satisfied.
>
> **注**：排名系统可跨越多个应用领域。在此情况下，应满足本标准所有适用要求。

### 1.1.3 Organisational Applicability | 组织适用性

This standard is applicable to the following categories of organisations involved in the ranking ecosystem:

本标准适用于排名生态系统中涉及的以下类别组织：

a) **Ranking entities** | **排名实体**: Organisations that initiate, design, and publish ranking systems (see 3.1.8).

发起、设计和发布排名系统的组织（见 3.1.8）。

b) **Executing parties** | **执行方**: Organisations that carry out data collection, processing, or analysis on behalf of a ranking entity, whether as contractors, subcontractors, or collaborative partners.

代表排名实体执行数据采集、处理或分析的组织，无论是作为承包商、分包商还是合作伙伴。

c) **Data providers** | **数据提供方**: Organisations or individuals that supply data used as input to a ranking system, whether directly or through intermediary platforms.

直接或通过中介平台提供排名系统输入数据的组织或个人。

d) **Auditing and certification bodies** | **审计与认证机构**: Independent organisations that assess the conformance of a ranking system with the requirements of this standard, consistent with the principles of ISO/IEC 17021.

评估排名系统与本标准要求一致性的独立组织，与 ISO/IEC 17021 的原则一致。

> **Note**: An organisation may fulfil multiple roles simultaneously (e.g., a ranking entity may also serve as the executing party). In such cases, the requirements applicable to each role shall be satisfied independently.
>
> **注**：一个组织可同时承担多个角色（例如，排名实体亦可担任执行方）。在此情况下，应独立满足适用于每个角色的要求。

## 1.2 Inclusions | 包含内容

This standard includes the following:

本标准包含以下内容：

### 1.2.1 Core Methodology Requirements | 核心方法论要求

Chapters 4 through 9 establish the core methodological requirements applicable to all ranking systems, covering:

第4章至第9章确立适用于所有排名系统的核心方法论要求，涵盖：

— principles governing the design and operation of ranking systems (Chapter 4);
排名系统设计和运行的原则（第4章）；

— indicator system design and selection (Chapter 5);
指标体系设计与选择（第5章）；

— weighting rules and procedures (Chapter 6);
权重规则和程序（第6章）；

— data collection and quality requirements (Chapter 7);
数据采集和质量要求（第7章）；

— scoring and ranking computation procedures (Chapter 8); and
评分和排名计算程序（第8章）；以及

— publication and transparency requirements (Chapter 9).
发布和透明度要求（第9章）。

### 1.2.2 Extension Framework | 扩展框架

The extension modules (SE-01 through SE-09) provide domain-specific requirements that supplement the core methodology requirements for particular categories of ranking systems. Each extension module:

扩展模块（SE-01至SE-09）提供补充特定类别排名系统核心方法论要求的领域特定要求。每个扩展模块：

— specifies indicators and data sources applicable to its domain;
规定适用于其领域的指标和数据源；

— defines domain-specific weighting rules and scoring procedures; and
定义领域特定的权重规则和评分程序；且

— provides domain-specific examples and guidance.
提供领域特定的示例和指引。

Conformance with an extension module is required only when a ranking system falls within the corresponding domain (see 1.4.2).

仅当排名系统属于相应领域时，才要求对扩展模块的一致性（见 1.4.2）。

### 1.2.3 Verification and Audit Provisions | 验证与审计条款

Chapters 10 and 11 establish the verification, audit, and compliance provisions, covering:

第10章和第11章确立验证、审计和合规条款，涵盖：

— audit and traceability requirements (Chapter 10); and
审计和可追溯性要求（第10章）；以及

— conformance assessment and compliance procedures (Chapter 11).
合规评估和合规程序（第11章）。

## 1.3 Exclusions | 排除内容

### 1.3.1 Out of Scope | 不在范围内

The following are explicitly outside the scope of this standard:

以下明确不在本标准范围内：

a) **Domain-specific indicator systems**: This standard does not prescribe the specific indicators, data sources, or scoring procedures for any particular domain. Domain-specific requirements are defined in the extension modules (SE-01 to SE-09), which are separate from the core standard.

领域特定指标体系：本标准不规定任何特定领域的具体指标、数据源或评分程序。领域特定要求在扩展模块（SE-01至SE-09）中定义，与核心标准分离。

b) **Legal and regulatory requirements**: This standard does not replace, override, or substitute for any legal or regulatory requirements applicable to ranking systems in any jurisdiction. Compliance with applicable laws and regulations remains the responsibility of the ranking entity.

法律和法规要求：本标准不替代、覆盖或取代任何管辖区域内适用于排名系统的法律或法规要求。遵守适用法律法规仍是排名实体的责任。

c) **Subjective evaluation of ranked entities**: This standard does not provide criteria for judging the inherent worth, merit, or value of ranked entities. It addresses only the methodological integrity of the ranking process, not the substantive correctness of any particular ranking outcome.

被排实体的主观评价：本标准不提供评判被排实体固有价值、优劣或意义的准则。它仅涉及排名过程的方法论完整性，而非任何特定排名结果的实质正确性。

### 1.3.2 Non-prescriptive Areas | 非规定性领域

The following areas are not prescribed by this standard but may be addressed in supplementary guidance:

以下领域本标准不作规定，但可在补充指引中涉及：

— the commercial model or business structure of ranking entities;
排名实体的商业模式或商业结构；

— the specific software tools or platforms used to implement ranking computations; and
用于实施排名计算的特定软件工具或平台；以及

— the design of user interfaces for presenting ranking results.
呈现排名结果的用户界面设计。

## 1.4 Conformance Targets | 一致性目标

### 1.4.1 Full Conformance | 完全一致

A ranking system achieves **full conformance** with this standard when it satisfies all of the following conditions:

排名系统满足以下全部条件时，达到对本标准的**完全一致**：

a) it satisfies all normative requirements of the core layer (Chapters 1–11);
满足核心层（第1–11章）的所有规范性要求；

b) it satisfies all normative requirements of every applicable extension module; and
满足每个适用扩展模块的所有规范性要求；且

c) its conformance has been verified through an independent audit conducted in accordance with Chapter 10.
其一致性已通过按照第10章进行的独立审计予以验证。

A fully conformant ranking system may claim conformance as follows:

完全一致的排名系统可按如下方式声称一致性：

> "This ranking system conforms to ICO Std 2002:2026 (Full Conformance)."
>
> "本排名系统符合 ICO Std 2002:2026（完全一致）。"

### 1.4.2 Partial Conformance | 部分一致

A ranking system achieves **partial conformance** with this standard when it satisfies all normative requirements of the core layer (Chapters 1–11) but does not satisfy all normative requirements of an applicable extension module, or when its conformance has not been verified through an independent audit as specified in Chapter 10.

排名系统满足核心层（第1–11章）的所有规范性要求，但未满足适用扩展模块的所有规范性要求，或其一致性未按第10章规定通过独立审计验证时，达到对本标准的**部分一致**。

A partially conformant ranking system shall identify the specific requirements that are not satisfied and provide a justification for each non-conformance. It may claim conformance as follows:

部分一致的排名系统应识别未满足的具体要求，并为每项不一致提供正当理由。可按如下方式声称一致性：

> "This ranking system conforms to ICO Std 2002:2026 (Partial Conformance). Non-conformant requirements: [list]."
>
> "本排名系统符合 ICO Std 2002:2026（部分一致）。不一致的要求：[清单]。"

> **Note**: Partial conformance is not equivalent to non-conformance. It acknowledges that a ranking system satisfies the universal core requirements while recognising that domain-specific or verification requirements may not yet be fully met. Ranking entities are encouraged to pursue full conformance over time.
>
> **注**：部分一致不等同于不一致。它承认排名系统满足通用核心要求，同时认识到领域特定或验证要求可能尚未完全满足。鼓励排名实体逐步追求完全一致。
