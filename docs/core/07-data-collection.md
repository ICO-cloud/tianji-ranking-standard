# Chapter 7: Data Collection Specifications | 第7章 数据采集规范

**ICO Std 2002:2026 — Chapter 7**

---

This chapter specifies the requirements for data collection, processing, quality assurance, ethical compliance, and archival in ranking systems. It establishes a comprehensive data governance framework that ensures the integrity and trustworthiness of the data underpinning ranking results.

本章规定了排名系统中数据采集、处理、质量保证、伦理合规和存档的要求。它建立了全面的数据治理框架，确保支撑排名结果的数据的完整性和可信度。

---

## 7.1 Data Source Classification | 数据源分级

All data sources used in a ranking system shall be classified according to the three-tier system defined below. The classification determines the evidentiary weight and verification requirements applicable to each source.

排名系统中使用的所有数据源应按照以下定义的三级体系进行分类。分类决定适用于每个来源的证据权重和验证要求。

### 7.1.1 Tier 1 Data Sources | 一级数据源

Tier 1 data sources are the most authoritative and reliable sources available.

一级数据源是可获得的最具权威性和可靠性的来源。

Tier 1 data sources include:

一级数据源包括：

a) **Official statistics** | **官方统计数据**: Data published by national statistical offices, intergovernmental organisations, and regulatory bodies in their official capacity.

国家统计局、政府间组织和监管机构以其官方身份发布的数据。

b) **Audited financial data** | **审计财务数据**: Financial statements and reports that have been independently audited in accordance with recognised auditing standards.

按照公认审计标准进行独立审计的财务报表和报告。

c) **Administrative records** | **行政记录**: Data generated by governmental or institutional administrative processes, such as registration records, licensing data, and tax filings.

由政府或机构行政流程产生的数据，如登记记录、许可数据和税务申报。

d) **Peer-reviewed research** | **同行评审研究**: Data published in peer-reviewed academic journals or reports, where the underlying methodology has been subject to independent scrutiny.

在同行评审学术期刊或报告中发布的数据，其基础方法论已经独立审查。

### 7.1.2 Tier 2 Data Sources | 二级数据源

Tier 2 data sources provide valuable information that may not have the same level of institutional authority as Tier 1 sources.

二级数据源提供有价值的信息，但可能不具备一级来源同等程度的机构权威性。

Tier 2 data sources include:

二级数据源包括：

a) **Public databases** | **公开数据库**: Databases maintained by research institutions, non-governmental organisations, or industry associations that are publicly accessible.

研究机构、非政府组织或行业协会维护的公开可访问的数据库。

b) **Academic literature** | **学术文献**: Published academic works that have not undergone formal peer review for the specific data used, or where the data is secondary.

未对所使用的具体数据进行正式同行评审的已发表学术著作，或数据为次级数据的情况。

c) **Industry reports** | **行业报告**: Reports produced by industry analysts, consultancies, or trade associations.

行业分析师、咨询公司或贸易协会制作的报告。

d) **Self-reported data with independent verification** | **经独立验证的自报数据**: Data provided by ranked entities where the ranking entity has conducted independent verification of a representative sample.

由被排实体提供且排名实体已对代表性样本进行独立验证的数据。

### 7.1.3 Tier 3 Data Sources | 三级数据源

Tier 3 data sources are the least authoritative and shall be used only when Tier 1 and Tier 2 sources are insufficient.

三级数据源权威性最低，仅在一级和二级来源不足时使用。

Tier 3 data sources include:

三级数据源包括：

a) **Media reports** | **媒体报道**: Data derived from news articles, press releases, or other media publications.

来自新闻报道、新闻稿或其他媒体出版的数据。

b) **Social media data** | **社交数据**: Data derived from social media platforms, online forums, or user-generated content platforms.

来自社交媒体平台、在线论坛或用户生成内容平台的数据。

c) **Unverified self-reported data** | **未经验证的自报数据**: Data provided by ranked entities without independent verification.

被排实体提供且未经独立验证的数据。

d) **Expert estimates** | **专家估计**: Quantitative estimates provided by individual experts in the absence of empirical data.

在缺乏实证数据时由个别专家提供的定量估计。

### 7.1.4 Evidentiary Rules for Data Source Tiers | 各级数据源的采信规则

The following rules govern the use of data from different tiers:

以下规则管辖不同层级数据源数据的使用：

a) Tier 1 data shall be used as the primary source wherever available.

一级数据应在可获取时作为主要来源使用。

b) Tier 2 data may be used as a supplementary or alternative source when Tier 1 data is unavailable or insufficient.

当一级数据不可用或不充分时，二级数据可作为补充或替代来源使用。

c) Tier 3 data may only be used when:

三级数据仅在以下情况下可使用：

1) no Tier 1 or Tier 2 data exists for the indicator; and

指标不存在一级或二级数据；且

2) the use of Tier 3 data is necessary for the ranking purpose; and

三级数据的使用对排名目的是必要的；且

3) the limitations of Tier 3 data are disclosed in the ranking publication.

三级数据的局限性在排名发布中予以披露。

d) When data from multiple tiers is available for the same indicator, the ranking entity shall document the rationale for the tier selected.

当同一指标有多级数据可用时，排名实体应记录选择该层级的理由。

e) The proportion of data points sourced from each tier shall be reported in the methodology documentation.

来自每层级的数据点比例应在方法论文件中报告。

## 7.2 Data Collection Process | 数据采集流程

Data collection shall follow a structured process that ensures completeness, accuracy, and traceability.

数据采集应遵循结构化过程，确保完整性、准确性和可溯源性。

### 7.2.1 Collection Plan Development | 采集计划制定

Prior to data collection, the ranking entity shall develop and document a data collection plan that includes:

在数据采集之前，排名实体应制定并记录数据采集计划，包括：

a) the list of indicators and the data requirements for each indicator, including:

指标列表及每个指标的数据要求，包括：

1) the data elements to be collected;

要采集的数据要素；

2) the data source tier and specific source for each element;

每个要素的数据源层级和具体来源；

3) the expected data format and unit of measurement;

预期的数据格式和计量单位；

4) the reference period for the data.

数据的参考期间。

b) the collection timeline, including milestones for data acquisition, validation, and integration;

采集时间表，包括数据获取、验证和整合的里程碑；

c) the collection methodology, specifying:

采集方法，规定：

1) whether data is obtained through direct collection, automated retrieval, or third-party provision;

数据是通过直接采集、自动检索还是第三方提供获得；

2) any data sharing agreements or access arrangements required;

所需的任何数据共享协议或访问安排；

3) the protocols for handling data access denials or partial responses.

处理数据访问拒绝或部分回应的协议。

d) the roles and responsibilities of personnel involved in data collection.

参与数据采集人员的角色和职责。

### 7.2.2 Data Collection Execution | 数据采集执行

Data collection shall be executed in accordance with the collection plan. The ranking entity shall:

数据采集应按照采集计划执行。排名实体应：

a) collect data for all ranked entities within the defined reference period;

在定义的参考期间内收集所有被排实体的数据；

b) apply consistent collection procedures across all entities;

在所有实体间采用一致的采集程序；

c) record the date and method of acquisition for each data point;

记录每个数据点的获取日期和方法；

d) flag any deviations from the collection plan and document the reasons and implications.

标记任何偏离采集计划的情况，并记录原因和影响。

### 7.2.3 Data Quality Verification | 数据质量校验

Upon completion of data collection, the ranking entity shall conduct quality verification as specified in 7.3. Data that does not meet the quality standards shall be:

数据采集完成后，排名实体应按照7.3的规定进行质量验证。不符合质量标准的数据应：

a) returned to the source for correction, where feasible;

在可行时退回来源更正；

b) supplemented with data from alternative sources;

以替代来源的数据补充；

c) marked as missing and handled in accordance with 7.4.1.

标记为缺失并按7.4.1的规定处理。

### 7.2.4 Data Entry and Labelling | 数据入库与标注

Data that has passed quality verification shall be entered into the ranking database with the following annotations:

已通过质量验证的数据应入库至排名数据库，并附带以下标注：

a) the entity identifier;

实体标识符；

b) the indicator identifier;

指标标识符；

c) the data source identifier and tier level;

数据源标识符和层级；

d) the reference period;

参考期间；

e) the acquisition date;

获取日期；

f) any quality flags or caveats;

任何质量标记或注意事项；

g) the version identifier of the data collection cycle.

数据采集周期的版本标识符。

## 7.3 Data Quality Standards | 数据质量标准

Data used in ranking systems shall meet the quality standards defined in 7.3.1 through 7.3.5. These standards are aligned with the principles of ISO 8000 (Data Quality).

排名系统中使用的数据应满足7.3.1至7.3.5定义的质量标准。这些标准与ISO 8000（数据质量）的原则一致。

### 7.3.1 Accuracy | 准确性

Data shall accurately represent the real-world value it purports to measure.

数据应准确代表其所声称衡量的真实世界值。

The accuracy of data shall be assessed by:

数据的准确性应通过以下方式评估：

a) comparing data values against independent, authoritative sources where available;

在可获取时，将数据值与独立的权威来源进行比对；

b) performing plausibility checks (e.g., verifying that values fall within expected ranges);

进行合理性检查（如验证值是否落在预期范围内）；

c) verifying computational derivations (e.g., that ratios are correctly computed from their numerator and denominator).

验证计算推导（如比率是否由分子和分母正确计算）。

The ranking entity shall document the accuracy assessment methods and results for each indicator.

排名实体应记录每个指标的准确性评估方法和结果。

### 7.3.2 Completeness | 完整性

The data set shall contain values for all entities and all indicators within the ranking scope, or shall explicitly document the extent and pattern of missing data.

数据集应包含排名范围内所有实体和所有指标的值，或应明确记录缺失数据的范围和模式。

Completeness shall be measured as:

完整性应衡量为：

$$\text{Completeness} = \frac{\text{Number of non-missing data points}}{\text{Total number of expected data points}} \times 100\%$$

The ranking entity shall:

排名实体应：

a) report the overall completeness rate for each indicator;

报告每个指标的整体完整率；

b) report the completeness rate for each entity;

报告每个实体的完整率；

c) document the pattern of missing data (e.g., random, systematic, or concentrated in specific entity groups);

记录缺失数据的模式（如随机、系统性或集中在特定实体群体）；

d) assess whether the pattern of missing data introduces bias (see 4.7).

评估缺失数据的模式是否引入偏见（见4.7）。

### 7.3.3 Consistency | 一致性

Data shall be internally consistent and consistent across sources.

数据应在内部一致且在来源间一致。

Consistency shall be verified by:

一致性应通过以下方式验证：

a) cross-referencing data values from different sources for the same entity and indicator;

对不同来源的同一实体和指标的数值进行交叉参照；

b) checking that aggregate values (e.g., totals, averages) are consistent with their component values;

检查聚合值（如合计、平均值）与其组成值一致；

c) verifying that units of measurement, currencies, and temporal references are uniform across the dataset.

验证计量单位、货币和时间参考在数据集中统一。

Where inconsistencies are identified, the ranking entity shall:

当识别到不一致时，排名实体应：

1) investigate the cause of the inconsistency;

调查不一致的原因；

2) determine the correct value through reference to the most authoritative source;

通过参照最权威来源确定正确值；

3) document the inconsistency and the resolution.

记录不一致和解决方案。

### 7.3.4 Timeliness | 时效性

Data shall be sufficiently current for the ranking purpose.

数据对排名目的而言应具有足够的时效性。

The ranking entity shall:

排名实体应：

a) specify the maximum acceptable age of data for each indicator, based on the rate of change of the underlying phenomenon and the purpose of the ranking;

基于被衡量现象的变化率和排名目的，规定每个指标数据的最大可接受年龄；

b) report the reference date of each data point;

报告每个数据点的参考日期；

c) document any data that exceeds the maximum acceptable age and the justification for its continued use.

记录任何超过最大可接受年龄的数据及其继续使用的理由。

### 7.3.5 Traceability | 可溯源性

Each data point shall be traceable to its original source through a documented chain of custody.

每个数据点应通过记录的保管链可追溯至其原始来源。

Traceability shall be maintained by:

可溯源性应通过以下方式保持：

a) recording the source, acquisition method, and date for each data point;

记录每个数据点的来源、获取方法和日期；

b) preserving the original data in its unmodified form;

以未修改的形式保存原始数据；

c) maintaining a log of all transformations applied to the data (see 7.4).

维护对数据施加的所有变换的日志（见7.4）。

## 7.4 Data Processing Specifications | 数据处理规范

### 7.4.1 Missing Data Handling | 缺失值处理

The ranking entity shall specify and document the method for handling missing data. The following methods are recognised:

排名实体应规定并记录处理缺失数据的方法。认可以下方法：

a) **Imputation** | **插值**: Missing values may be replaced with estimated values. The imputation method shall be documented and shall include:

缺失值可替换为估计值。插值方法应予以记录并应包括：

1) **Mean/median imputation** | **均值/中位数插值**: Replacing missing values with the mean or median of the available values for that indicator across other entities. This method shall only be used when the proportion of missing data is less than 5 % and the data is missing at random.

以该指标在其他实体中可用值的均值或中位数替换缺失值。此方法仅在缺失数据比例小于5%且数据随机缺失时使用。

2) **Regression imputation** | **回归插值**: Estimating missing values from a regression model using correlated indicators as predictors. The regression model and its goodness-of-fit shall be documented.

使用相关指标作为预测变量，从回归模型估计缺失值。回归模型及其拟合度应予以记录。

3) **Multiple imputation** | **多重插值**: Generating multiple plausible values for each missing data point and combining the results. This method should be used when the proportion of missing data exceeds 5 %.

为每个缺失数据点生成多个合理值并合并结果。当缺失数据比例超过5%时宜使用此方法。

b) **Exclusion** | **剔除**: Entities with missing data for a specific indicator may be excluded from the calculation of that indicator. The ranking entity shall:

特定指标缺失数据的实体可从该指标的计算中剔除。排名实体应：

1) document the number and identity of excluded entities;

记录被排除实体的数量和身份；

2) assess whether exclusion introduces systematic bias;

评估排除是否引入系统性偏见；

3) report the impact of exclusion on the ranking results.

报告排除对排名结果的影响。

c) **Flagging** | **标记**: Missing values may be flagged without imputation or exclusion, and the indicator score may be calculated on the basis of available data. The ranking entity shall:

缺失值可予以标记而不进行插值或排除，指标得分可基于可用数据计算。排名实体应：

1) clearly label the flag in the data and results;

在数据和结果中清晰标注标记；

2) disclose the proportion of flagged data for each entity;

披露每个实体的标记数据比例；

3) report the impact of flagging on the ranking results.

报告标记对排名结果的影响。

### 7.4.2 Outlier Handling | 异常值处理

The ranking entity shall establish and document procedures for identifying and handling outliers.

排名实体应建立并记录识别和处理异常值的程序。

#### 7.4.2.1 Outlier Identification | 异常值识别

Outliers shall be identified using at least one of the following methods:

异常值应使用至少以下方法之一识别：

a) **Statistical methods** | **统计方法**:

1) Z-score method: data points with |z| > 3 shall be flagged as potential outliers.

Z-score方法：|z| > 3的数据点应标记为潜在异常值。

2) Interquartile range (IQR) method: data points below Q1 − 1.5 × IQR or above Q3 + 1.5 × IQR shall be flagged as potential outliers.

四分位距（IQR）方法：低于Q1 − 1.5 × IQR或高于Q3 + 1.5 × IQR的数据点应标记为潜在异常值。

3) Mahalanobis distance: for multivariate outlier detection, observations with Mahalanobis distance exceeding the χ² critical value at α = 0.01 shall be flagged.

马氏距离：对于多元异常值检测，马氏距离超过α = 0.01的χ²临界值的观测值应标记。

b) **Domain knowledge** | **领域知识**: Domain experts may identify outliers based on substantive knowledge of the data context.

领域专家可基于对数据背景的实质知识识别异常值。

#### 7.4.2.2 Outlier Treatment | 异常值处理

Identified outliers shall be handled by one of the following methods:

识别的异常值应通过以下方法之一处理：

a) **Retention with justification** | **保留并论证**: If the outlier is determined to be a genuine data point reflecting a real phenomenon, it shall be retained with documented justification.

如果异常值被确定为反映真实现象的真正数据点，应在记录理由后予以保留。

b) **Winsorisation** | **缩尾处理**: Outlying values shall be set to the value of a specified percentile (e.g., the 1st and 99th percentiles). The percentile thresholds shall be documented.

异常值应设为指定百分位的值（如第1和第99百分位）。百分位阈值应予以记录。

c) **Transformation** | **变换**: The data may be transformed (e.g., log transformation) to reduce the influence of outliers. The transformation shall be documented and its impact on the distribution shall be assessed.

数据可进行变换（如对数变换）以减少异常值的影响。变换应予以记录，且其对分布的影响应予评估。

d) **Exclusion** | **排除**: Outliers may be excluded only when they are determined to be data errors. The exclusion shall be documented with the reason.

异常值仅在确定为数据错误时可排除。排除应记录原因。

#### 7.4.2.3 Outlier Documentation | 异常值记录

All outlier identification and treatment decisions shall be documented in the data processing log, including:

所有异常值识别和处理决定应在数据处理日志中记录，包括：

a) the identification method and criteria used;

使用的识别方法和标准；

b) the data points identified as outliers;

识别为异常值的数据点；

c) the treatment applied and the justification;

应用的处理及理由；

d) the impact on the ranking results.

对排名结果的影响。

### 7.4.3 Data Standardisation and Normalisation | 数据标准化与归一化方法

Where indicators are measured on different scales, the data shall be standardised or normalised before aggregation. The methods specified in Chapter 8, Section 8.2 shall apply.

当指标以不同尺度测量时，数据应在聚合前进行标准化或归一化。第8章第8.2节规定的方法应适用。

The ranking entity shall:

排名实体应：

a) specify the standardisation or normalisation method for each indicator;

规定每个指标的标准化或归一化方法；

b) document the reason for the choice of method;

记录方法选择的理由；

c) verify that the chosen method does not introduce bias or distort the indicator distribution in a manner that disadvantages specific entity groups.

验证所选择的方法不引入偏见或不以不利于特定实体群体的方式扭曲指标分布。

## 7.5 Data Ethics and Privacy | 数据伦理与隐私

### 7.5.1 Personal Information Protection | 个人信息保护

Where the ranking system collects or processes personal data, the ranking entity shall comply with applicable data protection legislation, including but not limited to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where it applies.

当排名系统收集或处理个人数据时，排名实体应遵守适用的数据保护法规，包括但不限于适用时的《通用数据保护条例》（GDPR）。

The ranking entity shall:

排名实体应：

a) establish a lawful basis for the collection and processing of personal data;

建立收集和处理个人数据的合法依据；

b) minimise the collection of personal data to what is necessary for the ranking purpose;

将个人数据的收集最小化为排名目的所必需；

c) implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data;

实施适当的技术和组织措施保护个人数据；

d) ensure that personal data is not used for purposes incompatible with the ranking purpose.

确保个人数据不用于与排名目的不相容的用途。

### 7.5.2 Data Use Authorisation | 数据使用授权

The ranking entity shall ensure that it has the right to use all data incorporated in the ranking system. The ranking entity shall:

排名实体应确保其有权使用纳入排名系统的所有数据。排名实体应：

a) verify the terms of use or licence for each data source;

验证每个数据源的使用条款或许可；

b) obtain necessary permissions for data that is not publicly available;

对非公开数据获取必要的许可；

c) comply with any restrictions on the use, reproduction, or redistribution of data.

遵守对数据使用、复制或再分发的任何限制。

### 7.5.3 Data Awareness and Objection Rights of Ranked Entities | 被排实体的数据知情权与异议权

Ranked entities shall have the following rights with respect to data about them that is used in the ranking:

被排实体对排名中使用的关于其的数据应享有以下权利：

a) **Right to be informed** | **知情权**: Ranked entities shall be informed of the data used about them in the ranking, upon request.

**知情权**：被排实体经请求应被告知排名中使用的关于其的数据。

b) **Right to object** | **异议权**: Ranked entities shall have the right to challenge the accuracy of data used about them. The ranking entity shall:

**异议权**：被排实体应有权质疑关于其数据的准确性。排名实体应：

1) establish a documented procedure for receiving and processing data objections;

建立接收和处理数据异议的记录程序；

2) investigate the objection within a reasonable period (not exceeding 30 calendar days);

在合理期间（不超过30个日历日）内调查异议；

3) correct any data found to be inaccurate and document the correction;

更正发现不准确的任何数据并记录更正；

4) inform the objecting entity of the outcome of the investigation.

将调查结果告知异议实体。

c) **Right to correct** | **更正权**: Where data is found to be inaccurate, the ranking entity shall correct the data and recompute the affected ranking results.

**更正权**：当发现数据不准确时，排名实体应更正数据并重新计算受影响的排名结果。

> **Note**: The right to object does not extend to the methodological choices of the ranking system (e.g., indicator selection, weighting), which are governed by the principles in Chapter 4 and the procedures in Chapters 5 and 6.
>
> **注**：异议权不扩展到排名系统的方法论选择（如指标选取、赋权），这些由第4章的原则和第5、6章的程序管辖。

## 7.6 Data Audit and Archival | 数据审计与存档

### 7.6.1 Data Provenance Chain | 数据溯源链记录

The ranking entity shall maintain a complete provenance chain for all data used in the ranking. The provenance chain shall include:

排名实体应为排名中使用的所有数据维护完整的溯源链。溯源链应包括：

a) the original source of each data point;

每个数据点的原始来源；

b) the date and method of acquisition;

获取日期和方法；

c) all transformations, corrections, and adjustments applied to the data;

对数据施加的所有变换、更正和调整；

d) the personnel responsible for each transformation;

每次变换的负责人员；

e) the date and authorisation of each transformation.

每次变换的日期和授权。

### 7.6.2 Raw Data Archival Requirements | 原始数据存档要求

The ranking entity shall archive all raw (unprocessed) data used in the ranking for a minimum period of five years from the date of publication of the ranking results. The archived data shall:

排名实体应将排名中使用的所有原始（未处理）数据自排名结果发布之日起至少保存五年。归档数据应：

a) be stored in a secure, accessible format;

以安全、可访问的格式存储；

b) include all metadata required for traceability (see 7.3.5);

包括可溯源性所需的所有元数据（见7.3.5）；

c) be protected against unauthorised modification;

防止未经授权的修改；

d) be available for audit purposes upon request.

应请求可供审计使用。

### 7.6.3 Data Change Log | 数据变更日志

The ranking entity shall maintain a data change log that records all modifications to the ranking dataset after initial collection. Each log entry shall include:

排名实体应维护数据变更日志，记录初始采集后对排名数据集的所有修改。每条日志应包括：

a) the date and time of the change;

变更的日期和时间；

b) the identity of the person authorising the change;

授权变更的人员身份；

c) a description of the change, including the original and modified values;

变更描述，包括原始值和修改值；

d) the reason for the change;

变更原因；

e) the impact of the change on the ranking results.

变更对排名结果的影响。

The data change log shall be retained for the same period as the raw data (see 7.6.2).

数据变更日志应与原始数据保留相同期限（见7.6.2）。

Ranking entities that maintain certification under these standards may reference their certified processes as evidence of compliance with the relevant provisions of this chapter.
